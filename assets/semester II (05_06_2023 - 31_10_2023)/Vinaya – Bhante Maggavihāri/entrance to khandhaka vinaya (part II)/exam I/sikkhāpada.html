sikkhāpada


  • sikkhāpada is {{15126430453517714::the training rules or precepts promulgated by the Bhagavā which are the foundation for achieving higher spiritual states, known as }}{{15126430453517714::adhisīla}}{{15126430453517714::, }}{{15126430453517714::adhicitta}}{{15126430453517714::, and }}{{15126430453517714::adhipaññā}}
  • 2 types of sikkhāpada
    • ovādasikkhāpada: these are exhortations by the Bhagavā that are found mostly in the Suttapiṭaka, where the Bhagavā advises bhikkhus on what actions to perform or avoid such as "pāṇātipātā paṭivirato hoti" (refrain from taking life) or the ovādapātimokkha
    • āṇāsikkhāpada: these are instructions by the Bhagavā, that are found in the Vinayapiṭaka, that have been established as binding rules
  • the threefold sāsana
    • pariyattisāsana (the study of the doctrine)
    • paṭipattisāsana (the practice)
    • paṭivedhasāsana (supramundane attainments)
  • 10 benefits of promulgating sikkhāpada
    • saṅghasuṭṭhutāya (for the excellence of the Saṅgha): under rājasikkhāpada: “Yo ca tathāgatassa {{7469453387277609::vacanaṃ sampaṭicchati, tassa taṃ dīgharattaṃ hitāya sukhāya hoti}}” (whoever accepts the Tathāgata's words, that would bring them benefit and happiness for a long time); on the other hand, Bhaddāli Sutta in the Majjhima Nikāya, emphasizes the detrimentals one has to face for not accepting them
      • is this the only meaning meant on this case? what about the excellence of the Saṅgha? #q🙋🏼‍♂
    • saṅghaphāsutāya (for the comfort of the Saṅgha): The establishment of precepts enables the Saṅgha to live together peacefully and in unity
    • dummaṅkūnaṃ puggalānaṃ niggahāya (for restraining obdurate individuals)
    • pesalānaṃ bhikkhūnaṃ phāsuvihārāya (for the comfort of well-behaved monks): when the boundaries of right and wrong are explicitly defined, well-behaved monks can practice their morality with ease, eliminating any doubts about what should or should not be done; furthermore, when immoral individuals are restrained, the observance of community acts is unhindered and moral monks can abide comfortably
    • diṭṭhadhammikānaṃ āsavānaṃ saṃvarāya (for the prevention of suffering here and now): adhering to the precepts prevents sufferings here and now from occurring, such as physical punishment, accusations, defamation, loss of respect or losing followers
    • samparāyikānaṃ āsavānaṃ paṭighātāya (for warding off suffering hereafter): adhering to the precepts prevents sufferings hereafter from occurring, such as bad destinations and various unpleasant results in saṁsāra
    • appasannānaṃ pasādāya (for instilling faith in those without faith): learning about the precepts and observing disciplined bhikkhus and bhikkhunīs can inspire faith even in non-Buddhists such as the case of a non-Buddhist brahmin, well-versed in Vedas, who, developed faith in the Buddhasāsana after studying the Vinaya
    • pasannānaṃ bhiyyobhāvāya (for the increase of faith in the faithful): learning about the precepts and observing disciplined bhikkhus and bhikkhunīs can increase the faith of Buddhists themselves
    • saddhammaṭṭhitiyāya (for the endurance of the true Dhamma); how does the promulgation of precepts lead to the endurance of the three saddhammas? #editing
      • saddhamma comprises three aspects:
        • pariyattisaddhamma: the teachings found in the Tipiṭaka
        • paṭipattisaddhamma: austere practices (dhutaṅga), duties (khandhaka vatta and asīti mahāvatta), morality (sīla), concentration (samādhi), and insight (vipassanā)
        • paṭivedhasaddhamma: supramundane path (magga), fruition (phala), and Nibbāna
      • the promulgation of precepts leads to the study of vinaya and in order to gain a deeper understanding of the vinaya, monks and nuns engage in the study of other teachings of the Buddha which strengthens the pariyattisaddhamma the observance of precepts allows the proper development and fulfilment of the practical aspects of the teaching such as austerities, duties, morality, concentration and insight, strengthening the paṭipattisaddhamma the observance of precepts assists disciples to easily fulfill their practice and achieve the paths, fruitions and Nibbāna, which strengthens the paṭivedhasaddhamma
    • vinayānuggahāya (for the fostering of discipline): The establishment of precepts strengthens the fourfold Vinaya within the sāsana
      • vinaya is fourfold
        • saṃvaravinaya (discipline as restraint)
        • pahānavinaya (discipline as eradication)
        • samathavinaya (discipline as settlement of disciplinary issues)
        • paññattivinaya (discipline as promulgated rules)
        • note: in this list the other ones are not included, they are: 3-/5-fold sampatti, and codanaṁ vā saraṇaṁ vā
