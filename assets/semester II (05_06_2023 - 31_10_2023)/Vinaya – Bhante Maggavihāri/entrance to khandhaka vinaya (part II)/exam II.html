  • vassūpagamana
    • vassa is twofold:
      • purimikā: entered on the {{727403775182196::1st day of the rainy season}}, the day after the {{727403775182196::Āsāḷhī}}{{727403775182196:: }}{{727403775182196::full-moon }}{{727403775182196::(July)}}, which is the {{727403775182196::8th}} uposatha of the {{727403775182196::hot }}season
      • pacchimikā: entered on the {{02495044542838465::1st day of the second month of the rainy season}}, the day after the {{02495044542838465::Sāvaṇa}}{{02495044542838465:: }}{{02495044542838465::full-moon}}, which is the {{02495044542838465::2nd}} uposatha of the {{02495044542838465::rainy }}season
      • note: not entering vassa on one of these two days is a {{3619713551769066::dkṭ}} offense; pāḷi (not for exam): "na bhikkhave, {{3619713551769066::vassaṃ na upagantabbaṃ, yo na upagaccheyya āpatti dukkaṭassa}}"
    • is there any difference between vassa and vassāna?→vassa is normally used for the 3 months rains retreat but may be used as vassa season, referring to the whole 4 months; vassāna is normally used referring to the whole season but may also be used for the 3 months rains retreat
    • recommended steps for entering vassa
      • clean the monastery where one lives
      • in the evening worship the triple gem by offering flowers and lamps in the shrine room
      • recite some paritta
      • transfer merit to devas
      • utter the phrase, "{{8501915739182913::imasmiṃ vihāre imaṃ temāsaṃ vassaṃ ūpemi}}" once, twice, or thrice
      • note: is the observance of vassa valid if one forgets to utter the statement for entering vassa?→if one is in the place one intends to enter vassa and one has the intention to do so, forgetting to utter the statement does not hinder the observance of vassa
    • suitable residence for spending vassa
      • the residence must have {{7790210451751818::a }}{{7790210451751818::roof}}{{7790210451751818:: and a }}{{7790210451751818::door}} if a suitable residence is not available, the monk should {{7790210451751818::seek assistance}}{{7790210451751818:: from someone to build one, or build it himself}} entering the rains retreat due to the unavailability of a residence does not exempt the monk from a {{7790210451751818::dkṭ}}{{7790210451751818:: }}offfence

    • counting dawns
      • one who left with sattāhakaraṇīya should return to the place where he observed the rains retreat before {{9363632331295968::the dawn of the 7th day (counting after) or the dawn of the 8th day (counting before)}} if one left on a Sunday, one must return before or on {{9363632331295968::Saturday, before the break of dawn of Sunday}}, otherwise vassaccheda will result
      • normally commentators count the {{2932309670540503::dawn of the the day after one has left (counting after)}} as the 1st dawn, but some elders count the {{2932309670540503::dawn of the day one has left (counting before)}} as the 1st one on the first method, one should return before the {{2932309670540503::7th}} dawn breaks on the second method, one should return before the {{2932309670540503::8th}} dawn breaks

  • pavāraṇā
    • pavāraṇā has two aspects:
      • rejection
        • "yo pana bhikkhu bhuttāvī pavārito anatarittaṃ khādanīyaṃ vā bhojanīyaṃ vā khādeyya vā bhuñjeyya vā pācittiyaṃ"
        • lay disciples relinquish ("reject") their uposatha sīla at the end of an uposatha day
      • invitation
        • the pavāraṇā referred to in this context is not about relinquishing or rejecting precepts, but is {{06651801726895878::the invitation made by a monk to one, two or three monks, or to the saṅgha of monks, to compassionately and constructively advise him on any mistakes or shortcomings he may have committed, whether they were seen, heard, or suspected, and the determination to make amends accordingly}}
        • note:
          • what is the purpose of pavāraṇā?→to facilitate personal growth and development within the monastic community; it does not signify the act of ending the rains retreat itself
    • 9 types of pavāraṇā
      • puggala- / kāraka-vasena (based on the number of individuals)
        • saṅghapavāraṇā (done by at least 5 monks: 1 who invites and 4 or more who listen)
        • gaṇapavāraṇā (done by 4, 3 or 2 monks : 1 who invites and 3 who listen, 1 who invites and 2 who listen, 1 who invites and 1 who listens)
        • puggalapavāraṇā (done by 1 monk); also called adhiṭṭhānapavāraṇā
        • note:
          • sometimes, monks who have the same number of vassas perform the pavāraṇā together; this is known as {{8075046178449385::samānavassika pavāraṇā}}
      • kattabbākārakavasena (if there are dangers or many monks present, one may recite the pavāraṇā twice or even just once)
        • tevācikapavāraṇā (pavāraṇā recited 3 times)
        • dvevācikapavāraṇā (pavāraṇā recited 2 times)
        • ekavācikapavāraṇā (pavāraṇā recited 1 time)
      • divasavasena (based on the day it is observed)
        • cātuddasīpavāraṇā (on the 14th)
        • paṇṇarasīpavāraṇā (on the 15th)
        • sāmaggīpavāraṇā (on the day a previously disunited saṅgha is reunited)
        • note:
          • the final uposatha of the purimikā rains retreat is the {{5522442715850697::6th}} uposatha of the {{5522442715850697::rainy}} season, making it a {{5522442715850697::paṇṇarasī}} day; usually pavāraṇā is performed on that day however, if necessary, the Saṅgha may choose to perform it on the {{5522442715850697::7th}} or {{5522442715850697::8th}} uposatha of the {{5522442715850697::rainy}} season if the pavāraṇā is conducted on the {{5522442715850697::7th}} uposatha, it becomes a {{5522442715850697::cātuddasī}} pavāraṇā if performed on the {{5522442715850697::8th}} uposatha, it is also a {{5522442715850697::paṇṇarasī }}pavāraṇā
          • if a matter suited for sāmaggī uposatha arises during the {{366596040466153::twenty-nine}} days between the {{366596040466153::first day of the waning moon after the Assayujapuṇṇamā (Purimakattika)}} and the {{366596040466153::Kattikapuṇṇamā (Pacchimakattika)}}, the {{366596040466153::sāmaggī }}pavāraṇā should be conducted
            • a matter suited for sāmaggīuposatha refers to a reconciliation of the previously divided saṅgha, right? does it matter when the division occured? #q🙋🏼‍♂

      • after the bhikkhus have gathered in the sīmā; one bhikkhu (A) must get the {{011162194989009944::ajjhesaka sammuti}} (permission to request) from the Saṅgha as follows
        • "suṇātu me bhante saṅgho, yadi saṅghassa pattakallaṃ, ahaṃ āyasmantaṃ tissaṃ pavāraṇāñattiṭhapanaṃ ajjheseyyaṃ"

      • vocab: #[[pāḷi vocab (1st year) ]]
        • ajjhesati [adhi + √is + *a + ti]↔pr. (+acc) requests; invites; asks
        • ajjhesaka→the requester, inviter
        • ajjhesanā (f.) [adhi + √is + *a + anā]↔request; invitation; solicitation; lit. inviting
        • ṭhapanā (f.) / ṭhapanaṁ (nt.) [√ṭhā + *āpe + anā]↔(f.) (+acc) setting up; (nt.) (+acc) fixing; fastening; setting in place; lit. causing standing
      • then the monk A who got the ajjhesaka sammuti should invite another monk (tissa: usually the most senior or another senior monk) to perform the pavāraṇāñatti (announcement of the pavāraṇā); invitation for tissa to perform the ñatti:
        • "saṅgho bhante, āyasmantaṃ tissaṃ pavāraṇāñattiṭhapanaṃ ajjhesati, ṭhapetu bhante āyasmā tisso pavāraṇāñattiṁ. saṅgho bhante, āyasmantaṃ tissaṃ pavāraṇāñattiṭhapanaṃ ajjhesati, ṭhapetu bhante āyasmā tisso pavāraṇāñattiṁ. saṅgho bhante, āyasmantaṃ tissaṃ pavāraṇāñattiṭhapanaṃ ajjhesati, ṭhapetu bhante āyasmā tisso pavāraṇāñattiṁ."
        • thereafter, the monk B (tissa), who was invited to perform the pavāraṇāñatti, should accept it by saying
          • "āma ṭhapemi, āvuso"
      • then monk B (tissa) should obtain the {{5720802038035004::vinayapucchaka sammuti }}(permission to inquire about disciplinary matters) from the saṅgha; this is the permission to question another monk (C: nāga) about vinaya matters related to the pavāraṇākamma as follows
        • "suṇātu me bhante saṅgho, yadi saṅghassa pattakallaṃ, ahaṃ nāgaṃ vinayaṃ puccheyyaṃ"
          • why no āyasmantaṁ in this case? #q🙋🏼‍♂
      • then monk C (nāga) should obtain the {{6744186366841793::vinayavissajjaka sammuti}} (permission to answer the disciplinary inquiries) from the Saṅgha as follows
        • "suṇātu me bhante saṅgho, yadi saṅghassa pattakallaṃ, ahaṃ āyasmatā tissena bhikkhunā vinayaṃ puṭtho vissajjeyyaṃ"
      • thereafter, the disciplinary questions and answers must be done, which are almost identical to what is done during the uposatha; once completed, the vinaya pucchaka monk (B: tissa) should make the invitation for the announcement of the pavāraṇā
        • "pubbakaraṇapubbakiccāni samāpetvā, desitāpattikassa samaggassa bhikkhusaṅghassa anumatiyā, pavāraṇāñattiṃ ṭhapetuṃ ārādhanaṃ karoma."
      • pavāraṇāñatti for saṅghapavāraṇā

      • saṅghapavāraṇā procedure in brief
        • (pubbakaraṇa and so on should be done)
        • A gets the ajjhesaka sammuti (permission to invite)
        • A invites B (tissa) to perform the pavāraṇāñatti
        • B accepts it
        • B gets the vinayapucchaka sammuti to question C (nāga) about vinaya matters
        • C gets the vinayavissajjaka sammuti to answer the vinaya questions to be asked by B
        • B questions and C answers
        • B (/ or C) invites the Saṅgha to do the ñatti (informs the saṅgha the ñatti will be done)
        • B does the pavāraṇāñatti
        • according to seniority monks do the pavāraṇā
        • note:
          • it is allowed for another monk to request the consent for another
          • B (tissa) is normally the mahāthera
          • many think C (nāga) should inform the saṅgha the ñatti will be done (in Galduwa it is done so)
