np 1 - paṭhamakaṭhinasikkhāpada


  • background
    • while the Buddha was residing at the {{39777304762119137::Gotamaka shrine,}}{{39777304762119137:: }}{{39777304762119137::Vesāli}}{{39777304762119137::,}} He allowed monks to use ticīvara; the {{39777304762119137::chabbaggiyas}}{{39777304762119137:: }}used to visit villages dressed in different sets of three robes; frugal bhikkhus did not appreciate it and reported it to the Buddha who proclaimed: “{{39777304762119137::Yo pana bhikkhu atirekacīvaraṃ dhāreyya nissaggiyaṃ pācittiyaṃ}}”; later, ven. {{39777304762119137::Ānanda }}received an additional robe and wished to offer it to ven. Sāriputta who was residing at the city of Sāketa; after inquiring from the Buddha how to proceed he was asked when would ven. Sāriputta return to which he guessed 9 or 10 days; the Buddha then preached: “{{39777304762119137::Anujānāmi bhikkhave dasāhaparamaṃ atirekacīvaraṃ dhāretuṃ}}” amending the rule
  • description
    • a bhikkhu's robes must be determined ({{028967352911764888::adhiṭṭhāna}}) or their ownership shared ({{028967352911764888::vikappana}}) with one of the five sahadhammikas, otherwise they are considered additional robes ({{028967352911764888::atireka}}{{028967352911764888:: cīvara}}) during a non-cīvarakāla season an atirekacīvara becomes nissaggiya and unfit for use after {{028967352911764888::10 }}days of receiving it; the bhikkhu will also be subject to a {{028967352911764888::pct}} offence
  • size of the robe
    • minumum size for a cloth to be considered a robe: {{3911401532386134::8x4}} sugataṅguli {{3911401532386134::24x12}} majjhimapurisaṅguli / vaḍḍhakī {{3911401532386134::36x18}} pakatipurisaṅguli maximum size: {{3911401532386134::9x6}} sugatavidatthi note: if any one of the dimensions is less then the minimum, the cloth does not qualify as a robe
  • pāḷi terms
    • atirekacīvara↔an adhiṭṭhānūpaga vikappanūpaga cloth that has not been determined nor its ownership shared
    • vikappanūpaga↔a robe fit for vikappana
    • adhiṭṭhānūpaga↔a robe fit for determination
    • adhiṭṭhāna↔the act of determining a suitable cloth as one of the 9 types of robes; the purpose is to increase the sense of responsibility and awareness towards the robes
    • adhiṭṭhita cīvara↔a robe that has been determined
    • vikappana↔the act of sharing ownership of a cloth with any of the pañcasahadhammikas; after one of the owners gives up his share of ownership, the other owner can use it as he likes
    • vikappita cīvara↔a robe of which the ownership has been shared
    • paccuddharaṇa / paccuddhāraṇa↔the act of relinquishing the determination of an adhiṭṭhitacīvara making it an atirekacīvara
      • procedure for paccuddhāraṇa
        • while being apart from the robe: {{962356267303176::considering the place the robe is kept and saying, “}}{{962356267303176::Etaṃ cīvaraṃ paccuddharāmi}}{{962356267303176::” }} while the robe is within hatthapāsa: {{962356267303176::if not in direct contact with the robe by saying, “}}{{962356267303176::Imaṃ cīvaraṃ paccuddharāmi}}{{962356267303176::” if in direct contact merely thinking, “}}{{962356267303176::Imaṃ cīvaraṃ paccuddharāmi}}{{962356267303176::” }}
    • cīvarakālasamaya / kāla samaya
      • the period for making robes which is 1 or 5 months depending on whether the monk has spread the kaṭhina or not; 1 month being from the day after the Assayujapuṇṇamā until the Kattikapuṇṇamā if the kaṭhina was not spread; 5 months being until Phaggunapuṇṇamā if it was spread; this is the period in which most robes are offered and made; if a monk leaves the residence where he observed vassa, and he has neither cīvarapalibodha nor āvāsapalibodha his cīvarakālasamaya ends “cīvarakālasamayo nāma anatthate kathine vassānassa pacchimo māso, atthate kaṭhine pañca māsā.”
    • kaṭhinānisaṁsa↔benefits of kaṭhina (one of them being that monks are allowed to keep atirekacīvaras without having to subject them to adhiṭṭhāna or vikappana before 10 days have elapsed)
    • cīvarapalibodha↔"obstacles related to robes"; a monk has the intention of making a robe; this ends when the monk finishes the robe or at the end of the cīvarakālasamaya
    • āvāsapalibodha↔"obstruction related to dwellings" a monk has the intention of returning to the monastery where he spent the vassa
    • kaṭhinuddhāra↔formal vinayakamma done to suspend the kaṭhinānisaṁsa in occasions allowed by the Buddha
    • vocab: #[[pāḷi vocab (1st year) ]]
      • palibodha [pari + √rudh + *a] (m.)↔(+dat) obstruction (for); impediment (for); hindrance (for); 2 drawback; disadvantage; lit. obstruction
  • cīvarakālasamaya
    • one who has observed purimikā rains, done the pavāraṇa on the Assayujapuṇṇamā and has cīvara or āvāsa palibodha, receives kaṭhinānisaṃsa from: {{06746287855322541::the day after the Assayujapuṇṇamā until the Kattikapuṇṇamā}} if such monk has spread the kaṭhina, the kaṭhinānisaṁsa is extended until: {{06746287855322541::Phaggunapuṇṇamā (5 months)}}
    • during which period is the paṭhamakaṭhinasikkhāpada applicable?→it is only applicable outside of the cīvarakālasamaya when one does not have kaṭhinānisaṁsa (if kaṭhinuddhāra is done, it is applicable)

  • counting days
    • how should the days be counted here?
      • the days should be counted starting from the dawn of the day one received the cloth; only the days outside of the cīvarakālasamaya are counted, in other words only the days in the akālasamaya are counted here, so the counting can be interrupted during the kālasamaya and resumed afterwards: | 1. {s} | 2. m | 3. t | 4. w | 5. th | 6. f | 7. sa | 8. s | 9. m | 10. t | 11. w | {1.} | 2. | 3. | 4. | kālasamaya | 5. | 6. | 7. | 8. | 9. | 10. | 11. {} = the day the cloth is received | = the 11th dawn in which the offense is committed
  • if any of the following occurs before the 11th dawn breaks, there is no offense
    • if the cloth is adhiṭṭhita
    • if it is vikappita
    • if ownership is lost (it is given away, destroyed or stolen)
    • if it is taken on trust by someone else
    • if it is cut into small pieces that are neither vikappanūpaga nor adhiṭṭhānūpaga
  • aṅgāni (factors)
    • ubbhatasmiṁ kaṭhine: absence of kaṭhinānisaṁsa
    • dasāhaparamaṁ...taṁ atikkāmayato: exceeding the 10 days
    • atirekacīvaraṁ: the robe being an atirekacīvara
      • (at least one robe made with one out of six allowable materials for robes)
      • (the robe being vikappanūpaga)
  • cha cīvarāni: 6 types of allowable robe materials
    • khomaṁ↔linen
    • kappāsikaṁ↔cotton
    • koseyyaṁ↔silk
    • kambalaṁ↔wool / goat hair
    • sāṇaṁ↔Madras hemp / Sunn hemp: cloth woven from hemp thread
    • bhaṅgaṁ↔mixture of various threads
    • 6 extra types of allowable cīvara based on the catumahāpadesa
      • dukūlaṃ: a soft / fine cloth
      • pattuṇṇaṃ: a kind of silk
      • cīnapaṭṭaṃ: Chinese silk
      • somārapaṭṭaṃ: silk from the country of Somāra
      • iddhimayikaṃ: made by psychic powers
      • devadattiyaṃ: given by deities
      • note:
        • catumahāpadesa: Yaṃ, bhikkhave, mayā ‘Idaṃ na kappatī’ti appaṭikkhittaṃ tañce akappiyaṃ anulometi, kappiyaṃ paṭibāhati, taṃ vo na kappati. Yaṃ, bhikkhave, mayā ‘Idaṃ na kappatī’ti appaṭikkhittaṃ tañce kappiyaṃ anulometi, akappiyaṃ paṭibāhati, taṃ vo kappati. Yaṃ, bhikkhave, mayā ‘Idaṃ kappatī’ti ananuññātaṃ tañce akappiyaṃ anulometi, kappiyaṃ paṭibāhati, taṃ vo na kappati. Yaṃ, bhikkhave, mayā ‘Idaṃ kappatī’ti ananuññātaṃ, tañce kappiyaṃ anulometi, akappiyaṃ paṭibāhati, taṃ vo kappatī

    • counting vassa
      • different ways of counting vassa
        • at the beginning of the vassa retreat
        • at the end of the retreat (problematic)
          • if vassa (seniority) is counted at the end of the vassa, a pacchimika who has ordained before another monk who entered purimikāvassa will be one vassa after his junior
        • at the beginning of the vassāna season (less problematic)
        • at the end of the vassāna season (less problematic)
        • the day of one's higher ordination (less problematic)
      • mgvhr: it is better if the vassa is counted either in the beginning or the end of the vassāna season or on the day of one's ordination
