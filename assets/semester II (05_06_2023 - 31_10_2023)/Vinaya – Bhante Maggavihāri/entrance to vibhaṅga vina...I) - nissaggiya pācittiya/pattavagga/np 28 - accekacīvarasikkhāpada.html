np 28 - accekacīvarasikkhāpada #edited


  • background
    • The Tathāgata was residing at the Jetavana temple near the city of Sāvatthi. One senior minister was looking to travel to a far off region and sent a messenger to convey a message to the bhikkhus as follows: “Sir! Please visit me. I will offer the vassāvāsika robes.” The bhikkhus did not visit him as by then the Buddha had allowed only the monks who had finished the vassa to accept vassāvāsika robes. At this instance, the senior minister began to bad-mouth the bhikkhus. The Buddha who got to know this declared: “Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, accekacīvaraṃ paṭiggahetvā nikkhipituṃ.” Then the bhikkhus accepted acceka cīvaras and surpassed (?) the robe season (cīvarakālasamaya). Based on this matter, the following training rule was imposed. mem aid: Jetavana minister informs the monks he wishes to offer vassāvāsika robe at that time only those who had finished vassa could accept it so they did not go they were criticized
  • training rule in pāḷi
    • Dasāhānāgataṃ kattikatemāsikapuṇṇamaṃ, bhikkhuno paneva accekacīvaraṃ uppajjeyya, accekaṃ maññamānena bhikkhunā paṭiggahetabbaṃ, paṭiggahetvā yāva cīvarakālasamayaṃ nikkhipitabbaṃ. Tato ce uttariṃ nikkhipeyya, nissaggiyaṃ pācittiyaṃ. A bhikkhu receives an accekacīvara ten days before the pavāraṇa. The bhikkhu who is aware of this must accept it. It can be accepted and kept till the cīvara kāla samaya (suitable season for making robes) (without determining it). If it is kept beyond this period, the bhikkhu will be committing a nissaggiya pācittiya offence.
