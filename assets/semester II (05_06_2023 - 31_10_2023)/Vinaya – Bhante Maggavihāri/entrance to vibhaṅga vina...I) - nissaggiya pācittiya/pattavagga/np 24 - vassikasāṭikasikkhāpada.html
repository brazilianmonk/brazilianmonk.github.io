np 24 - vassikasāṭikasikkhāpada #edited


  • background
    • The Tathāgata was residing at the Jetavana monastery near the city of Sāvatthi. The Chabbaggiya bhikkhus went in search of vassikasāṭika (rain robes) well ahead of rainy season saying that the Buddha had recommended vassikasāṭika for bhikkhus. The stitching was done and the robes were used well in advance. Due to have been used in advance, vassikasāṭika robes decayed during the rainy season. As a result, some monks of the Chabbaggiya group took baths naked in open areas. Other bhikkhus started criticising this act. They informed the Tathāgata also. It is then that this training rule was imposed. mem aid: Jetavana, chabbaggiyas procured, made and worn vassikasāṭikacīvaras well before vassāna and the robes decayed during the rainy season, leading them to bathe naked in public
  • training rule in pāḷi
    • ‘Māso seso gimhānanti, bhikkhunā vassikasāṭikacīvaraṃ pariyesitabbam, ‘aḍḍhamāso seso gimhāna’nti, katvā nivāsetabbaṃ. ‘Orena ce māso seso gimhāna’nti, vassikasāṭikacīvaraṃ pariyeseyya, ‘orenaḍḍhamāso seso gimhāna’nti, katvā nivāseyya, nissaggiyaṃ pācittiyaṃ. Rain robes must be looked for during the last month of the summer. It could be put to use from the last half month of the summer. If one searches for (and obtains) or wears a rain robe before this time frame, a nissaggiya pācittiya offence will be committed.
    • vocab: #[[pāḷi vocab (1st year) ]]
      • orena [ora + ena]↔ind. (+gen) earlier (than); sooner (than); lit. by low
      • ora [ava > o + ra]↔adj. lower; inferior; 2 nt. this side; this bank; near side
  • description
    • a vassikasāṭikacīvara can be procured (pariyesati) during which period?→from the last month of the gimhāna season (āsāḷhī) to the end of the vassāna season; this 5 month-period is called pariyesanakhetta
    • a vassikasāṭikacīvara can be procured (pariyesati), made (karoti) and worn (nivāseti) during which period?→from the sukkapakkha of āsāḷhī (the last pakkha of the gimhāna season) to the end of the vassāna season; this period is called kāraṇa-nivāsana-khetta
    • a vassikasāṭikacīvara can be determined (adhiṭṭhāti) during which period?→only during the vassāna season
    • note:
      • there is a disagreement about the word karaṇa
  • pāḷi terms
    • vassikasāṭikacīvaraṁ↔rain-robe
    • catubiddhaṁ khettaṁ
      • pariyesana khetta↔the period in which vassikasāṭika can be procured which lasts for 5 months (from āsāḷhī to the end of the rainy season) (Māso seso gimhānan'ti bhikkhunā vassikasāṭikacīvaraṃ pariyesitabbam)
      • nivāsana khetta↔the period in which vassikasāṭika can be worn which lasts for 4 1/2 months (the sukkapakkha of āsāḷhī and the rainy season) it is identical with the kāraṇa khetta
      • kāraṇa khetta↔the period in which vassikasāṭika can be made which lasts for 4 1/2 months (the sukkapakkha of āsāḷhī and the rainy season) it is identical with the nivāsana khetta
        • kāraṇa nivāsana khetta↔the period in which vassikasāṭika can be made, and worn which lasts for 4 1/2 months (the last pakkha of āsāḷhī and the rainy season) (aḍḍhamāso seso gimhānan'ti katvā nivāsetabbaṃ); during this period it can also be procured (pariyesana)
      • adhiṭṭhāna khetta↔the period in which vassikasāṭika can be determined which is the 4 months of vassāna, outside of which it is normally kept under vikappana but this requirement changes if a kaṭhina was spread and due to other allowances
    • paṭilābho (masc.) [pati + √labh + *a]↔(+gen) obtaining (of); acquiring (of); gaining (of); personal acquisition (of)
    • paridahita↔pp. put on, clothed, dressed oneself
  • instances of offenses
    • if the robe is kept beyond 10 days without determining it or sharing its ownership: {{01210394145972038::np1}} if received 10 days before vassāna season, it must be made and determined: {{01210394145972038::on the day the rainy season starts}} if received during the vassāna season, it must be made and determined: {{01210394145972038::within 10 days}} if it takes more than that to be made, it must be determined: {{01210394145972038::on the very same day it is completed}} if procured and received before the āsāḷhī: {{01210394145972038::np}} if worn before the last pakkha of āsāḷhī (sukkapakkha): {{01210394145972038::np}} if procured and received mistakenly thinking it to be the right time: {{01210394145972038::np}}
    • np24 apply in two ways
      • for paṭilābha (receiving) it due to pariyesana (searching) outside the pariyesanakhetta
      • for nivāsana (wearing) it outside the kāraṇa-nivāsana-khetta
    • this training rule is (sacittaka / acittaka?) {{2626957782979973::acittaka}} and (saññāvimokkha / nosaññāvimokkha?) {{2626957782979973::nosaññāvimokkha}}
  • aṅgāni (in two ways)
    • paṭilābha due to pariyesana
      • it being for oneself
      • orena ce māso seso gimhānan'ti vassikasāṭikacīvaraṃ pariyeseyya: seeking it outside the pariyesanakhetta
      • receiving the rain robe
    • nivāsana
      • having robes to wear and cover
      • there being no allowed reasons to wear it, such as dangers and so on
      • vassikasāṭikacīvaraṁ: being a rain robe that belongs to oneself
      • orenaḍḍhamāso seso gimhānan'ti katvā nivāseyya: wearing it before the stipulated time period (before the sukkapakkha of āsaḷhī)
  • nissajjana
    • Idaṃ me, bhante, vassikasāṭikacīvaraṃ atirekamāse sese gimhāne pariyiṭṭhaṃ atirekaddhamāse sese gimhāne katvā paridahitaṃ nissaggiyaṃ, imāhaṃ āyasmato nissajjāmi
