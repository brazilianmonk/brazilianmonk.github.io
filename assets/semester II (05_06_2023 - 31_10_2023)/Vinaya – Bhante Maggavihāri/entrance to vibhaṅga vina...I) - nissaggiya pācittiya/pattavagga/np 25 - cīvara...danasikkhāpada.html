np 25 - cīvaraacchindanasikkhāpada #edited


  • background
    • The Tathāgata was residing at the Jetavana monastery near the city of Sāvatthi. Venerable Upananda, said the following to a student monk (saddhivihārika) of his brother who was also a monk: “Friend, come; I will be travelling in the countryside.” Then he replies: “Sir! My robes are weak. Therefore, I’m not going.” Then Venerable Upananda asked him to join in his journey saying, “Come with me, Friend. I shall give you a robe.” This bhikkhu (student of Venerable Upananda’s brother) then heard that the Buddha was also travelling at the same time in the countryside. Therefore, he did not travel with Upananda Thera. This made Venerable Upananda very angry, and he took away the robe that he gave that particular monk. The Buddha who came to know about this incident, imposed this training rule. mem aid: Jetavana Upananda Thera gave another monk a robe for them to travel together but after the monk changed his mind deciding to travel with the Buddha, Upananda Thera got angry and snatched the robe back
  • training rule in pāḷi
    • Yo pana bhikkhu bhikkhussa sāmaṃ cīvaraṃ datvā kupito anattamano acchindeyya vā acchindāpeyya vā, nissaggiyaṃ pācittiyaṃ. If a bhikkhu gives a robe to another bhikkhu, and later on, takes it back or gets someone else to take it back forcefully, out of dissatisfaction and anger, a nissaggiya pācittiya offence will be committed.
    • vocab: #[[pāḷi vocab (1st year) ]]
      • sāmaṃ↔ind. oneself; by oneself; for oneself; 2 ind. individually; personally
      • acchindati [ā + √chid + ṃa + ti]↔pr. (+acc) takes back; snatches back; tears away; 2 pr. breaks off

  • aṅgāni
    • bhikkhussa sāmaṁ...datvā: giving by oneself to a bhikkhu
    • cīvaraṁ: being a vikappanūpaga robe
    • sakasaññā: the perception of it being one's own
    • kupito anattamano acchindeyya vā acchindāpeyya vā: taking it back by oneself or through another person out of an angry mind
  • forfeiture
    • Idaṃ me, bhante, cīvaraṃ bhikkhussa sāmaṃ datvā acchinnaṃ nissaggiyaṃ. Imāhaṃ āyasmato nissajjāmi.
