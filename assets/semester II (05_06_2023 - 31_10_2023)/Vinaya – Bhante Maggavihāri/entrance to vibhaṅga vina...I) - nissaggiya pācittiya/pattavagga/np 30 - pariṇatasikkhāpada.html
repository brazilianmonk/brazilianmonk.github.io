np 30 - pariṇatasikkhāpada #edited


  • background
    • Buddha in: Jetavana, Sāvatthi monks involved: chabbaggiya incident: devotees from Sāvatthi annually offered alms together with robes to the Saṅgha and on one occasion the chabbaggiyas asked for those robes for themselves and being pressurized by them, gave in and handed the robes; the Buddha laid down the rule
  • training rule in pāḷi
    • Yo pana bhikkhu jānaṃ saṅghikaṃ lābhaṃ pariṇataṃ attano pariṇāmeyya, nissaggiyaṃ pācittiyaṃ. If a bhikkhu knowingly allocates a requisite upon himself, which has been set aside to be offered to the Saṅgha, and receives it, a nissaggiya pācittiya offence will be committed
    • vocab: #[[pāḷi vocab (1st year) ]]
      • pariṇāmeti [pari + √nam + *e + ti]↔pr. (+acc & +dat) transfers (to); allocates (to); designates (for); diverts (to); lit. inclines
  • description
    • purpose of the rule→to prevent monks from committing the immoral act of redirecting requisites allocated to the Saṅgha to oneself
  • pāḷi terms
    • pariṇata↔a requisite that has been designated / assigned / allocated by expressing it verbally "I will give this to X"
  • instances of offenses
    • asking for the pariṇata requisites allocated to the Saṅgha for oneself: {{08295525542501925::dkṭ}} receiving it: {{08295525542501925::np}} asking for the pariṇata requisites to be diverged (in any case): {{08295525542501925::dkṭ}} -donation allocated to the saṅgha diverged to yourself: {{08295525542501925::np 30}} another person: {{08295525542501925::pc 82}} another saṅgha / cetiya: {{08295525542501925::dkṭ}} -donation allocated to a cetiya diverged to yourself / another person / another saṅgha / another cetiya: {{08295525542501925::dkṭ}} -donation allocated to another person even a dog diverged to saṅgha / cetiya / another person / yourself: {{08295525542501925::dkṭ}} -donation allocated to yourself diverged to anyone else: {{08295525542501925::no offense}} if asked to be given to oneself not knowing it was allocated to any other recipient: {{08295525542501925::no offense}}
  • nature
    • this sikkhāpada is (sacittaka / acittaka) {{9985929189588225::sacittaka}} and (saññāvimokkha / asaññāvimokkha) {{9985929189588225::saññāvimokkha (yo pana bhikkhu }}{{9985929189588225::jānaṁ...)}}
  • aṅgāni
    • jānaṁ...attano pariṇāmeyya: asking the donor to offer it to oneself knowing that it has been allocated to the Saṅgha
    • saṅghikaṁ lābhaṁ pariṇataṁ: being allocated to be offered to the Saṅgha
    • receiving it for oneself
  • nissajjana
    • Idaṃ me, bhante, jānaṃ saṅghikaṃ lābhaṃ pariṇataṃ attano pariṇāmitaṃ nissaggiyaṃ. Imāhaṃ āyasmato nissajjāmi.
  • notes:
    • diverging is not bandhadeyya because it is not saṅghika yet
    • monks cannot accuse the person who diverged the money of depriving the sangha or a monk of money; no one can blame a monk who prevented something against vinaya; "so bhikkhu issaro"
    • bahukārasutta (5 monks who are of great help to the Saṅgha) (TBC) #q🙋🏼‍♂
      • sīlavanta
      • bahussuta
      • vinayadhara
      • who knows how to do reparations, make robes, etc
      • one who informs his relatives and devotees to offer to the saṅgha
    • donations to future buddhas are questionable
    • cakkavattirājas are born in tāvatimsa, not in apāya
    • a method for dealing with dukkaṭa objects is not given
    • if a monk is asked to whom the donation should be given to, the standard answer is {{2814117872880604::to whatever (person or place) }}{{2814117872880604::inspires }}{{2814117872880604::one, and wherever the donation will }}{{2814117872880604::not be destroyed}}{{2814117872880604:: }}
    • when does the pubbacetanā occur according to vinaya and according to abhidhamma?→according to vinaya, pubba cetanā starts when the donor first says "I will give this to so and so" (pariṇata); while in abhidhamma it starts when the first thoughts of giving occur
    • can a monk ask for a requisite that has been allocated to him to be given?→yes, after it is pariṇata the monks can say 'ok, give it / give it soon, etc'
