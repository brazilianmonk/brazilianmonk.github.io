    • what is the meaning of na ca so, na ca añño (neither him nor another)
      • na ca so: from the perspective of nānattanaya, which focuses on each dhamma as a different reality, the realities that arise in the new life are "not the same"
      • na ca añño: from the perspective of ekattanaya, which focuses on collective dhammas as a unity, the realities that arise in the new life "not another"
      • nānatta- and ekatta-nayas ‒ what are they?
        • nānattanaya (method of multiplicity): perspective that focuses on individual dhammas as different realities
        • ekattanaya (method of unity): perspective that takes santatis, groups of dhammas connected in terms of kamma, kilesas, ārammaṇa, jīvitindriya, anantarapaccaya, and upādāna as one singularity
