12.2 grammar


  • *declension of the noun mātu (f.; satthādigaṇa)
    • nom. mātā / mātaro
    • voc. (bhoti) māta, " / (bhotiyo) "
    • acc. mātaraṃ, (mātuṁ [nrtdpn]) / mātaro, mātare
    • ins/abl. mātuyā, mātyā, mātarā / mātūhi, mātūbhi, mātarehi, mātarebhi
    • dat/gen. mātu, mātuyā, mātyā, mātussa / mātūnaṁ, mātānaṁ, mātarānaṁ
    • loc. mātuyā, mātyā, mātuyaṃ, mātyaṃ, mātari / mātūsu, mātaresu
    • mem aid:
      • closely related to pitu
      • pitunā > mātuyā, mātyā
      • extra forms in abl. and loc.
    • note:
      • dhītu / duhitu (daughter) are similarly declined but without the shortened forms mātyā or mātyaṁ
  • declension of the noun dhītu / duhitu (f.) (satthādigaṇa) #[[final exam pāḷi]]
    • nom. dhītā / dhītaro
    • voc. (bhoti) dhīta, dhītā / (bhotiyo) dhītaro
    • acc. dhītaraṃ / dhītaro
    • ins. & abl. dhītuyā, dhītarā / dhītūhi/ūbhi, dhītarehi/ebhi
    • dat/gen. dhītu, dhītuyā, dhītussa / dhītūnaṁ, dhītarānaṁ, dhītānaṁ
    • loc. dhītuyā, dhītuyaṃ, dhītari / dhītūsu, dhītaresu
    • mem aid:
      • same as mātu, without the mātyā and mātyaṁ forms
  • niggahīta sandhi
    • niggahīta lopa sandhi (sara / vyañjana)
      • sandhi in which the niggahīta is elided, when followed by either a vowel (vidūnaṃ + aggaṃ > vidūna + aggaṃ > vidūnaggaṃ (a remains short due to the saññoga that follows)) or a consonant (ariyasaccānaṃ + dassanaṃ > ariyasaccānadassanaṃ)
      • note:
        • sometimes the object is written in the genitive case: kammassa karaṇaṁ ariyasaccānaṁ dassanaṁ > ariyasaccāna dassanaṁ etaṁ buddhāna(ṁ) sāsanaṁ
    • niggahīta āgama sandhi
      • sandhi in which a niggahīta is inserted cakkhu + udapādi > cakkhuṃ udapādi sometimes between two letters: ta + sampayuttā > taṃsampayuttā (taṃsampayuttakānañca dhammānaṃ)
