### "So that..."

#### Result or consequence

When it expresses a **result or consequence**, it can be followed by a clause **without** modal verbs.

- *The door was left ajar so that the cat walked right in.* (result)
- *The agreement was structured so that each party received fair compensation.* (factual outcome)
- *He explained it simply so that everyone understood the concept.* (result of his explanation)

#### Purpose or possibility

When it expresses a **specific intended purpose** (why something is done) or possibility, it is mostly followed by a clause **with** modal verbs.

- *She studied hard so that she could pass the exam.* (intended purpose/possibility)
- *He spoke quietly so that he wouldn't disturb the others.* (intended purpose/avoidance)

#### Summary

| Meaning | Modals          |
|---------|-----------------|
| Result  | with or without |
| Purpose | mostly with     |
