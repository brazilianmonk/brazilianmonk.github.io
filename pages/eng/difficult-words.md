## B level

On a warm Wednesday, a retired colonel boarded his lavish yacht near a remote island to escape a severe drought. Despite his wealthy bourgeois business background, he hired a personal chauffeur who arrived with a fresh bouquet of flowers for the two women singing in the local choir.

The colonel had a mischievous scheme to bury a hidden cache of gold and settle an old debt. Though he had no doubt it might cause temporary chaos, he walked down the aisle of the galley to gauge the subtle flavors of a vegetable recipe while resting on a comfortable sofa.

To conduct a thorough study on animal psychology, he kept a tamed leopard on board. Navigating through rough and tough waters, the echo of music from a forgotten film genre drifted over the ocean debris. In the end, the sovereign ruler of the boat handed his guests a formal receipt, maintaining his place at the top of the ship's strict hierarchy while everyone else waited patiently in a queue.
