### Meanings of "for"

1. **Purpose or intended recipient**
    - *A gift for you*
    - *A tool for cutting wood*
    - *A course for beginners*

2. **Duration**
    - *Waited for hours*
    - *Lived here for ten years*

3. **Support or favor**
    - *I'm for the new policy*
    - *Are you for or against it?*

4. **Exchange or cost**
    - *Bought it for $10*
    - *Traded his watch for a book*

5. **Benefit or advantage**
    - *Good for your health*
    - *I'll carry that for you*

6. **Reason or cause**
    - *Praised for honesty*
    - *Thank you for helping me*

7. **Destination or aim**
    - *Heading for the mountains*
    - *Reached for the book*

8. **Suitability or appropriateness**
    - *Not suitable for children*
    - *Perfect for summer weather*

9. **Substitution or representation**
    - *Spoke for the whole team*
    - *Stood in for the manager*

10. **Comparison or standard**
    - *Tall for his age*
    - *Expensive for a paperback*

11. **Idiomatic expressions**

| Expression          | Meaning            |
|---------------------|--------------------|
| for good            | permanently        |
| for now             | temporarily        |
| for sure            | definitely         |
| for the sake of     | in the interest of |
| for all I know      | as far as I know   |
| for crying out loud | frustration        |
| for better or worse | regardless of outcome |

12. **Literary or archaic use (conjunction-like)**
    - *He stayed home, for he was ill.*
    - *We must hurry, for the sun is setting.*
