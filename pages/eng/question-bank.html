B1 Relative Clauses B1 Grammar Q14
Explain the difference in meaning: (a) The Buddha, who attained arahatship, taught the Dhamma. (b) The Buddha who attained arahatship taught the Dhamma.
Answer
(a) is a non-defining (non-restrictive) relative clause, set off by commas — it gives extra information about the Buddha, who is already uniquely identified; attaining arahatship is not needed to identify him.
(b) is a defining (restrictive) relative clause, with no commas — it implies there could be more than one "Buddha" being discussed, and specifies that it is the one who attained arahatship who taught (distinguishing him from others).

a. The teachers refuse ________ the problem to continue. (to allow, allowing, allow)
b. Hermione reassured ______ they had not been cursed. (them that, that, to them that)
c. Harry promised _________ anything that would put his friends in danger. (not doing, not to do, to not do)
d. Teachers are demanding __________ Defense Against the Dark Arts. (that students be taught, students to be taught, that students to be taught)
Answer
a. to allow
b. them that
c. not to do
d. that students be taught
C1 Passive Voice C1 Final Exam Q10
Turn the sentence passive — don't include the agent.
a. The wizarding community thinks the sport of Quidditch was invented in England.
b. Professor Quirrell claimed that he was innocent.
c. Someone said that there is a three-headed dog in the school.
Answer
a. The sport of Quidditch is thought to have been invented in England.
b. Professor Quirrell claimed to be innocent.
c. There is said to be a three-headed dog in the school.

C1 Cleft Sentences C1 Final Exam Q12
Rewrite the sentence giving a different emphasis.
a. Hagrid invited us to his hut yesterday. → It was Hagrid…. / It was yesterday… / It was to his hut….
b. Hagrid told Harry he was a wizard on his 11th birthday. → It was Hagrid… / It was on his 11th birthday… / It was Harry…
c. Hagrid said that Fluffy was a gentle creature. → It was Fluffy… / It was Hagrid… / It was a gentle creature…
Answer
a. It was Hagrid who invited us to his hut yesterday. / It was yesterday that Hagrid invited us to his hut. / It was to his hut that Hagrid invited us yesterday.
b. It was Hagrid who told Harry he was a wizard on his 11th birthday. / It was on his 11th birthday that Hagrid told Harry he was a wizard. / It was Harry (who/that) Hagrid told he was a wizard on his 11th birthday.
c. It was Fluffy that Hagrid said was a gentle creature. / It was Hagrid who said that Fluffy was a gentle creature. / It was a gentle creature that Hagrid said Fluffy was.
