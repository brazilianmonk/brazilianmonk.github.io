## Common Sayings / Expressions / Proverbs

1. **Actions speak louder than words.**
    - What you do is more important and shows your true intentions more clearly than what you say.
2. **Bite the bullet.**
    - To endure a difficult or unpleasant situation with courage and stoicism.
3. **Don't cry over spilled milk.**
    - There's no point in getting upset over something bad that has already happened and cannot be changed.
4. **Every cloud has a silver lining.**
    - Every difficult or unpleasant situation has some hidden benefit or positive aspect.
5. **Get cold feet.**
    - To become nervous or hesitant about doing something one had planned to do.
6. **Hit the nail on the head.**
    - To describe exactly what is causing a situation or problem.
7. **It's raining cats and dogs.**
    - It's raining very heavily.
8. **Kill two birds with one stone.**
    - To achieve two things at the same time with a single action.
9. **Let the cat out of the bag.**
    - To accidentally reveal a secret.
10. **Once in a blue moon.**
    - Something that happens very rarely.
11. **Practice makes perfect.**
    - Regularly repeating an activity is the best way to become good at it.
12. **The early bird catches the worm.**
    - Those who start early or act promptly are more likely to succeed.
13. **When in Rome, do as the Romans do.**
    - When visiting a foreign place, you should follow the customs of the people who live there.
14. **Where there's a will, there's a way.**
    - If you are determined enough, you can find a way to achieve what you want, even if it's difficult.
15. **Break a leg!**
    - A friendly expression used to wish someone good luck, especially before a performance.
16. **Add insult to injury.**
    - To make a bad situation even worse by doing something else that is offensive or hurtful.
17. **Barking up the wrong tree.**
    - To pursue a mistaken course of action; to make a wrong assumption about something.
18. **Burn the midnight oil.**
    - To work late into the night.
19. **Cost an arm and a leg.**
    - Something is very expensive.
20. **Don't put all your eggs in one basket.**
    - Don't risk everything on a single venture; diversify your risks.
21. **Get something off your chest.**
    - To talk about something that has been worrying you for a long time.
22. **In the same boat.**
    - To be in the same difficult or unpleasant situation as others.
23. **Jump on the bandwagon.**
    - To join others in doing something popular or successful.
24. **No pain, no gain.**
    - You have to work hard and endure difficulties to achieve success.
25. **Under the weather.**
    - Feeling slightly ill or unwell.
26. **"Physician, heal thyself."**
    - This implies that one is skilled at identifying and solving problems for others, but often struggles to apply that same wisdom or insight to their own issues.
27. **"The shoemaker's children go barefoot."**
    - This refers to someone who provides a service or product to others, but neglects to provide it for their own family or self.
