### Present Perfect Simple vs Continuous

<table border="2" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="6" rules="groups" frame="hsides">


<colgroup>
<col  class="org-left" />

<col  class="org-left" />

<col  class="org-left" />
</colgroup>
<thead>
<tr>
<th scope="col" class="org-left"><b>Situation / Focus</b></th>
<th scope="col" class="org-left"><b>P. P. Simple</b></th>
<th scope="col" class="org-left"><b>P. P. Continuous</b></th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td class="org-left">Completed action (result/quantity)</td>
<td class="org-left">I've painted the room</td>
<td class="org-left">-</td>
</tr>

<tr>
<td class="org-left">How many / how much</td>
<td class="org-left">I've read 3 books this month</td>
<td class="org-left">-</td>
</tr>

<tr>
<td class="org-left">Permanent or finished result</td>
<td class="org-left">She's broken her leg</td>
<td class="org-left">-</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td class="org-left">Duration / how long (temporary)</td>
<td class="org-left">-</td>
<td class="org-left">I've been living here for 2 years</td>
</tr>

<tr>
<td class="org-left">Ongoing or repeated action</td>
<td class="org-left">-</td>
<td class="org-left">She's been calling him all day</td>
</tr>

<tr>
<td class="org-left">Recent activity (evidence now)</td>
<td class="org-left">-</td>
<td class="org-left">Why are you so dirty? → I've been gardening</td>
</tr>

<tr>
<td class="org-left">Emphasis on the activity itself</td>
<td class="org-left">-</td>
<td class="org-left">I've been working hard (that's why I'm tired)</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td class="org-left">Stative verbs</td>
<td class="org-left">I've known him since 2000.</td>
<td class="org-left">- (almost never used)</td>
</tr>

<tr>
<td class="org-left">Both possible (slight difference)</td>
<td class="org-left">I've learned a lot today</td>
<td class="org-left">I've been learning a lot today (focus on process)</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

Quick rule:  

-   Result / how many → Simple
-   Process / how long / recent activity → Continuous
