### Refining Common Adjectives

- **Good**: excellent, superb, commendable, virtuous, proficient, wholesome
- **Bad**: terrible, detrimental, pernicious, abysmal, deleterious, harmful
- **Happy**: joyful, joyous, elated, ecstatic, content, jovial, glad, enraptured, delighted
- **Sad**: melancholic, despondent, somber, lugubrious, doleful, depressed, sorrowful, gloomy, desolate
- **Big**: enormous, gigantic, ginormous, substantial, vast, mammoth, huge, massive, towering, large, immense, colossal
- **Small**: tiny, minute, diminutive, microscopic, paltry, mini, narrow (mind), minuscule, infinitesimal, minor, pocket-size, small-scale, slight, petite
- **Old**: ancient, aged, venerable, obsolete, antiquated, elderly, senile, advanced in years
- **New**: novel, pristine, contemporary, innovative, modern
- **Pretty**: beautiful, exquisite, attractive, alluring, captivating, aesthetic
- **Important**: crucial, significant, pivotal, paramount, essential, key
