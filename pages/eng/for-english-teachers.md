## English Language Course Structure
### English Language Foundational Course Structure

-   **Target Students**: IIT English Medium Students
-   **Duration**: 3 years
-   **Goal**: learning the English grammar and vocabulary according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFRL) emphasizing on grammar, vocabulary, and writing skills
-   **Grammar Source**:
    -   A1-B2: [Test-English.com](https://test-english.com/)
-   **Vocabulary Source**: [(here](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.co.uk/wordlists/oxford3000-5000))
    -   American Oxford 3.000 by CEFR level
    -   American Oxford 5.000 by CEFR level
    -   Oxford Phrase List 750 phrases
    -   OPAL: 2420 words/phrases (~150/week)
        -   Written Single words: 1200 (8 weeks)
        -   Written Phrases: 370 (2 weeks)
        -   Spoken Single Words: 600 (4 weeks)
        -   Spoken Phrases: 250 (2 weeks)
-   **Listening Source**: Audiobooks: 3 h / week


### English Language Advanced Course Structure

-   **Target Students**: students who have completed the Foundational Course and aspire for mastery of the language, for teaching positions in IIT or outside, for studying, teaching, or living abroad in English speaking countries, etc.
-   **Duration**: 2 years
-   **Goal**: Review of weak grammar points, writing practice, research, speaking, public speaking, teaching, etc. aiming at mastery and familiarity with the language on a high level
-   **Speaking**
    -   Pronunciation
    -   Public Speaking
-   **Writing**
    -   Essay
    -   Summaries
    -   Short Stories
    -   POVs
    -   Tone
    -   Punctuation
-   **Teaching** English Language

## Suggested Principles
### Pronunciation
#### Step-by-step
- Learn the pronunciation of each word individually
- Learn how to join sounds (some sounds are dropped when words are combined)
- Learn where to stop in the sentence (commas, periods, phrases)
- Learn intonation, pitch (rise and fall) at the right occasions (rise in questions, sustain in commas, descend in periods)
- Gradually increase the difficulty and speed

#### Resources
- [**Tongue Twisters**](/summaries/eng/tongue-twisters)
- [**Difficult Words**](/summaries/eng/difficult-words)

### Speaking
#### Step-by-step
- Start with a small audience and safe environment: just the teacher or another student, no recordings, no presentations.
- Correct the student but also allow the conversation to flow (don't correct too much, which leads to low confidence and mental blockage)
- Gradually increase the audience: a student, the teacher, small group, bigger group, entire class, presentations, recordings, etc.

## Grading System

This is the current grading system. The system rewards:
- **Participation**: graded based on the attendance, attitude, and homeworks. This is to encourage and reward continuous effort and dedication, not only for a test.
- **Academic Results**: graded based on tests and final exams. This is to encourage and reward knowledge acquisition and content mastery.
- **Beyond-expected Results**: Bonuses can be given for exceptional, beyond-expected results, such as perfect attendance and so on. This is to encourage and reward going beyond what is expected.


- **40 % Participation**
  - Attendance X Attitude: Sick and duty days are not counted
  - Homeworks
  - Listening Homeworks
- **60 % Academic Results**
  - Tests During Semester
	- Vocabulary Test
	- Writing Tests
	- Speaking Tests
	- Listening Comprehension Tests
  - Final Exam
- **0-5% Bonus**
  - Perfect Attendance
  - Perfect Test Scores, etc.
