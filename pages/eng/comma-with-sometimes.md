### Comma with "sometimes"

In which situations should we add a comma after "sometimes" when it begins a sentence?

#### With comma

Add a comma after "sometimes" at the beginning of a sentence in these situations:

1. **When it introduces a longer or more complex introductory phrase or clause** that follows it.
    - *Sometimes, when the sun is setting, the sky turns incredible colors.*
    - *Sometimes, after a long day, all I want is a quiet evening.*

2. **When it acts as a "sentence adverb"** modifying the entire statement, often creating a slight pause or emphasizing the condition. This is often a stylistic choice, but common for clarity or flow.
    - *Sometimes, it's hard to know what to do.*
    - *Sometimes, I just need a moment to myself.*

3. **For emphasis or rhetorical effect**, to create a deliberate pause.
    - *Sometimes, the simplest solutions are the best.*

#### Without comma (normally)

When you can omit the comma (and often do):

- When "sometimes" **directly modifies the verb that immediately follows it**, and the sentence is short and clear without the pause.
    - *Sometimes I go for a walk.* (Here, "sometimes" directly tells when you go.)
    - *Sometimes she forgets her keys.*
