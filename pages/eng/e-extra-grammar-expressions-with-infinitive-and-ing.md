# Expressions with "to + infinitive"

-   **It was so nice OF him to explain the grammar point.**
    -   He explained the grammar point.
    -   What was nice was the action he did.
-   **It was so brave OF her to ask me to marry her.**
    -   she aske me to marry her
    -   her action was brave
-   Different meaning:
    -   **The class was really nice FOR Sumana.**
        -   The class was nice.
        -   Sumana liked the class.
    -   **I believe this meditation subject is good FOR you.** (it will help you)
-   TO DECIDE TO GO -> DECISION TO GO
-   TO ADVISE TO DO -> ADVICE TO DO


# Expressions with "-ing"

-   **I can't help** (I can't avoid)
    -   I can't help noticing you are tired.
    -   I can't help but laughing
    -   I can't help watching TV
    -   I can't help it.
-   **I have touble -ing** (it is difficult for me to do that)
    -   I have touble sleeping (I cannot sleep)
    -   I have trouble waking up in the morning. (It is difficult for me to wake up)
    -   I have touble memorizing vocabulary.
-   **I have a hard time -ing**
    -   I have a hard time getting used to Anki.
    -   I have a hard time typing as fast as Brazil Bhante.
    -   I have a hard time speaking English like a native.
