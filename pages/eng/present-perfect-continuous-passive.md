# Present Perfect Continuous Passive

## Is it correct?
**Yes**, grammatically. But **no**, it is almost never used in real life.

## Why is it avoided?
- **Clunky & Awkward:** The sequence "has been being" sounds heavy and unnatural.
- **Rhythm:** Native speakers avoid the jarring repetition of "-ing" sounds.

## What do we use instead?

| Instead of...                                       | Use this...                                   | Example                                                |
|:----------------------------------------------------|:----------------------------------------------|:-------------------------------------------------------|
| *The car **has been being repaired** for 2 hours.*  | **Present Perfect Passive** (Focus on result) | *The car **has been repaired**.*                       |
| *The project **has been being reviewed** all week.* | **Active Voice** (Focus on the doer)          | *The committee **has been reviewing** the project.*    |
| *The house **has been being built** since Jan.*     | **Noun Phrase** (Simpler & natural)           | *The house **has been under construction** since Jan.* |

## The Verdict
- **Use it?** ❌ No. Avoid it completely.
- **Understand it?** ✅ Yes, if you see it in advanced texts.
- **Better Strategy:** Master the alternatives (Passive, Active, or Noun Phrases) for fluent English.
