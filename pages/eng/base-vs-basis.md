## Base vs. Basis

**Base**

- **Foundation (literal or figurative):** The bottom part of something; a starting point.
    - The **base** of the statue.
    - Her argument lacked a factual **base**.
- **Main ingredient / component:**
    - The sauce has a tomato **base**.
- **Military installation:**
    - An air force **base**.

**Basis**

- **Fundamental reason or principle:** The underlying justification or groundwork for an idea, decision, or system. Almost always abstract.
    - What is the **basis** for your claim?
    - They met on a regular **basis**.
    - The decision was made on the **basis** of strong evidence.

**Key difference:** "Base" can be concrete (the bottom) or a starting point/foundation. "Basis" refers almost exclusively to the **reason** or **principle** upon which something is founded or done.
