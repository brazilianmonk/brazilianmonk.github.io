      • Adinnādānasikkhāpadaṁ #editing
        • background
          • while the Buddha was residing in the city of Rājagaha, the King Bimbisāra of Magadha issued a statement saying that all timbre in the forests of his kingdom could be used by the monks. Ven. Dhaniya who then had built several kuṭis for himself that had been subsequently destroyed, (did what?) used the statement to steal timber from the king's reserves. The Buddha then declared the second prjk and set the value of the object of prjk to be 5 pādas or more. A group of chabbaggiya bhikkhus (did what?) stole cloth from a forest area and the Buddha amended the rule adding "gāmā vā araññā vā" to include both village and forest.
          • attitude of a judging bhikkhu
            • a judging bhikkhu should always consider the monk as innocent until proven guilty and should try to find righteous ways his innocence is proven. If concluded as being guilty the offender should be sent to several vinaya teachers for their opinion and given a meditation subject before taking conclusions. The conclusion should be done by the offender himself.
