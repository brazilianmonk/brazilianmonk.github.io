<li><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>methunadhammasikkhāpada</b></mark>
<b>Pārājiko hotīti seyyathāpi nāma puriso sīsacchinno abhabbo tena sarīrabandhanena jīvituṃ, evameva bhikkhu methunaṃ dhammaṃ paṭisevitvā assamaṇo hoti asakyaputtiyo. Tena vuccati – ‘Pārājiko hotī’ti.</b> 
As a man who has been beheaded cannot live further by fixing the disembodied head to the headless body, a monk who has sex cannot be a recluse or a son of the Buddha any longer. Owing to that fault, he ceases to be a recluse or a son of the Buddha. </li>
<li><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>adinnādānasikkhāpadaṁ</b></mark>
<b>Pārājiko hotīti seyyathāpi nāma paṇḍupalāso bandhanā pavutto abhabbo haritatthāya, evameva bhikkhu pādaṃ vā pādārahaṃ vā atirekapādaṃ vā adinnaṃ theyyasaṅkhātaṃ ādiyitvā assamaṇo hoti asakyaputtiyo. Tena vuccati – ‘Pārājiko hotī’ ti.</b> 
As a leaf withered turned brown and fallen from the branch is not possible to turn green again, a monk who has stolen a thing that is  worthy of a pāda or an extra-pāda cannot be a recluse or a son of the Buddha any longer. Owing to that fault, he ceases to be a recluse or a son of the Buddha. </li>
<li><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>manussaviggahasikkhāpadaṁ</b></mark><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;">

As a large rock split into two halves cannot be refixed, a monk who intentionally deprives a human being of its life cannot be a recluse or a son of the Buddha any longer. Owing to that fault, he ceases to be a recluse or a son of the Buddha.</li>
<li><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>uttarimanussadhammasikkhāpadaṁ</b></mark>
<b>Pārājiko hotīti seyyathāpi nāma tālo matthakacchinno abhabbo puna virūḷhiyā, evameva bhikkhu pāpiccho icchāpakato asantaṃ abhūtaṃ uttarimanussadhammaṃ ullapitvā assamaṇo hoti asakyaputtiyo. Tena vuccati – ‘Pārājiko hotī’ ti.</b> 
As a palm tree of which the top (head) is cut off does not grow any more, a monk who has falsely claimed himself to possess sublime attainments with evil desires to gain praise and gain cannot be a recluse or a son of the Buddha any longer. Owing to that fault, he ceases to be a recluse or a son of the Buddha  </li>
