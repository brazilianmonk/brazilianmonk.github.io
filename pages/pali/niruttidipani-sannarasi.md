## <i><b>saññā</b></i>rāsi (collection of names)

### <i><b>garusaññārāsi</b></i> (collection of weighty terms)

#### garusaññārāsi <br><i><b>map</b></i>

##### <i><b>vaṇṇo</b></i>

###### <i>1. aādayo titālīsa(ṃ) vaṇṇā</i>

- <i>atthaṁ vaṇṇenti pakāsentīti vaṇṇā</i>
- <i>nakkharanti khayavayaṃ na gacchantīti akkharā</i>
##### <i><b>saro</b></i>

###### 2.<i><b>dasādo sarā</b></i>

- <i><b>sayameva laddhasarūpā hutvā rājanti virocentīti sarā</b></i>
##### <i><b>savaṇṇo</b></i>

###### <i>3.</i><i><b>dve dve savaṇṇā</b></i>

- <i><b>samāno vaṇṇo suti etesan'ti savaṇṇā</b></i>
##### <i><b>rasso</b></i>

###### 4.<i><b>pubbo rasso</b></i>

- <i><b>rassakālo nāma akkhidalānaṁ ummisananimmisanasamakālo</b></i>
- <i><b>tattha e., o. iti dve ekapadasaṃyoge pare kvaci labbhanti</b></i>
  - ekapadasaṁyoga
    - ettha
  - padantara- / nānā-saṁyoga
    - maṁ ce tvaṁ nikhaṇaṁ vane
##### <i><b>dīgho</b></i>

###### 5. <i><b>paro dīgho</b></i>

- <i><b>dighakālo nāma rassehi diguṇakālo</b></i>
##### <i><b>byañjano</b></i>

###### 6. <i><b>kādayo byañjanā</b></i>

- <i><b>atthaṁ byañjayantīti byañjanā</b></i>
  - <i><b>te pana suddhā addhamattikā, </b></i><br><i><b>rassayuttā diyaddhamattikā, </b></i><br><i><b>dīghayuttā tiyaddhamattikā</b></i>

###### 7. <i><b>pañcapañcakā vaggā</b></i>

##### <i><b>niggahītaṃ</b></i>

###### 8. <i><b>bindu niggahītaṃ</b></i>

- <i><b>niggayha gayhati uccāriyatīti niggahītaṃ</b></i>
#### 1. <i><b>aādayo</b></i>

- <i><b>aādayo bindantā tecattālīsakkharā vaṇṇā nāma honti.</b></i>
- **vocab:** see the [Part I Vocabulary](/summaries/pali/niruttidipani-part-1-vocab) page
- <i><b>a, ā, i, ī, u, ū, e, ē, o, ō. </b></i><br><i><b>ka, kha, ga, gha, ṅa, </b></i><br><i><b>ca, cha, ja, jha, ña, </b></i><br><i><b>ṭa, ṭha, ḍa, ḍha, ṇa, </b></i><br><i><b>ta, tha, da, dha, na, </b></i><br><i><b>pa, pha, ba, bha, ma, </b></i><br><i><b>ya, ra, la, va, sa, ha, ḷa, aṃ.</b></i>
- <i><b>atthaṃ vaṇṇenti pakāsentīti</b></i>
- **vocab:** see the [Part I Vocabulary](/summaries/pali/niruttidipani-part-1-vocab) page
- <i><b>akkharāti ca vuccanti, nāmapaññattirūpattā </b></i>(due to the state of being a name of the sound) (letters are names given to sounds)<i><b> nakkharanti </b></i><i>(do not undergo destruction)</i><i><b> khayavayaṃ na gacchantīti</b></i>
- "rūpaṁ jīrati maccānaṁ<br>nāmagottaṁ na jīrati"<br>the rūpa of mortal decays,<br>names and clans do not decay
- **vocab:** see the [Part I Vocabulary](/summaries/pali/niruttidipani-part-1-vocab) page
#### 2.<i><b>dasādo sarā</b></i>

- <i><b>Tesu vaṇṇesu ādimhi dasa vaṇṇā sarā nāma honti. </b></i><u><i><b>Sa</b></i></u><i><b>yameva laddhasarūpā hutvā </b></i><i>(having gained their own sound)</i> <u><i><b>rā</b></i></u><i><b>janti virocantīti</b></i>
- ratti (f.) (loc. sg.) rattiyaṁ, rattiyā, ratyaṁ, <i><b>ratto</b></i><br>ādi (m.) ⇒ (loc. sg.) ādimhi, ādismiṁ, <i><b>ādo</b></i>
- short version: <i><b>sayameva</b></i> (sa) <i><b>rājantīti</b></i> (rā) <i><b>sarā</b></i> (sa + rā)
- **vocab:** see the [Part I Vocabulary](/summaries/pali/niruttidipani-part-1-vocab) page
#### 3.<i><b>dve dve</b></i>

- <i><b>Tesu saresu dve dve sarā savaṇṇā nāma honti.</b></i>
- <i><b>A, ā avaṇṇo, i, ī ivaṇṇo, u, ū uvaṇṇo, e, ē evaṇṇo, o, ō ovaṇṇo.</b></i>
- <i><b>Samāno vaṇṇo</b></i> <i><b>suti etesan'ti</b></i>
- **vocab:** see the [Part I Vocabulary](/summaries/pali/niruttidipani-part-1-vocab) page
#### 4.<i><b>pubbo rasso</b></i>

- <i><b>Dvīsu dvīsu savaṇṇesu yo yo pubbo hoti, so so rasso nāma hoti. Rassena kālena vattabbāti rassā, rassakālo nāma akkhidalānaṃ ummisananimmisanasamakālo. </b></i>(<i><b>exam</b></i>)
- **vocab:** see the [Part I Vocabulary](/summaries/pali/niruttidipani-part-1-vocab) page
- <i><b>Tattha e., o. iti dve ekapadasaṃyoge pare </b></i><u><i><b>kvaci</b></i></u><i><b> labbhanti </b></i>(<i><b>exam</b></i>)<i><b>. Eṭṭhi, seṭṭho, oṭṭho, sotthi. Ekapadasaṃyogeti kiṃ? Padantarasaṃyoge pare rassā mā hontūti. Maṃ ce tvaṃ nikhaṇaṃ vane, putto tyāhaṃ mahārāja. </b></i><u><i><b>Kvacī</b></i></u><i><b>ti kiṃ? Ekapadasaṃyogepi vaggantesu vā ya, ra, la, vesu vā paresu rassā mā hontūti </b></i>(study)<i><b>. Enti, senti, eyya, bhāseyya, meṇḍo, soṇḍo.</b></i>
- <i><b>vagganta</b></i>: ṅ, ñ, ṇ, n, m
- 2 types of <i><b>saṁyoga </b></i>(<i><b>exam with example</b></i>)
- <i><b>ekapada</b></i>saṁyoga: when the succeeding conjunct is in the same word - such as in <i><b>etth</b></i>a
  - such saṁyoga is pronounced short
- <i><b>padantara- / nānāpada-</b></i>saṁyoga: when the succeeding conjunct is in the other term - such as in c<i><b>e tv</b></i>aṁ (maṁ ce tvaṁ nikhaṇaṁ vane)
  - such vowel is pronounced long
- <i><b>note</b></i>:
  - when can short e and o be found in pāḷi?
- when vaggantas or y / r / l / v are succeeding, the vowel is long - such as bhās<i><b>ey</b></i>ya (controversial)
- in kaccāyana if a conjunct succeeds, the e / o are short
- acaritvā brahmacariyaṁ<br>aladdhā yobbane dhanaṁ<br>senti cāpā'tikhīṇāva<br>purāṇāni anutthunaṁ
- **vocab:** see the [Part I Vocabulary](/summaries/pali/niruttidipani-part-1-vocab) page
#### 5. <i><b>paro dīgho</b></i>

- <i><b>dvīsu dvīsu savaṇṇesu yo yo paro hoti, so so dīgho nāma hoti. dīghena kālena vattabbāti dīghā, dīghakālo nāma rassehi diguṇakālo. </b></i><i>(</i><i><b>exam</b></i><i>)</i>
#### 6. <i><b>kādayo </b></i>(ka ādayo)<i><b> byañjanā</b></i>

- <i><b>tesu vaṇṇesu kādayo bindantā vaṇṇā byañjanā nāma honti. atthaṃ byañjayantīti byañjanā </b></i><i>(</i><i><b>exam</b></i><i>)</i><i><b>. te pana suddhā </b></i>(pure, not mixed)<i><b> addhamattikā </b></i>(half measure / mora)<i><b>, rassayuttā </b></i>(combined with short vowels)<i><b> diyaddhamattikā </b></i>(SL diyaḍḍha - 1 1/2)<i><b>, dīghayuttā tiyaddhamattikā </b></i>(2 1/2)<i><b>.</b></i>
- mo·ra (môr′ə)
- <i>n.</i> <i>pl.</i> mo·rae (môr′ē) or mo·ras
- 1.  The minimal unit of metrical time in quantitative verse, equal to the short syllable.
- 2.  <i>Linguistics</i> A unit of phonological quantity based on both vowel quantity and the occurrence of consonants within a given syllable.
- <i><b>mattāsaddo</b></i> (the term mattā) <i><b>accharāsaṅghāta</b></i> (snapping) <i><b>akkhinimīlana</b></i> (blinking) <i><b>saṅkhātaṁ</b></i> (called as) <i><b>kālaṁ vadati</b></i> (rūpasiddhi)
- <i><b>ekamatto bhave rasso</b></i><br><i><b>dvimatto dīghamuccate</b></i><br><i><b>timatto tu pluto ñeyyo</b></i><br><i><b>byañjanañcaḍḍhamattikaṁ</b></i><br>one mora is short, (a)<br>two long, (ā)<br>three should be known as pluta, (āa) <br>and a consonant is half mora (k)<br><i><b>dūravhāne ca gāne ca</b></i><br><i><b>rodane ca pluto mato</b></i><br>pluta should be known in three places, far calling, singing and crying
  - <i><b>note</b></i>:
    - when a short vowel is at the end of a line of a verse - that is also considered as long
    - ka = 1 1/2 mora<br>kā = 2 1/2 mora
- **vocab:** see the [Part I Vocabulary](/summaries/pali/niruttidipani-part-1-vocab) page
#### 7. <i><b>pañcapañcakā vaggā</b></i>

- <i><b>tesu byañjanesu kādi-mantā </b></i>(starting with k and ending with m) <i><b>pañcabyañjanapañcakā </b></i>(5 pentads of consonants)<i><b> vaggā nāma honti. kādi pañcako kavaggo, cādi cavaggo, ṭādi ṭavaggo, tādi tavaggo, pādi pavaggo. sesā avaggāti siddhaṃ </b></i>(18)<i><b>. vaṇṇuddese ekaṭṭhānikānaṃ byañjanānaṃ vagge samūhe niyuttāti </b></i>(those which are combined into a group)<i><b> vaggā</b></i>
- **vocab:** see the [Part I Vocabulary](/summaries/pali/niruttidipani-part-1-vocab) page
- <i><b>note</b></i>:
- why each letter can be called vagga? they can be called so for being part of the vagga (in the sense of 'part of the group')
- when the meaning of a term is not clear, grammarians add another term to clarify it (atthaṁ vaṇṇenti <i><b>pakāsentī</b></i>ti vaṇṇā)
#### 8. <i><b>bindu niggahītaṃ</b></i>

- <i><b>ante </b></i>(at the end)<i><b> bindumatto </b></i>(mere dot)<i><b> vaṇṇo niggahītaṃ nāma. niggayha gayhati uccāriyatīti niggahītaṃ </b></i><i>(</i><i><b>exam</b></i><i>)</i><i><b>. garusaññārāsi niṭṭhito.</b></i>
- in kaccāyana niggahīta is "aṁ" - it is older than moggallāna
- niggahita uses the nose, ṅ uses throat + nose (confusing)
- <i>buddha</i><i><b>ṁ gh</b></i><i>ana - sa</i><i><b>ṅgh</b></i><i>a</i>
- **vocab:** see the [Part I Vocabulary](/summaries/pali/niruttidipani-part-1-vocab) page
