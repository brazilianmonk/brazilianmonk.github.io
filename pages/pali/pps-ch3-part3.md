### niggahīta sandhi

- ñakārādesa sandhi: sandhi in which the ṁ, when followed by ‘h', ‘e’ and ‘y’, changes into ñ
  - ṁ + h > ñh: evaṃ + hi > evañhi (evañhi vo bhikkhave sikkhitabbaṃ) ṁ + e > ññe (double): paccattaṃ + eva > paccattaññeva (paccattaññeva parinibbissāmi) ṁ + y > ññ: saṃ + yācikāya > saññācikāya (saññācikāya pana bhikkhunā kuṭiṃ kārayamānena)

### parassara dīgha sandhi

- sandhi in which the previous vowel is elided and the following is lengthened (vuddhi) such as: buddha + anussati > buddh + anussati > buddh + ānussati > buddhānussati; yāni + idha > yānīdha (yānīdha bhūtāni samāgatāni); appassuto + ayaṃ > appassutāyaṃ (appassutāyaṃ puriso); saddhā + idha > saddhīdha (saddhīdha vittaṃ purisassa seṭṭhaṃ)

### niggahīta sandhi

## Lesson 13

- when is the suffix -āya used in the purisādigaṇa?
  - when the dative case of the purisādigaṇa has the meaning of "for", the suffix used is -āya instead of -assa (tassatthāya "for his benefit"), therefore "namo buddhāya" is not in accordance with the pāḷi grammar but is a sanskritization, since the sanskrit grammar allows such use
- 27 sabbanāmas (pronouns) in Pāḷi (divided into 6 groups):
  - sabba, katara, katama, ubhaya (collection of 2), itara, añña
  - aññatara, aññatama
    - all above are decined as sabba
      - except sabba and ubhaya, the others have 'i' form as katarissa
  - pubba, para, apara, dakkhiṇa, uttara, adhara
    - decined as sabba, but with added purisādi forms in m. and nt., nom.pl., abl.sg. and loc.sg. - pubbā, pubbe
  - (from here onwards, no ālapana and each with its own declension)
  - ya (like sabba), ta, eta, ima, amu, kiṃ
  - eka, ubha, dvi, ti, catu
  - tumha, amha

### cakārādesa sandhi

- sandhi in which the syllable ‘ti’ of the prefixes 'ati-', 'pati-' and indeclinable 'iti', is substituted by the consonant ‘c’ which is then doubled as 'cc' (due to dvebhāvasandhi) pati- (pati + ayo > pac + ayo > paccayo) ati- (ati + antaṃ > ac + antaṃ > accantaṃ) iti (iti + etaṃ > ic + etaṃ > iccetaṃ)

### Dakārādi avasesa ādesa sandhi

- sandhi in which the consonant ‘dh’ changes into ‘d’ (ekamidāhaṃ bhikkhave samayaṁ ⇒ ekamidha + ahaṃ ⇒ ekamidh + ahaṃ ⇒ ekamidh + āhaṃ ⇒ ekamid + āhaṃ ⇒ ekamidāhaṃ)
- sandhi in which the consonant ‘dh’ changes into ‘h’ when followed by 'u' (sādhu dassanamariyānaṃ ⇒ sāhu dassanamariyānaṃ)

### Yakārādesa sandhi

- sandhi in which the vowels 'e' and 'i' are substituted by 'y', when followed by a vowel and a dissimilar vowel respectively 'e' + vowel (me + ayaṃ > my + ayaṃ > myāyaṃ [adhigato kho myāyaṃ dhammo]) (note: a becoming ā is a vyañjana sandhi) 'i' + dissimilar vowel (paṭisanthāravutti + assa > paṭisaṅthāravuttyassa > paṭisaṅthāravutyassa (tty becomes ty due to another sandhi)
  - according to grammar, when three consonants are together and two of them are similar, the first among them will be elided; this is called byañjana visaṃyoga

### Vakārādesa sandhi

- sandhi in which the vowels ‘o’ and ‘u’, when followed by a vowel, are substituted by ‘v’ 'o' + vowel (atha kho assa > atha khvassa) 'u' + vowel (anu + eti ⇒ anveti)

### Abbha-ajjha ādesa sandhi

- sandhi in which the prefixes ‘abhi’ and 'adhi', when followed by a vowel, are substituted by ‘abbha’ and ‘ajjha’ respectively abhi -> abbha (abhi + okāso -> abbha + okāso -> abbhokāso [pubbassaralopasandhi]) adhi -> ajjha (adhi + agama -> ajjha + agamā -> ajjhagamā)

## Lesson 14

- why the verb labhati is with a short a, while the noun lābha is with a long ā?: because the noun is constructed by adding the vikaraṇapaccaya ṇa as so: labh + (ṇ)a > lābha (noun) labh + a + ti > labhati (verb)
- -tha (suffix) (tattha, yattha, kattha, kuttha, sabbattha, ettha [eta+tha]): suffix added to pronouns with locative meaning, sometimes meaning "to" as the goal of motion; the word becomes a nipāta
- 5 types of nāma (nouns)
  - nāmanāma (proper nouns)
  - sabbanāma (pronouns)
  - samāsanāma (compound nouns): constructed by combining two nouns
  - taddhitanāma (secondary derivatives): constructed by adding a suffix to a noun
  - kitakanāma (primary derivative): constructed by adding a suffix to a root (tbc)
- divādigaṇa verbs may have active or passive meaning such as nassati or muccati; since the vikaraṇa paccaya used in both meanings is ya

### vyañjana sandhi

- lopāgama sandhi: sandhi in which the vowel ‘o’ is elided, and 'a' is inserted when followed by a consonant so + ve = sa ve ([sa ve kāsāvamarahati])
- o ādesa sandhi: sandhi in which the prefix ava- is substituted by the vowel 'o' when followed by a consonant (ava + sānaṃ ⇒ o + sānaṃ ⇒ osānaṃ; the same occurs to okāso, otarati, and oruhati)
- paṭi ādesa sandhi: sandhi in which the prefix pati- changes into paṭi- when followed by a vowel or a consonant (paṭaggi dātabbo [pati + aggi ⇒ paṭi + aggi])
- dīgha sandhi: sandhi in which a vowel is lengthened when followed by a consonant; this mostly happens in gāthās (evaṃ gāme munī care [muni + care])
- rassa sandhi: sandhi in which a vowel is shortened when followed by a consonant; this mostly happens in gāthās (puggalā dhammadasā > puggala dhammadasā)
- asadisa dvebhāva sandhi: when an aspirated consonant is duplicated, its unaspirated counterpart is augmented (paṭhama + jhāna > paṭhamajjhānaṃ)

## Lesson 15

### Asavaṇṇādesasandhi

- sandhi in which the vowels i / ī is substituted by e, and the vowels u / ū are substituted by o; these substitutions happen only when the dissimilar previous vowel is elided (upa + ikkhati = upekkhati) (yathā udake ⇒ yath_ + odake ⇒ yathodake)
- the vowels e and o do not have another vowel with similar sound so they are called asavaṇṇa

## Lesson 17

- kālātipatti vibhatti (conditional mode)
  - kālātipatti vibhatti is used to express:
    - an action that could have happened but didn't
    - an action that could have happened but was prevented by to another action
    - the action that prevented the main action
    - note:
      - sometimes both actions are expressed by kālātipatti
        - 'sace so paṭhamavaye pabbajjaṃ alabhissa, arahā abhavissa'
      - sometimes both sattamī and kālātipatti verbs are used in the same sentence
        - 'sace sukkā dhammā lokaṃ na pāleyyuṃ, sambhedaṃ loko agamissa'
  - verbal terminations of kālātipatti
    - (a)....(i)ssā, (a)....(i)ssa / (a)....(i)ssaṃsu
    - (a)....(i)sse / (a)....(i)ssatha
    - (a)....(i)ssaṃ / (a)....(i)ssāmhā, (a)....(i)ssāmha
    - note:
      - while forming verbs in the kālātipatti vibhatti, as in the case of ajjatanī vibhatti, the vowel ‘a’ is augmented at the beginning of the root and the vowel ‘i’ is augmented at the end of the root
      - long endings ā can become short a
