- Cakkhupālattheravatthu - III
  - part I
    - "kiṃ panetaṃ, āvuso, patirūpaṃ, nanu appamattehi bhavitabbaṃ"? "mayañhi dharamānakassa buddhassa santikā kammaṭṭhānaṃ gahetvā āgatā, buddhā ca nāma na sakkā pamādena ārādhetuṃ, kalyāṇajjhāsayena te (the Buddhas) vo (by you) ārādhetabbā. pamattassa ca nāma cattāro apāyā sakagehasadisā, appamattā hothāvuso"ti. "kiṃ tumhe pana, bhante"ti? "ahaṃ tīhi iriyāpathehi vītināmessāmi, piṭṭhiṃ na pasāressāmi, āvuso"ti. "sādhu, bhante, appamattā hothā"ti.
      - notes:
        - the question is 'etam patirūpaṁ', and 'kiṁ panetaṁ' shows it is a question
        - āvuso is used in the plural here - it is a nipāta
        - nanu shows a negative question

      - “But, friends, is this proper? It should be done with heedfulness, shouldn't it? We came after having taken the meditation subject from the living Buddha, and those called Buddhas cannot be pleased by heedlessness, you should please them by means of virtuous determination. The four woeful states are like the home of the those called heedless. May you be heedful, friends!", "But what about you, venerable sir?", "I will spend my time with three postures. I shall not stretch out (lay down) the back, friends.", "Well done, venerable sir! May you be heedful!".
    - Atha therassa niddaṃ anokkamantassa paṭhamamāse atikkante majjhimamāse sampatte akkhirogo uppajji. Chiddaghaṭato udakadhārā viya akkhīhi assudhārā paggharanti. So sabbarattiṃ samaṇadhammaṃ katvā aruṇuggamane gabbhaṃ pavisitvā nisīdi. Bhikkhū bhikkhācāravelāya therassa santikaṃ gantvā, “Bhikkhācāravelā, bhante”ti āhaṃsu. “Tena hi, āvuso, gaṇhatha pattacīvara”nti. Attano pattacīvaraṃ gāhāpetvā nikkhami. Bhikkhū tassa akkhīhi assūni paggharante disvā, “Kimetaṃ, bhante”ti pucchiṃsu.
      - Then, when the first month was over and the middle month was reached, an eye-ailment arose to the thera who was not lying down to sleep. Like streams of water from a cracked water-pot, streams of tears ozzed out from (his) eyes. Having done the contemplative's practice every night, when dawn broke, he entered his cell and sat. At the time of going for alms, the monks approached the thera and said, "Bhante, it is time for going for alms". "If it is so, friends, take the bowl and robe". Having caused them to take his bowl and robe, he departed. Seeing the tears ozzing out from his eyes the monks asked: "What is this, bhante?"
      - when using iva/viya, the verb in that clause should be -anta/-māna
      - tena hi: uyyojanatthanipāta

    - "akkhīni me, āvuso, vātā (hetvatthapañcamī?) vijjhantī"ti. "nanu, bhante, vejjena pavāritamhā, tassa kathemā"ti. "sādhāvuso"ti te vejjassa kathayiṃsu. so telaṃ pacitvā pesesi. thero nāsāya telaṃ āsiñcanto nisinnakova āsiñcitvā antogāmaṃ pāvisi. vejjo taṃ disvā āha – "bhante, ayyassa kira akkhīni vāto vijjhatī"ti? "āma, upāsakā"ti. "bhante, mayā telaṃ pacitvā pesitaṃ, nāsāya vo telaṃ āsitta"nti? "āma, upāsakā"ti.
      - "Friends, the wind(s) pierces my eyes". "Bhante, we have been invited by the doctor, haven't we? Let us talk to him". "It is good, friends". They talked to the doctor. He cooked and sent a (medicinal) oil. The elder, while remaining seated, poured the oil through the nostril(s) and having done that, entered the village. The doctor, upon seeing him, asked "Bhante, is it said that the wind pierces the venerable's eyes?". "Yes, devotee". "Bhante, an oil was cooked and sent by me. Has the oil been poured through the nostrils by you?". "Yes, devotee".

    - "idāni kīdisa"nti? "rujjateva upāsakā"ti. vejjo "mayā ekavāreneva vūpasamanasamatthaṃ telaṃ pahitaṃ, kiṃ nu kho rogo na vūpasanto"ti cintetvā, "bhante, nisīditvā vo telaṃ āsittaṃ, nipajjitvā"ti pucchi. thero tuṇhī ahosi, punappunaṃ pucchiyamānopi na kathesi. so "vihāraṃ gantvā therassa vasanaṭṭhānaṃ olokessāmī"ti cintetvā – "tena hi, bhante, gacchathā"ti theraṃ vissajjetvā vihāraṃ gantvā therassa vasanaṭṭhānaṃ olokento caṅkamananisīdanaṭṭhānameva disvā sayanaṭṭhānaṃ adisvā, "bhante, nisinnehi vo āsittaṃ, nipannehī"ti pucchi. thero tuṇhī ahosi.
      - "How is it now?", "It really hurts, devotee". The doctor, after considering: "The oil which is able to cure with just one single application was sent by me. Why was the ailment not cured?", asked: "Bhante, was the oil spinkled by you while sitting or while lying?". The elder remained silent and even after being asked repeatedly did not speak. He (the doctor) then thought, "I will go to the monastery and take a look at the dwelling place of the elder" - "If that is so, bhante, you may go", he answerd the elder. He (the doctor) went to the monastery and while taking a look at the elder's dwelling place he saw only the walking-meditation place and sitting places, but not seeing the lying place, he asked: "Bhante, was it applied by you while sitting or while lying down?". The elder remained silent.

    - "mā, bhante, evaṃ karittha, samaṇadhammo nāma sarīraṃ yāpentena sakkā kātuṃ, nipajjitvā āsiñcathā"ti punappunaṃ yāci. "gaccha tvaṃ tāvāvuso (first), mantetvā jānissāmī"ti vejjaṃ uyyojesi. therassa ca tattha neva ñātī, na sālohitā atthi (nipāta, plural meaning), tena saddhiṃ manteyya?
      - "Bhante, do not do like this. The so called contemplative practice can only be done by continuing to live. Apply it while lying down!" the doctor begged repeatedly. "You go first, friend. After taking council I'll know what to do" he dismissed the doctor. But the elder had neither relatives, nor blood relations there. With whom could he take council?
      - sakkā goes with tumanta
      - atthi here (na sālohitā atthi) is a nipāta which can be used with singular or plural

    - karajakāyena pana saddhiṃ mantento "vadehi tāva, āvuso pālita, tvaṃ kiṃ akkhīni olokessasi, udāhu buddhasāsanaṃ? anamataggasmiñhi saṃsāravaṭṭe tava akkhikāṇassa gaṇanā nāma natthi, anekāni pana buddhasatāni buddhasahassāni atītāni. tesu te ekabuddhopi na pariciṇṇo, idāni imaṃ antovassaṃ tayo māse na nipajjissāmīti temāsaṃ nibaddhavīriyaṃ karissāmi. tasmā te cakkhūni nassantu vā bhijjantu vā, buddhasāsanameva dhārehi, mā cakkhūnī"ti bhūtakāyaṃ ovadanto imā gāthāyo abhāsi –
      - Therefore, deliberating with his own person, "Firstly, friend Pālita, say this - will you focus on your eyes or the dispensation of the Awakened One? In the round of rebirths that has indeed no conceivable beginning there is no calculating the number of times you were blind of one eye / your eyes were blind, but many hundreds of Buddhas, many thousands of Buddhas have passed. Not even one among them was attended by you. Now, in these three months of the rainy season I will not lie down. I will exert continuous effort for three months. Therefore, let your eyes be squandered or come apart, uphold the dispensation of the Awakened One only, not your eyes!". He uttered these verses admonishing the conglomeration of elements:

    - "cakkhūni hāyantu mamāyitāni, sotāni hāyantu tatheva kāyo, sabbampidaṃ hāyatu dehanissitaṃ, kiṃ kāraṇā pālita tvaṃ pamajjasi. "cakkhūni jīrantu mamāyitāni, sotāni jīrantu tatheva kāyo, sabbampidaṃ jīratu dehanissitaṃ, kiṃ kāraṇā pālita tvaṃ pamajjasi. "cakkhūni bhijjantu mamāyitāni, sotāni bhijjantu tatheva kāyo, sabbampidaṃ bhijjatu dehanissitaṃ, kiṃ kāraṇā pālita tvaṃ pamajjasī"ti.
      - Let the eyes, regarded as mine, dwindle, let the ears dwindle, likewise the body, let all of this connected with the body dwindle, Due to what cause, Pālita, are you heedless?" Let the eyes, regarded as mine, decay, let the ears decay, likewise the body, let all of this connected with the body decay, Due to what cause, Pālita, are you heedless?" Let the eyes, regarded as mine, come apart, let the ears come apart, likewise the body, let all of this connected with the body come apart, Due to what cause, Pālita, are you heedless?"

    - evaṃ tīhi gāthāhi attano ovādaṃ datvā nisinnakova natthukammaṃ katvā gāmaṃ piṇḍāya pāvisi. vejjo taṃ disvā "kiṃ, bhante, natthukammaṃ kata"nti pucchi. "āma, upāsakā"ti. "kīdisaṃ, bhante"ti? "rujjateva upāsakā"ti. "nisīditvā vo, bhante, natthukammaṃ kataṃ, nipajjitvā"ti. thero tuṇhī ahosi, punappunaṃ pucchiyamānopi na kiñci kathesi. atha naṃ vejjo, "bhante, tumhe sappāyaṃ na karotha, ajjato paṭṭhāya ‘asukena me telaṃ pakka’nti mā vadittha, ahampi ‘mayā vo telaṃ pakka’nti na vakkhāmī"ti āha. so vejjena paccakkhāto vihāraṃ gantvā "tvaṃ vejjenāpi paccakkhātosi, iriyāpathaṃ mā vissajji samaṇāti.
      - In this way, having admonished himself with these three verses and done the nasal treatment while sitting, he went for alms (lit. he entered for alms). The doctor, seeing him, inquired: "Bhante, was the nasal treatment done?". "Yes, devotee". "How is it, bhante?", "It hurts, devotee". "Bhante, was the nasal treatment done by you while sitting or while lying down?". The elder remained silent. Even after being asked repeatedly, he did not say anything. Then the doctor told him, "Bhante, you do not do what is beneficial, from today onwards do not say a medicinal oil was prepared for me by so and so, and I too shall not say a medicinal oil was prepared by me for you". Having been given up by the doctor and gone back to the monastery "You have even been given up by the doctor, do not relinquish your postures, contemplative".

    - "paṭikkhitto tikicchāya, vejjenāpi vivajjito, niyato maccurājassa, kiṃ pālita pamajjasī"ti. – imāya gāthāya attānaṃ ovaditvā samaṇadhammaṃ akāsi. athassa majjhimayāme atikkante apubbaṃ acarimaṃ akkhīni ceva (ca eva) kilesā ca bhijjiṃsu. so sukkhavipassako arahā hutvā gabbhaṃ pavisitvā nisīdi.
      - Abandoned by the physician, avoided by the doctor too, destined to the king of death, why, Pālita, are you heedless?" After admonishing himself with this verse, he engaged in the contemplative practice. Then, when the middle watch was over, not before, not later, his eyes and the defilements were destroyed. Becoming a bare-insight worthy one he entered the chamber and sat down.

    - bhikkhū bhikkhācāravelāya āgantvā "bhikkhācārakālo, bhante"ti āhaṃsu. "kālo, āvuso"ti? "āma, bhante"ti. "tena hi gacchathā"ti. "kiṃ tumhe pana, bhante"ti? "akkhīni me, āvuso, parihīnānī"ti. te tassa akkhīni oloketvā assupuṇṇanettā hutvā, "bhante, mā cintayittha, mayaṃ vo paṭijaggissāmā"ti theraṃ samassāsetvā kattabbayuttakaṃ vattapaṭivattaṃ katvā gāmaṃ piṇḍāya pavisiṃsu. manussā theraṃ adisvā, "bhante, amhākaṃ ayyo kuhi"nti pucchitvā taṃ pavattiṃ sutvā yāguṃ pesetvā sayaṃ piṇḍapātamādāya gantvā theraṃ vanditvā pādamūle parivattamānā roditvā, "bhante, mayaṃ vo paṭijaggissāma, tumhe mā cintayitthā"ti samassāsetvā pakkamiṃsu.
      - The monks came at the time for alms round and informed, "Bhante, it is the time for the alms round.", "Is it, friends?", "Yes, bhante". "In that case, you may go", "But what about you, bhante?", "Friends, my vision is lost (lit. my eyes are destroyed)". After inspecting his eyes, their eyes became full of tears and they said, "Bhante, do not think thus. We will take care of bhante". They reassured the elder, performed the duties and minor responsibilities that were suitable to be done, and entered the village for alms. The people, not seeing the elder, asked - "Bhante, where is our elder?". Upon hearing the news they sent porridge, took his own alms food, went to the monastery, payed respects to the elder and rolling at his feet lamented - "Bhante, we will look after bhante, may bhante not worry (lit. think)!". After reassuring him, they left.

    - tato paṭṭhāya nibaddhaṃ yāgubhattaṃ vihārameva pesenti. theropi itare saṭṭhi bhikkhū nirantaraṃ ovadati. te tassovāde ṭhatvā upakaṭṭhāya pavāraṇāya sabbeva saha paṭisambhidāhi arahattaṃ pāpuṇiṃsu. te vuṭṭhavassā ca pana satthāraṃ daṭṭhukāmā hutvā theramāhaṃsu, "bhante, satthāraṃ daṭṭhukāmamhā"ti. thero tesaṃ vacanaṃ sutvā cintesi
      - From then onwards, they regularly sent rice and porridge directly to his dwelling. And the elder admonished the other sixty bhikkhus uninterruptedly. Having been established in his admonishments, when the Invitation was approaching, all of them together achieved arahatship with discriminating knowledges. And the Rains being finished, they became desirous of seeing the Teacher and told the elder: "bhante, we are desirous of seeing the Teacher". Hearing their announcement, the elder thought:

    - "- ahaṃ dubbalo, antarāmagge ca amanussapariggahitā aṭavī atthi, mayi (loc.) etehi saddhiṃ gacchante sabbe kilamissanti, bhikkhampi labhituṃ na sakkhissanti, ime puretarameva pesessāmī"ti. atha ne āha – "āvuso, tumhe purato gacchathā"ti. "tumhe pana bhante"ti? "ahaṃ dubbalo, antarāmagge ca amanussapariggahitā aṭavī atthi, mayi tumhehi saddhiṃ gacchante sabbe kilamissatha, tumhe purato gacchathā"ti.
      - "I am weak and there is wilderness owned by non-humans along the road. While I am going with them, everyone will grow tired. They will also not be able to get alms-food. I will surely send them ahead." Then he said - "Friends, may you go ahead". "But what about you, bhante?" I am weak and there is wilderness owned by non-humans along the road. While I am going with you, everyone will grow tired. May you go ahead."

  - part II
    - "mā, bhante, evaṃ karittha, mayaṃ tumhehi saddhiṃyeva gamissāmā"ti. "mā vo, āvuso, evaṃ ruccittha, evaṃ sante mayhaṃ aphāsukaṃ bhavissati, mayhaṃ kaniṭṭho pana tumhe disvā pucchissati, athassa mama cakkhūnaṃ parihīnabhāvaṃ āroceyyātha, so mayhaṃ santikaṃ kañcideva pahiṇissati, tena saddhiṃ āgacchissāmi, tumhe mama vacanena dasabalañca asītimahāthere ca vandathā"ti te uyyojesi.
      - "Bhante, do not say that. We will only go with you". "Friend, do not do so / (don't prefer in this way / You don't want to do thus). If so, it will be uncomfortable for me. But my younger brother, after seeing you, will inquire. Then you may inform him of my vision having been lost (of my eyes' state of being destroyed). He shall send someone to my presence and with such person I shall go. May you pay respect to the One with the Ten Powers and the eighty Great Elders with my word." (Saying that,) he dismissed them.

    - te theraṃ khamāpetvā antogāmaṃ pavisiṃsu. manussā te disvā nisīdāpetvā bhikkhaṃ datvā "kiṃ, bhante, ayyānaṃ gamanākāro paññāyatī"ti? "āma, upāsakā, satthāraṃ daṭṭhukāmamhā"ti. te punappunaṃ yācitvā tesaṃ gamanachandameva ñatvā anugantvā paridevitvā nivattiṃsu. tepi anupubbena jetavanaṃ gantvā satthārañca asītimahāthere ca therassa vacanena vanditvā punadivase yattha therassa kaniṭṭho vasati, taṃ vīthiṃ piṇḍāya pavisiṃsu. kuṭumbiko te sañjānitvā nisīdāpetvā katapaṭisanthāro "bhātikatthero me, bhante, kuhi"nti pucchi. athassa te taṃ pavattiṃ ārocesuṃ.
      - After having the elder forgive them, they entered the village. The people, upon seeing them, had them sit and after having given them alms asked - "Bhante, has the venerables' manner of going appeared?" - "Yes, devotees, we wish to see the Teacher". They begged them repeatedly, but understanding their desire to go regardless, they followed after them and as they cried they turned back. The monks indeed went by stages to Jetavana and worshipped the Teacher and the eighty great elders with the elder's word. The next day, they entered for alms on the road where the elder's younger brother dwelt. The householder, having recognized them, had them sit and after doing the hospitalities asked, "Bhante, where is my elder brother?". They then informed him of the tidings.

    - so taṃ sutvāva tesaṃ pādamūle parivattento roditvā pucchi – "idāni, bhante, kiṃ kātabba"nti? "thero ito kassaci āgamanaṃ paccāsīsati, tassa gatakāle tena saddhiṃ āgamissatī"ti. "ayaṃ me, bhante, bhāgineyyo pālito nāma, etaṃ pesethā"ti. "evaṃ pesetuṃ na sakkā, magge paripantho atthi, taṃ pabbājetvā pesetuṃ vaṭṭatī"ti. "evaṃ katvā pesetha, bhante"ti. atha naṃ pabbājetvā aḍḍhamāsamattaṃ pattacīvaraggahaṇādīni sikkhāpetvā maggaṃ ācikkhitvā pahiṇiṃsu.
      - Just as he heard it, he rolled about their feet crying and asked, "Bhante, what should be done now?". "The elder expects the arrival of someone from here who will accompany him when his time of coming approaches". "Bhante, this is a nephew of mine called Pālita. Do send him". "It is not possible to send him in these circumstances. There is an obstruction on the way. It is suitable to send him after having ordained him". "After doing thus do send him, bhante". Then, having ordained him and trained him in using the bowl, robes, etc for just half a month, they described the way to him and sent him.
    - so anupubbena taṃ gāmaṃ patvā gāmadvāre ekaṃ mahallakaṃ disvā, "imaṃ gāmaṃ nissāya koci āraññako vihāro atthī"ti pucchi. "atthi, bhante"ti. "ko nāma tattha vasatī"ti? "pālitatthero nāma, bhante"ti. "maggaṃ me ācikkhathā"ti. "kosi tvaṃ, bhante"ti? "therassa bhāgineyyomhī"ti.
      - Having reached that village travelling by stages, he saw a certain elderly citizen and asked, "Is there any forest monastery that depends on this village?", "Yes, bhante", "What is the name of the one who dwells there?", "The elder's name is Pālita, bhante", "Please show me the way", "Who are you, bhante?", "I'm the elder's sister's son".
    - atha naṃ gahetvā vihāraṃ nesi. so theraṃ vanditvā aḍḍhamāsamattaṃ vattapaṭivattaṃ katvā theraṃ sammā paṭijaggitvā, "bhante, mātulakuṭumbiko me tumhākaṃ āgamanaṃ paccāsīsati, etha, gacchāmā"ti āha. "tena hi imaṃ me yaṭṭhikoṭiṃ gaṇhāhī"ti.
      - Then, the elder citizen took and led him to the monastery. After paying respect to the elder, doing the major and minor duties for as much as half a month, and taking proper care of the elder, the novice said: "Bhante, the uncle householder expects bhante's arrival with me. Come, let us go." "In that case, grab hold of this end of my walking-stick."

  - part III
    - so yaṭṭhikoṭiṃ gahetvā therena saddhiṃ antogāmaṃ pāvisi.
      - Grabbing the end of the walking-stick, the novice entered the village along with the elder.
    - manussā theraṃ nisīdāpetvā "kiṃ, bhante, gamanākāro vo paññāyatī"ti pucchiṃsu. "āma, upāsakā, gantvā satthāraṃ vandissāmī"ti. te nānappakārena yācitvā alabhantā theraṃ uyyojetvā upaḍḍhapathaṃ gantvā roditvā nivattiṃsu. sāmaṇero theraṃ yaṭṭhikoṭiyā ādāya gacchanto antarāmagge aṭaviyaṃ kaṭṭhanagaraṃ nāma therena upanissāya vuṭṭhapubbaṃ gāmaṃ sampāpuṇi, so gāmato nikkhamitvā (refers to the woman) araññe gītaṃ gāyitvā dārūni uddharantiyā ekissā itthiyā gītasaddaṃ sutvā sare nimittaṃ gaṇhi. itthisaddo viya hi añño saddo purisānaṃ sakalasarīraṃ pharitvā ṭhātuṃ samattho nāma natthi. tenāha bhagavā – "nāhaṃ, bhikkhave, aññaṃ ekasaddampi samanupassāmi, yaṃ evaṃ purisassa cittaṃ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati, yathayidaṃ, bhikkhave, itthisaddo"ti (a. ni. 1.2).
      - The people, after having had the elder sit, asked, ""Bhante, has the venerable's manner of going appeared (is bhante leaving)?" "Yes, devotees, after having left, I shall pay respect to the Teacher." They begged him (to stay) in many ways, but without getting it they saw him off, going (with him) half of the way, then, crying, turned back. The novice took (lit. having taken) the end of the walking-stick of the elder, and while he was travelling, he reached a village where the elder had dwelt in dependence on in the past (lit. a village that was dwelt and depended on previously by the elder) called Kaṭṭhanagara. Having heard the sound of a song of a certain woman, who had gone out of the village and was picking up firewood and singing a song in the forest, the novice took the sign of (lit. in) that voice. There is indeed no other such sound that is capable of pervading the entire body of men and remaining as the sound of women. On that account, the Blessed One said: "Monks, I do not see even one other sound which completely preoccupies and fixates the mind of a man in this way, monks, as the form of a woman."
      - the gerunds refer to the following verb
        - nikkhamitvā gāyitvā refer to uddharantiyā
        - sutvā refers to gaṇhi
