## Meanings of pañcamī vibhatti



-   **apādānattha / avadhi attha**: the place from where one separates or moves away
    -   gāmā apenti munayo (the monks depart from the village)
-   **hetvattha / kāraṇattha**
    -   avijjāpaccayā saṅkhārā
-   **aññavibhattiyattha** 
    -   **karaṇattha**
        -   sahatthā dānaṃ deti (he gives alms with his own hand)
    -   **sahayoga**   
        -   saha parinibbānā (together with parinibbāna)
        -   saha bodhipatiṭṭhānā (together with the establishment of bodhi tree (in anurādhapura))
        -   note:
            -   saha with pañcamī: before word
            -   saha with tatiyā: after word (normally)
    -   **bhāvalakkhaṇa** (rare)
        -   *uppādā* vā bhikkhave tathāgatānaṃ *anuppādā* vā tathāgatānaṃ *ṭhitāva* sā dhātu yadidaṃ idappaccayatā (monks! whether tathāgatas arise or not, the principle of dependent origination remains)
            -   (*:q: clarify uppādā*)
        -   explained under Meanings of chaṭṭhī vibhatti(#org78878e5)

### apādāna- / avadhi- attha &#x2013; definition


**ito apanetvā aññaṃ ādadātīti apādānaṃ**: that from which something is removed, taken elsewhere, departs

-   apa: having removed (apanetvā)
-   ādāna: takes up (ādadāti)
-   apādāna = avadhi (boundary, limit, limitation)
-   note:
    -   this is the apādānakāraka
    -   this is the main meaning but others exist (see below)

gāmā apenti munayo (the monks depart from the village) 

-   gāmā: place from which they depart &#x2013; apādāna

    

-   3 main apādānas &#x2013; acc. to the verb

    
    
    -   **niddiṭṭhavisaya** (explicitly expressed action): the verb connected with the apādāna is directly expressed
        -   gāmā apenti munayo
        -   (*:q: why is visaya rendered as action here?*)
    -   **upāttavisaya** (implied action): the verb is not directly expressed but must be mentally inserted
        -   valāhakā (nikkhamitvā) vijjotate vijju (lightning flashes (having come out) from the clouds)
        -   agārasmā (nikkhamitvā) anagāriyaṃ pabbajito (he went forth to the monk life (having left) the household life)
        -   (*:q: clarify upātta*)
    -   **anumeyyavisaya** (inferred action): the verb is neither directly expressed nor mentally inserted but must be inferred
        -   māthurā pāṭaliputtakehi abhirūpatarā (the people of Mathura city are more beautiful than those of Pāṭaliputta)
        -   sīlameva sutā seyyo (morality is greater than learning)
        -   (*:q: clarify difference between upātta and anumeyya*)
    
    **niddiṭṭhavisayaṃ kiñci, upāttavisayaṃ tathā,
    anumeyyavisayañca, apādānaṃ tidhā mataṃ** (rpsd)
    
    
    -   4 other types of apādāna (extra)
    
        
        
        -   (saṁyogapubba)
            -   **kāyasaṃyogapubba apādāna** (preceded by bodily connection): physical connection must exist prior to separation
                -   gāmā apenti munayo: before departing, the sages must have been physically present in the village
            -   **cittasaṃyogapubba apādāna** (preceded by mental connection): mental connection prior to separation
                -   pāpā cittaṃ nivāraye (one should restrain the mind from evil)
        -   (movement)
            -   **cala apādāna** (moving ablative): separation from something that moves
                -   puriso dhāvatā assā patati (the man falls from a running horse)
                    -   dhāvatā assā: a moving source
            -   **acala apādāna** (static ablative): separation from something static
                -   gāmā apenti munayo (the monks depart from the village)
        
        

-   27 meanings of apādāna

    
    
    -   **place of departure**
        -   gāmā apenti munayo
    -   **place of origin**
        -   himavatā pabhavanti pañcamahānadiyo (the five great rivers originate from the himalayas)
    -   **that from which something arises**
        -   kāmato jāyatī soko (sorrow is something that arises from desire)
            -   kāmato: can also be interpreted as hetvattha
        -   yāni kānici bhikkhave bhayāni uppajjanti, sabbāni tāni bālatoyeva uppajjanti, no paṇḍitato (monks, all fears arise from fools, not from the wise)
    -   **cause of fear**
        -   corā bhayaṃ jāyati (fear arises from the thief)
        -   pāpato uttasati (he trembles from evil)
            -   notes:
                -   also with dutiyā in kammattha: paralokaṃ bhāyeyya (one should fear the next world)
                -   also with chaṭṭhī: sabbe bhāyanti maccuno (all fear death)
    -   **one by whom someone is defeated**
        -   buddhasmā parājenti aññatitthiyā (other sectarians are defeated from (by) the Buddha)
    -   **one from whom learning is obtained**
        -   ācariyato sippaṃ gaṇhāti (he learns a craft from the teacher)
        -   dvāsītiṃ buddhato gaṇhiṃ (i learned eighty-two (dhammakkhandhas) from the buddha)
            -   dvāsītiṁ buddhato gaṇhiṁ
                dve sahassāni bhikkhuto
                carurāsīti sahassāni
                ye me dhammā pavattino
        -   yamhā dhammaṃ vijāneyya, sammāsambuddhadesitaṃ (from whom one gets to know the dhamma preached by the buddha)
        -   ito sutvā (having heard from here)
    -   with **aññatra / aññaṁ / tato / etc** meaning “other than”
        -   nāññatra dukkhā sambhoti, nāññaṃ dukkhā nirujjhati (nothing arises apart from suffering; nothing perishes apart from suffering)
        -   tato aparena samayena (at another time)
    -   with **prefixes of avoidance &#x2013; apa / pari**
        -   apasālāya āyanti vāṇijā (the merchants come avoiding the hall)
        -   paripabbata devo vassati - “the rain falls avoiding the mountain.”
    -   with **ā-**
        -   mariyādā (within boundary): the boundary is not included in the area
            -   āpabbatā khettaṃ (the field extends up to the mountain): does not include the mountain
                -   (*:q: shouldn't it be āpabbatā?*)
        -   abhividhi (inclusion): the boundary is included in the area
            -   ābrahmalokā saddo abbhuggacchati (the sound rises up to (and including) the brahma world)
    -   with **pati-** (in place of / instead of) 
        -   buddhasmā pati sāriputto dhammaṃ deseti (sāriputta teaches in place of the buddha)
        -   telasmā pati ghataṃ dadāti (he gives ghee instead of oil; he exchanges ghee with oil)
    -   with **rite, vinā, aññatra, puthu, nānā, yāva**
        -   rite (without) 
            -   rite saddhammā kuto sukhaṃ? (without the true dhamma, from where could there be happiness?)
                -   note: also with dutiyā: rite saddhammaṃ
        -   vinā (without) 
            -   vinā saddhammā natthañño koci nātho (without the true dhamma, there is no other refuge)
                -   note: also with dutiyā / tatiyā: vinā saddhammaṃ, vinā saddhammena
        -   aññatra (apart from) 
            -   aññatra saddhammā, aññatra saddhammaṃ, aññatra saddhammena
        -   puthu (separate from) 
            -   ariyehi puthu jano (a person separated from the Noble Ones)
        -   nānā (separated; away) 
            -   te bhikkhū nānā kulā (those monks are away from the family)
            -   piyehi manāpehi nānābhāvo (separation from the dear and pleasant ones)
        -   yāva (up to, as far as) 
            -   yāva brahmalokā saddo abbhuggacchi (the sound rose up to the brahma world)
    -   with the meaning **“since / from the time”**
        -   yato’haṃ bhagini ariyāya jātiyā jāto (sister, from the time i was born into the noble lineage)
        -   yato paṭṭhāya (from the time onward)
    -   **that which is protected / one against whom protection is made**
        -   kāke rakkhanti taṇḍulā (they protect the rice from crows)
            -   taṇḍulā is protected
            -   (*:q: kāke is dutiyā?*)
        -   rājato vā corato vā ārakkhaṃ gaṇhantu (let them have protection from the king or from thieves)
    -   **hiding from someone** 
        -   upajjhāyā antaradhāyati sisso (the pupil disappears (hides) from the preceptor) 5
            -   note: also with chaṭṭhī: antaradhāyissāmi samaṇassa gotamassa (i shall hide from the ascetic gotama)
    -   (distance)
        -   **distance / direction** from a place 
            -   place from which something is distant
                -   gāmato dūre (far from the village)
                -   ārakā te imasmā dhammavinayā (they are far from this sāsana)
            -   distance itself
                -   dūrato’va namassanti (they pay homage from afar)
            -   place from where direction is given
                -   ito sā purimā disā (from here, that is the eastern direction)
                -   uddhaṃ pādatalā adho kesamatthakā (upward from the soles of the feet, downward from the top of the head)
        -   **distance / time** from a point 
            -   distance 
                -   rājagahato pañcacattālīsa-yojanamatthake sāvatthī (sāvatthī is forty-five yojanas from rājagaha)
            -   time 
                -   ito ekanavutikappamatthake vipassī bhagavā loke udapādi (91 aeons before this, the blessed one vipassī arose in the world)
        -   **at such a distance (time / place) from here**
            -   ito pakkhasmā migaṃ vijjhati (a fortnight from now, he shoots a deer)
            -   ito kosā kuñjaraṃ vijjhati (a kosa from here, he shoots an elephant)
            -   (*:q: clarify*)
        -   **measurements** (length, width, etc.) 
            -   āyāmato ca vitthārato ca yojanaṃ (a yojana in length and in breadth)
            -   dīghaso navavidatthiyo (9 spans in length)
                -   note: after dīgha and ora, -smā > so: dīghaso (in length), oraso (from this side)
                -   note: also with tatiyā: vitthārena, ubbedhena
    -   **comparison**
        -   sīlameva sutā seyyo (morality is greater than learning)
        -   mayā sutā assutameva bahutaraṃ (what i have not heard is greater than what i have heard)
        -   attadanto tato varaṃ (one who has tamed himself is superior to that)
        -   note: also with chaṭṭhī: aggohamasmi lokassa (i am superior to the world)
    -   **virati**
        -   pāṇātipātā veramaṇī (abstaining from killing)
        -   ārati virati pāpā (refraining from evil)
    -   **suddha**: that which one is pure from
        -   lobhanīyehi dhammehi suddho (pure from greedy states)
    -   **mutti**: that which one is free from
        -   parimutto dukkhāsmā (freed from suffering)
        -   jātijarāmaraṇamhā parimuccissāmi (i shall be freed from birth, old age, and death)
    -   **viveka**: that which one is seluded from
        -   vivicceva kāmehi (secluded from sensual pleasures)
    -   **pubba / tato / etc** meaning “before” and “after”: that before / after which something happened
        -   pubbeva sambodhā (even before enlightenment)
        -   tato paraṃ (after that)
    -   **bound by debt**: that due to which someone is indebted
        -   satasmā baddho (bound by a hundred (coins))
    -   **thoka** meaning "by a little; narrowly; barely"
        -   thokā muccati (he is freed from a little; he narrowly escapes; he barely escapes)
    -   with **bhiyyo, bhiyyoso** meaning “more than”
        -   khantiyā bhiyyo na vijjati (there is nothing greater than patience)
        -   bhiyyoso mattāya attamano ahosi (he was happy beyond measure; excessively)

### kāraṇa- / hetu- attha


**karoti attano phalanti kāraṇaṃ**: that which produces its own result is called a cause

-   note:
    -   this definition encompasses the janakahetu
    -   kcyn: non-janakahetus are treated separately
    -   all vibhattis can express cause
    -   not to be confused with karaṇa (instrument)
-   e.g.:
    -   avijjāpaccayā saṅkhārā (volitional formations arise because of ignorance)
    -   avijjānirodhā saṅkhāranirodho (with the cessation of ignorance, formations cease)
    -   kasmā hetunā? (for what reason?)



-   3 hetus

    
    
    -   **janaka hetu** (generative cause): that which produces something
        -   avijjāpaccayā saṅkhārā
        -   (*:q: clarify the sense of production here*)
    -   **ñāpaka hetu** (indicative cause): that which indicates or informs something
        -   hutvā abhāvato’niccā, udayabbayapīḷanā
            dukkhā avasavattittā, anattāti tilakkhaṇaṃ
            (because they arise and cease, they are impermanent; because they are oppressed by arising and ceasing, they are suffering; because they are not under control, they are non-self)
    -   **guṇa hetu** (qualitative cause): an internal quality as cause 
        -   sīlato naṃ pasaṃsanti (they praise him because of his virtue)
            -   bhante: seems janaka because his virtue generates praise
        -   avijjānirodhā saṅkhāranirodho (with the cessation of ignorance, volitional formations cease)
            -   both can refer to Nibbāna; one does not produce the next; each is a quality of Nibbāna; thus guṇa
