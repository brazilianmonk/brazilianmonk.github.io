# Samāsakaṇḍa
## Avyayībhāva / Abyayībhāva Samāsa


<a id="org208baf2"></a>

### abyayībhāva &#x2013; definition, 2 meanings



-   **upasagga-nipāta-pubbako abyayībhāvo** (kcyn): the first member is either a prefix or an indeclinable
    -   pubbako can also mean more prominent (next meaning)
-   **pubbapadatthappadhāno abyayībhāvo** (rpsd): the meaning of the first member is predominant (padhāno)
    -   it's the only samāsa with this characteristic
        -   (*:q: the first one or the second ot both?*)
    -   note: adjectives are not as prominent as the nouns they modify
        -   dussīla: not abyayībhāva (du as bad (adj))
        -   dubbhikkhā: abyayībhāva (du as scarcity)
-   2 meanings
    -   **abyayānaṃ atthaṃ vibhāvetīti abyayībhāvo**: it expresses the meaning of indeclinables
    -   **anabyayaṃ abyayaṃ bhavatīti abyayībhāvo**: the non-indeclinable compound becomes indeclinable
        -   "becoming indeclinable": appearing in the same form across different genders, cases, and numbers, like an indeclinable word
            -   like upavadhu
-   note
    -   also called adverbial compound
-   e.g.:nagarassa samīpaṃ upanagaraṃ (vicinity of the city)
    -   upa ((vicinity, nearness &#x2013; upasagga) + nagara
        -   main meaning is that of the upasagga




<a id="org491a92e"></a>

<hr style="width: 40%; margin: 1rem auto; border: 1px solid #AD6100;">
### abyaya &#x2013; definition


**abyayamiti upasagga-nipātānaṃ saññā, liṅga-vacana-bhedepi byayarahitatta** (rpsd): abyaya / avyaya is the designation given to prefixes and particles for not undergoing change according to gender or number

-   byaya: change
-   abyaya: that which has no change, indeclinable
    -   nrtdpn: natthi byayo etassāti abyayo




<a id="orgb58f9f9"></a>

<hr style="width: 40%; margin: 1rem auto; border: 1px solid #AD6100;">
### 2 viggahas acc. to the words it uses



-   **assapadaviggaha**: uses a substitute word
    -   sapada: own words
    -   a + sapada: not its own words
    -   nagarassa samīpaṃ upanagaraṃ
        -   nagarassa: chaṭṭhīvibhatti
        -   upa (nearness): substituted by another word such as samīpa since upa cannot be used as an independent word
    -   note:
        -   sīho viya sīho: upamā taddhita viggaha
            -   sīha: normally, lion, but here sīha + a (taddhita suffix of comparison) means one who is like a lion
-   **sapadaviggaha**: uses the original words
    -   nīlaṃ uppalaṃ nīluppalaṃ
    -   mahanto puriso mahāpuriso
    -   sāriputto ca moggallāno ca sāriputtamoggallānā




<a id="org345f7ef"></a>

<hr style="width: 40%; margin: 1rem auto; border: 1px solid #AD6100;">
### substitutes for upasaggas



-   **nī**: abhāva
-   **anu**
    -   pacchā (after)
    -   yogga (suitability)
    -   anu (in vicchā usage)
    -   anupubba (sequence, order)
-   **adhi-**: adhikicca (based on / concerning)
-   **pati**
    -   pati (in vicchā usage)
    -   patiloma (in opposition, against)
-   **su-**: samiddhi (prosperity)
-   **ā-**: (mariyādā, abhividhi: limit or extent)
-   **upa**: samīpa
-   note: abhi, pati, pari, and anu when kammappavacanīya: can be used independently (sapadaviggaha)
-   (*:q: are the other remaining upasaggas used (pa, para, etc.)?*)

<hr style="width: 40%; margin: 1rem auto; border: 1px solid #AD6100;">
### upasaggas that do not require substitute


**abhi, pari, pati, anu** when kammappavacanīya



<a id="orgbef2788"></a>

<hr style="width: 40%; margin: 1rem auto; border: 1px solid #AD6100;">
### from viggaha to samāsa

 

-   viggaha: naragassa samīpaṁ
-   case endings are elided
    -   nagarassa -> nagara
    -   samīpaṁ -> upa
        -   substitute word is reverted to the respective abyaya
-   other occasional modifications
    -   in neuter: long ending vowels (gaṅgā) are shortened (gaṅga)
    -   upago -> upagu (o is shortened to u)
    -   non-a-kāranta abyayībhāvas: all case endings are dropped entirely
        -   upavadhu
-   compounding of the terms in proper order: upanagara
    -   abyaya must come first in an avyayībhāvasamāsa
    -   nt. nom.: the final compound is declined in neuter gender, nominative case, singular or plural

      


<a id="org042206c"></a>

<hr style="width: 40%; margin: 1rem auto; border: 1px solid #AD6100;">
### abyayībhāva's gender & declension



-   gender: mostly neuter
-   declension: as citta but with an optional substitution of -aṃ in all cases
    -   some say: always substituted
    -   not in pañcamīvibhatti
        -   nrtdpn: rare but found

### 2 types acc. to first word



-   **upasaggapubbaka abyayībhāva**: first word is an upasagga
-   **nipātapubbaka abyayībhāva**: first word is a nipāta




<a id="org2246d60"></a>

<hr style="width: 40%; margin: 1rem auto; border: 1px solid #AD6100;">
### upasaggapubbaka examples



-   **upasaggapubbaka abyayībhāva**
    -   **upa-**: samīpa (nearness)
        -   gaṅgāya samīpaṃ upagaṅgaṃ (near the Ganges) 
            -   upa + gaṅgā: upagaṅga
                -   declined like upanagaraṃ
                -   in a neuter noun, long ending vowel (gaṅgā) -> short (gaṅga)
                    -   this occurs in many types of samāsa
        -   vadhuyā samīpaṃ upavadhu (near the woman / daughter-in-law) 
            -   non-a-ending abyayībhāva compounds: all case endings are dropped entirely
        -   note: upago -> upagu (o is shortened to u)
    -   **ni-**: abhāva (absence)
        -   darathānaṃ abhāvo niddarathaṃ (absence of burning; absence of worries)
        -   makasānaṃ abhāvo nimmakasaṃ (absence of mosquitoes)
    -   **anu-**
        -   pacchā (after) 
            -   rathassa pacchā anurathaṃ (after the chariot)
            -   vātassa pacchā anuvātaṃ (after the wind)
        -   yogga (suitability) 
            -   rūpassa yoggaṃ anurūpaṃ (suitable to the nature, appropriate)
        -   anu with vicchā (sapada)
            -   addhamāsaṃ addhamāsaṃ anu anvaddhamāsaṃ (every half a month; at intervals of half a month)
        -   anupubba: sequence / order
            -   jeṭṭhānaṃ anupubbo anujeṭṭhaṁ (order of seniority)
    -   **pati-**
        -   pati with vicchā (sapada)
            -   attānaṃ attānaṃ pati paccattaṃ (within each individual)
                -   dutiyāvibhatti in locative meaning (attani)
        -   paṭiloma (opposition)
            -   sotassa paṭilomaṁ paṭisotaṁ (against the current)
            -   pathassa paṭilomaṁ paṭipathaṁ (against the path)
            -   vātassa paṭilomaṁ paṭivātaṁ (against the wind)
    -   **adhi-**: adhikicca (based on / concerning)
        -   attanaṃ adhikicca pavattaṃ ajjhattaṁ (things that exist based on oneself; internal phenomena)
    -   **ā-**: (mariyādā, abhividhi: limit or extent)
        -   ā + pāṇakoṭiyā āpāṇakoṭikaṃ (up to the end of life)
            -   -ka is added
        -   ā + kumārehi yaso ākumāraṁ (fame extending even to young children)
    -   **su-**: samiddhi (prosperity)
        -   bhikkhāya samiddhi subhikkhaṁ (abundance of alms)




<a id="orgc4fd8ac"></a>

<hr style="width: 40%; margin: 1rem auto; border: 1px solid #AD6100;">
### nipātapubbaka examples



-   **nipātapubbaka avyayībhāva**
    -   pāsādassa anto antopāsādaṁ (inside the palace)
    -   vassassa anto antovassaṁ (within the rainy season)
    -   nagarassa bahi bahinagaraṁ (outside the city)
    -   pāsādassa upari uparipāsādaṁ (above the palace)
    -   mañcassa heṭṭhā heṭṭhāmañcaṁ (under the bed)
    -   bhattassa pure purebhattaṁ (before the meal)
    -   bhattassa pacchā pacchābhattaṁ (after the meal)
    -   gaṅgāya oraṁ oragaṅgaṁ (this side of the ganges; this shore of the ganges)
        -   niggahīta is dropped
        -   also oregaṅgaṁ by vowel substitution
    -   kuṭṭassa tiro tirokuṭṭaṁ (behind the wall)
    -   yattakaṃ / yāva jīvo yāvajīvaṁ (for as long as life lasts)
    -   yattakaṃ / yāva / yāvatā āyu yāvatāyukaṁ (for as long as the life span lasts)
        -   t is inserted (unless yāvatā)
        -   -ka is inserted to the samāsa
    -   sattiyā anurūpaṃ / yathā satti yathāsatti (according to ability)
    -   kamassa anurūpaṃ / yathā kamo yathākkamaṁ (according to order)
    -   vuḍḍhānaṃ paṭipāṭi / yathā vuḍḍhā yathāvuḍḍhaṁ (according to seniority, sequence of seniors)
    -   yāva attho yāvadatthaṁ (as much as needed)
    -   saha makkhikāhi samakkhikaṁ (together with flies)
        -   bhante: seems to be bahubbīhi; if adverb, abyayibhāva
        -   saha in abyayībhāva, also changes to sa
