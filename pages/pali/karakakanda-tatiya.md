## Meanings of tatiyā vibhatti



-   **kattvattha** (agent meaning): agent of the passive voice
-   **karaṇattha** (instrumental meaning)
-   **kāraṇa- / hetu- attha** (cause meaning): hetu is the reason for the occurrance of something
-   **kriyāpavaggattha** (meaning of fast completion of an action)
-   **sahādiyogattha** (combination with the particle saha, saddhiṁ, samaṁ, nānā, vinā)
-   (lakkhaṇa)
    -   **itthambhūtalakkhaṇattha** (indicating a state or condition)
    -   **aṅgavikāralakkhaṇattha** (indicating a bodily deformity)
-   **aññavibhattiyattha**
    -   **kammattha**
        -   *tilehi* khette vapati (he sows sesame in the field) 
            -   tilehi: kamma of vapati
        -   jigucchāmi *kāyaduccaritena* (i reproach bodily misconduct)
    -   **apādānattha**
        -   sumuttā mayaṃ *tena mahāsamaṇena* (we have attained complete freedom from that great ascetic)
        -   pathavyā *ekarajjena*, saggassa *gamanena* vā, *sabbalokādhipaccena*, sotāpattiphalam varaṃ (the fruit of stream-entry is superior from (to) sole sovereignty over the earth, to going to heaven, to lordship over the whole world)
        -   cakkhu suññaṃ *attena* vā *attaniyena* vā (the eye is empty from (of) self or of what belongs to self)
    -   **ādhārattha**
        -   *tena samayena* buddho bhagavā verañjāyaṃ viharati (at that time, the buddha, who is the blessed one, was dwelling at verañjā)
        -   *puratthimena* dhataraṭṭho, *dakkhiṇena* virūḷhako (dhataraṭṭha in the east, virūḷhaka in the south)
        -   *kālena* dhammasavanaṃ (listening to the dhamma at the proper time)
        -   itiha *tena khaṇena tena muhuttena* yāva brahmalokā saddo abbhuggacchi (at that very instant, at that very moment, the sound arose up to the brahmā world)

### kattvattha &#x2013; definition


**karotīti kattā**: one who (primarily) does (performs an action) is called the agent (kattu)

-   rukkho purisena chijjate (the tree is cut by the man) 
    -   purisena: agent who cuts the tree 1
-   note: kattu is the main kāraka

**attappadhāno kiriyaṃ, yo nibbatteti kārako
appayutto payutto vā, sa kattāti pavuccati** (rpsd)
that kāraka which itself becomes principal and produces an action, whether independently or when caused by another, is called the agent
    

-   vuttakattu &#x2013; definition

    
    **vuttakattu** (expressed agent): agent expressed by the verb, in which case, the tatiyāvibhatti is not used
    

-   3 kinds of (vutta) kattus

    
    
    -   vuttakattus
        -   **suddha- / sayaṃ- kattu** (pure agent): agent who performs the action directly by himself
            -   puriso kammaṃ karoti (the man does the work)
            -   note: me: may include dhātukattu
                -   (*:q: does it include the dhātukattu?*)
        -   **hetu- / kārita- / payojja- kattu** (causative agent): agent who causes another person to perform an action 
            -   puriso dāsaṃ kammaṃ kāreti (the man makes the servant do the work)
                -   puriso: hetukattu / kāritakattu
                -   dāsaṁ: dhātukattu
        -   **kammakattu** (object-agent): agent which is actually an object treated as agent
            -   kusūlo sayameva bhijjati (the granary breaks by itself) 
                -   kusūlo: object treated as agent
                    -   the granary is actually broken by someone
                    -   it is spoken of as if it breaks by itself since it can be broken very easily
            -   ghaṭo sayameva bhijjati (the pot breaks by itself)
        -   note: all are expressed in paṭhamāvibhatti for being vuttakattus here
    -   avuttakattu
    -   note: **dhātukattu**: agent of the verbal root
    
    **hetukattāti kathito, kattuno yo payojako
    kammakattāti sukaro, kammabhūto kathīyati**
    one who causes the agent to act is called the causative agent; that which is an object but is easily acted upon is spoken of as the object-agent
    

-   paṭhamā vs tatiyā &#x2013; which is the agent?

    
    
    -   **vuttakattu**: agent is expressed by the verb, so the noun appears in paṭhamāvibhatti
        -   used in active sentences
    -   **avuttakattu**: agent is not expressed by the verb but by the noun in tatiyāvibhatti in kattvattha
        -   called hetu-, kārita- or payojaka- kattu
        -   used in causative sentences
    
    tatiyāvibhatti is the case that truly expresses the agent meaning

### karaṇattha &#x2013; definition


**karīyate anenāti karaṇaṃ**: that by means of which an action is performed is called instrument, which is expressed by the tatiyāvibhatti

cakkhunā rūpaṃ passati (he sees a form with the eye)

-   passati: action done with the eye as instrument
-   cakkhunā: instrument
-   note:
    -   this is the karaṇa kāraka (the kriyāsādhaka karaṇa in particular)
    -   not to be confused with kāraṇa (cause)



-   5 types of karaṇa

    
    
    -   **ajjhattika karaṇa** (internal instrument): bodily organs of the agent
        -   cakkhunā rūpaṃ passati (he sees a form with the eye)
        -   hatthena kammaṃ karoti (he does the work with the hand)
        -   manasā dhammaṃ vijānāti (he knows the dhamma with the mind)
    -   **bāhira karaṇa** (external instrument): external tools or implements
        -   pharasunā rukkhaṃ chindati (he cuts the tree with an axe)
        -   dāttena vīhayo lunāti (he harvests rice with a sickle)
        -   yānena gacchati (he goes by vehicle)
    -   **kriyāsādhaka karaṇa** (action-accomplishing instrument): instrument that directly accomplishes the action &#x2013; this is the karaṇa kāraka
        -   cakkhunā rūpaṃ passati (he sees a form with the eye)
    -   **visesana karaṇa** (qualifying instrument): adjectives and adverbs expressed in the instrumental case
        -   adjectives (bhinnādhikaraṇa visesana)
            -   gottena gotamo nātho (the lord is gotama by lineage)
            -   ādicco nāma gottena (ādicca by clan)
            -   sākiyo nāma jātiyā (sākiyan by race)
            -   jātiyā sattavassiko (seven years old by birth)
            -   ekūnatiṃso vayasā (twenty-nine by age)
            -   tapasā uttamo (excellent by austerity)
            -   pakatīyā abhirūpo (handsome by nature)
        -   adverbs
            -   dhammena rajjaṃ kāreti (he rules righteously)
            -   thero attanāva attānaṃ sammānati (the elder appoints himself by himself)
    -   **nānatta karaṇa** (miscellaneous instruments): instrument that is not directly connected to a verb but with various indeclinables or nouns such as **kiṃ, alaṃ, pubba, sadisa, sama, ūna, attha (need), kalaha, nipuṇa, missaka, sakhila**, etc
        -   kiṃ me ekena tiṇṇena, purisena thāmadassinā (what is the use for me of (me) crossing (the saṃsāra) alone as a man who sees one’s strength?)
            -   "me" here is catutthīvibhatti
        -   kiṃ te jaṭāhi dummedha, kiṃ te ajinasāṭiyā (fool! what is the use of matted locks to you? what is the use for deerskin garment to you?)
        -   alaṃ te idha vāsena (there is no use for you in living here)
        -   alaṃ me buddhena (i have no need of the buddha)
        -   kinnu me buddhena (what use is the buddha to me)
        -   māsena pubbo (earlier by a month)
        -   pitarā sadiso (similar to his father)
        -   mātarā samo (equal to his mother)
        -   kahāpaṇena ūno (less by one kahāpaṇa)
        -   dhanena vikalo (deficient in wealth)
        -   pāniyena attho (there is a need for drinking water)
        -   asinā kalaho (a fight with a sword, a sword-fight)
        -   vācāya nipuṇo (skilful in speech)
        -   guḷena missako (mixed with jaggery)
        -   vācāya sakhilo (gentle in speech)

### kāraṇa- / hetu- attha &#x2013; definition


**kāraṇa / hetu**: reason for an action; includes negative meaning (not a reason for the action)

-   annena vasati (he lives because of food; with the expectation of obtaining food)
-   na jaccā vasalo hoti (one is not an outcaste because of birth)
-   kammunā vasalo hoti (one becomes an outcaste because of actions)
-   dānena bhogavā (one is wealthy because of generosity)
-   kena hetunā? (for what reason?)

  

-   karaṇa vs kāraṇa

    
    
    -   **karaṇa** (instrument): directly present and connected with the action
        -   cakkhunā rūpaṃ passati
            -   cakkhu must be present at every moment of seeing
    -   **kāraṇa / hetu**: may exist before or after the action takes place
        -   annena vasati
            -   living in expectation of food occurs first, while the obtaining of food happens later

### kiriyāpavaggattha &#x2013; definition


**kriyāpavaggoti kriyāya āsuṃ-pariniṭṭhāpanaṃ**: expresses the fast completion of an action 

-   **kiriya**: action
-   **apavagga**: fast completion
    -   ekāheneva bārāṇasiṁ agamāsi
    -   jnk: even if it's slow, it can be used
        -   navahi masehi niṭṭhāpesi

### sahādiyogattha &#x2013; definition and 3 types


connected with **saha, saddhiṃ, samaṃ, nānā, vinā**

3 types

1.  **sahayoga** (association with saha)
    -   pitā puttena saha āgacchati (the father comes together with the son)
    -   3 types
        -   **dabba-samavāya** (joint connection with a substance)
            -   pitā puttena saha dhanavā (the father is wealthy together with the son)
        -   **guṇa-samavāya** (joint possession of a quality) 
            -   pitā puttena saha thūlo (the father is fat together with the son)
        -   **kriyā-samavāya** (joint performance of an action)
            -   pitā puttena saha āgacchati (the father comes together with the son)
2.  **sahādi-yoga** (association with other related particles): with saddhiṃ / samaṃ / nānā / vinā 
    -   nisīdi bhagavā saddhiṃ *bhikkhusaṅghena* (the blessed one sat together with the saṅgha of monks)
    -   *sahassena* samaṃ mitā (they were compared with a thousand)
    -   *sabbehi piyehi manāpehi* nānābhāvo vinābhāvo (separation from all that is dear and pleasing)
        -   in ekavacana, such as "piyena vinā", it becomes clear that this is not pañcamī
3.  **sahattha** (in the implicit meaning of association): the meaning "together with" is implicit or expressed through another word kusumena
    -   implicit
        -   bhagavā rājagahaṃ piṇḍāya pāvisi *aññatarena bhikkhunā* (the Blessed One entered Rājagaha for alms with a certain monk)
    -   expressed by another word
        -   dukkho *bālehi* saṃvāso (living together with fools is painful)
        -   mā *piyehi* samāgañchi, *appiyehi* kudācanaṃ (do not associate with the dear; never associate with the unpleasant) 
            -   note: -saṃ in saṃvāso and samāgañchi conveys the saha-meaning



-   sahādi &#x2013; before or after the term

    
    
    -   tatiyāvibhatti: normally, after the term (puttena saha)
        -   st, before: saddhiṁ bhikkhusaṅghena
    -   pañcamīvibhatti: normally, before the term (saha parinibbānā)
    -   (*:q: add other cases*)

### itthambhūtalakkhaṇattha &#x2013; definition


**summary**: tatiyāvibhatti words that indicate (lakkhaṇa) the state (itthaṁ) reached (bhūta) by someone or something

-   **itthaṁ kiñci pakāraṁ. ayaṃ pakāro itthaṃ** (itthaṃ means whatever manner / state)
    -   itthaṁ (nipāta): by a specific state, manner, condition
    -   pakāra (pa + √kar + \*a)
        -   masc. method; manner; way
        -   masc. arrangement; construction; way something is put together
-   **bhūto patto** (one who has become, reached (the state, manner, condition))
    -   bhūta: become, reached
-   **itthaṃ bhūto itthambhūto** (one who has reached a particular state is called itthambhūta)
-   **itthambhūtassa lakkhaṇaṃ itthambhūta-lakkhaṇaṃ**: the expression that marks or indicates that particular state reached by someone is called itthambhūta-lakkhaṇa, expressed by the tatiyāvibhatti
    -   lakkhaṇa: words that characterize the state

-   ūnapañcabandhanena pattena aññaṃ navaṃ pattaṃ cetāpeyya (if one requests another new bowl with a bowl with fewer than five bindings)
-   sā bhinnena sīsena paggharantena lohitena paṭivīsake ujjhāpesi (she caused the neighbours to reproach them, being with a broken head and flowing blood) 
    -   itthaṁ: the state of having a cracked skull and losing blood
    -   itthambhūta: the servant who has come to this state
    -   itthambhūtalakkhaṇa: the words "bhinnena sīsena paggharantenena lohitena", which indicate that state
-   assupuṇṇehi nettehi pitaraṃ so udikkhati (he looks at his father with tear-filled eyes)
-   tidaṇḍakena paribbājakamaddakkhi (he saw an ascetic with (carrying) three sticks bound together)
-   na ukkhittakāya antaraghare gamissāmi (i will not enter the village with the robe lifted up)
-   pāsādikena abhikkantena paṭikkantena piṇḍāya pavisati (he enters for alms with pleasing movements of going and returning)

### aṅgavikāralakkhaṇattha &#x2013; definition


words that describe someone by making reference to a defective body part, expressed in the tatiyāvibhatti

-   **aṅgavikāra**: a deformed body part, organ
-   **lakkhaṇa**: words that indicate such aṅgavikāra
    -   akkhiṇā kāṇo
    -   hatthena khañjo
    -   sotena badhiro
