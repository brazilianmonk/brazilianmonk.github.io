### [Brazilian Monk](https://youtube.com/@brazilian-monk?feature=shared)

This is a personal channel dedicated to Dhamma content such as:
- **Abhidhamma Lectures**: Abhidhamma lessons I gave in IIT (theravado.com) covering the first 5 chapters of the Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha
- **Recitations**: Recitations of Dhamma texts.
- **Dhamma talks**: Talks in English, Spanish, or Portuguese. 
- **Others**: glances into the monk life such as the making of robes, baking of bowls, monastery life, Saṅgha gatherings, etc.
