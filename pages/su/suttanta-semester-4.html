      • Brahmadatta, Bārāṇasī, bodhisatta seṭṭhi, 6 dānasālās
        • King Brahmadatta in Bārāṇasī, Bodhisatta in a rich family, at 16 mastered all arts and skills, father passes, he becomes seṭṭhi, six dānasālas: 4 at the four city gates, one in the middle of the city and one near his house gate; virtue and uposatha.
      • paccekabuddha, piṇḍapāta, māra, 2 intentions, pit,
        • Paccekabuddha 7 days in nirodha, approaches bdst while the latter was being offered various tasty foods, bdst stood up, asked one of his servants to bring the Paccekabuddha’s bowl; Māra came and created a pit of embers of (burnt) acacia-wood which is eighty cubits deep; embers were burning as in the hell of Avīci; Māra had two evil intentions: one that the pckbdh should die and that the bodhisatta should not fulfill his donation and therefore his pāramīs;
      • challenge, verse, walking to the pit
        • the bodhisatta questions Māra and boldly challenges him by walking towards the pit to make his donation saying: Kāmaṃ patāmi nirayaṃ, uddhaṃpādo avaṃsiro, Nānariyaṃ karissāmi, handa piṇḍaṃ paṭiggahāti. Willingly I plunge into hell, with feets up headfirst, I shan't commit what is ignoble, come now! do accept this alms;
      • offering, pck flies away to Himālaya, māra leaves disappointed, dhammatalk on lotus
        • large lotuses hold him and their golden pollen glistens around him, he makes the donation, the paccekabuddha flies away to the himālaya followed by his bowl, Māra returns disappointed, the bodhisatta gives a dhammatalk standing on the lotus on the benefits of dāna and sīla

        • which beings get immediate benefits when rejoicing in merits dedicated to them? #[[suttanta exam ]]
          • specific:
            • paradattūpajīvīpeta, vemānikapetas, vinipātika-asuras and even some devas can accrue results in the same life by rejoicing in the sharing of merits specifically for them
