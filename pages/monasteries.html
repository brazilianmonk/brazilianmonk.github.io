Theravāda Buddhist monasteries known to maintain the Theravāda Vinaya and traditional Tipiṭaka heritage meeting the following criteria:
- Monks follow the Buddha's Vinaya rules in full (no money, no dinner, recite the pātimokkha, etc.).
- Accept the entire Pāḷi Tipiṭaka and its traditional long-standing explanations.
- Teach & practice accordingly.
Use the filters below to narrow the list by country, language, or area of focus. Click any column header to sort.
