          • Vattaṃ na paripūrento na sīlaṃ paripūrati, asuddhasīlo duppañño cittekaggaṃ na vindati. Vikkhittacitto nekaggo saddhammaṃ na ca passati apassamāno saddhammaṃ dukkhā na parimuñcati. (vnyvncy) One who does not fulfill their duties (vatta) he does not complete their moral conduct (sīla). One, without having pure sīla, cannot attain concentration. A distracted and unsettled mind does not see the Dhamma. Without seeing the Dhamma, one cannot be freed from suffering.
