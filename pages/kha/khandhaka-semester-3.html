        • “Tayo pattassa vaṇṇā (size) ukkaṭṭho patto majjhimo omako patto. Ukkaṭṭho nāma patto aḍḍhāḷhakodanaṃ gaṇhāti catubhāgaṃ (1/4) khādanaṃ tadupiyañca vyañjanaṃ. Majjhimo nāma patto nāḷikodanaṃ gaṇhāti catubhāgaṃ khādanaṃ tadupiyañca vyañjanaṃ. Omako nāma patto patthodanaṃ gaṇhāti catubhāgaṃ khādanaṃ tadupiyañca vyañjanaṃ.
          • t can be considered that the small bowl can hold food sufficient for consumption by a healthy, generally well built Bhikkhu and the medium bowl can hold food sufficient for two and the large bowl can hold enough food for consumption by four. Some of the bowls used at present can be considered as large and medium.
          • 3 / 9 bowl sizes (vaṇṇā) and their measures
            • ukkaṭṭha: 1/2 āḷhaka of raw rice ⇒ cook it ⇒ + 1/4 of the volume of cooked rice of green gram (sūpa), +1/4 curry = 1 1/2 total volume
              • ukkaṭṭhukkaṭṭha (not allowed as patta): this volume does not reach the rim
              • ukkaṭṭha: it reaches the rim - this should cater for four healthy well-built bhikkhus
              • ukkaṭṭhomaka: it goes past the rim
            • majjhima: 1 nāḷika + 1/4 + 1/4
              • majjhimukkaṭṭha: this volume does not reach the rim
              • majjhima: it reaches the rim - this should cater for two healthy well-built bhikkhus
              • majjhimomaka: it goes past the rim
            • omaka: 1 pattha + 1/4 + 1/4
              • omakukkaṭṭha: this volume does not reach the rim
              • omaka: it reaches the rim - this should cater for a single healthy well-built bhikkhu
              • omakomaka (not allowed as patta): it goes past the rim

      • Atha pana titthiyehi sahagacchanti, te ca nesaṃ kusacīravākacīraphalakacīrāni denti, tānipi laddhiṃ aggahetvā nivāsetuṃ vaṭṭanti, nivāsetvāpi laddhi na gahetabbā. (vinaya aṭṭhakathā - nissaggiyakaṇḍaṃ)
        • Likewise, if the monk who has been robbed of his robes meets the other ascetics and wrong believers (Thitthiya) and they offer their wearings, this monk is allowed to take and wear the kusachīra of those ascetics without taking their believes. Even using the ascetic’s wearings, one should not take their wrong believes.
