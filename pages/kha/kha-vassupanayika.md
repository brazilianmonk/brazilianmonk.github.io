## Main Allowance


<a id="org608d60c"></a>

### Background and Allowance



-   monks travelled during the vassa
-   people criticized them for
    -   hemantampi gimhampi vassampi cārikaṃ carissanti: travelling on all 3 seasons
    -   haritāni tiṇāni sammaddantā: trampling crops and grass
    -   ekindriyaṃ jīvaṃ viheṭhentā: harming one-facultied life (plants thought of as living)
    -   bahū khuddake pāṇe saṅghātaṃ āpādentā: harming many small animals
-   people compared them with other ascetics and birds, who did not do so
-   allowance: **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, vassaṃ upagantuṃ**




<a id="orgd88f56f"></a>

### Purpose



-   **criticism**: avoid public criticism, protect reputation of the Saṅgha
-   **harm**: prevent harm to living beings
-   **discipline**: promote disciplined monastic living through stability and continuous practice
-   **strengthen relations** with lay supporters, who could regularly associate monks




<a id="org840c8c3"></a>

### Period of Observance



-   Background: monks inquired about the period of entrance
-   **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, vassāne vassaṃ upagantuṃ**: allowed to observe it during the rainy season




<a id="orgbae02b3"></a>

### 2 Vassūpanāyikā



-   **Vassāvāsa**:
    -   time for the rains residence
    -   the rains residence itself
-   **Vassūpanāyika / Vassūpagamana**: the entering of the rains residence by means of determination
-   Background: monks inquired the number of entrances
-   **Dvemā, bhikkhave, vassūpanāyikā – purimikā, pacchimikā.**: 2 entrances to the rains-residence: the early and the late entry
    -   **Aparajjugatāya āsāḷhiyā purimikā upagantabbā**: the early entry is to be undertaken on the day after Āsāḷhī puṇṇamā (normally in July)
        -   this is the standard observance
        -   aparajjugatā: when the next day has arrived, after the passing of a day, on the pāṭipadika
    -   **māsagatāya āsāḷhiyā pacchimikā upagantabbā**: the late entry is to be undertaken the day after a month has passed since Āsāḷhī Puṇṇamā (usually in August)
        -   māsagatāya: when a month has passed (after the completion of a month) on the pāṭipadika
        -   allowed when unable to enter the first for valid reasons
        -   entering the second without valid reason: **dkṭ**
    -   **Imā kho, bhikkhave, dve vassūpanāyikā**

   


<a id="org123ab06"></a>

#### Valid Reasons for Pacchimikā Vassūpanāyikā


khds-p-ṭ: **chinnavassonupāgato**

-   **Chinnavasso**: one who has broken the purimikā within the first month
-   **Anupagato**: one who has not entered the purimikā
    -   vnv: antarāyena vassaṁ nopagato: dangers prevent one from entering the purimikā: **anāpatti**
        -   vnv-ṭ: any, including rājantarāya, etc.
    -   khds: jānaṁ anupagacchato: knowingly not entering: **dkṭ**
        -   even if one remains sitting in the monastery
    -   note: includes any case in which one has not entered

khds:
**Nopetukāmo āvāsaṃ, tadahūtikkameyya vā,
bhaveyya dukkaṭāpatti, jānaṃ vānupagacchato**
should one who does not intend to enter let that day pass,
or knowingly not entering, there is a dukkaṭa offense

khds-p-ṭ:
**Dutiyaṃ upagaccheyya, chinnavassonupāgato,
na pakkameyya temāsaṃ, avasitvāna cārikaṃ.**
he should enter the second, one whose rains is broken or unentered; 
he should not depart wandering about without having stayed for the three months



<a id="org6d878b6"></a>

#### Consequences of not Entering


khds: **bhaveyya dukkaṭāpatti**: dkṭ offense
khds-p-ṭ: **dutiyaṃ upagaccheyya**: should enter the second




<a id="orge8d4a37"></a>

### Procedure for Entering


V-a:

-   **āsāḷhīpuṇṇamāya anantare pāṭipadadivase**: day after āsāḷha full moon
    -   if entering the second residence, that respective day
-   **inside the vihāra / vajādi**
-   **vihāraṃ paṭijaggitvā**: prepare and clean the monastery
-   **pānīyaṃ paribhojanīyaṃ upaṭṭhapetvā**: arrange drinking and washing water
-   **sabbaṃ cetiyavandanādisāmīcikammaṃ niṭṭhāpetvā**: complete respective duties, such as paying homage at the cetiya
-   **enter by ālaya or vacībheda**




<a id="orga69e207"></a>

#### Ālaya & Vacībheda & When to use them



-   **Ālaya / Ābhoga**: the arising of the thought (cittuppādamattaṃ): "idha vassaṃ vasissāmi"
    -   **ālayapariggāha**: the establishment of intention (khds-p-ṭ)
-   **Vacībheda**: vācaṃ nicchāretvā &#x2013; breaking into speech; saying out loud
    -   in vihāra: "imasmiṁ vihāre imaṁ temāsaṁ vassaṁ upemi"
    -   in vajādi with kavāṭasenāsana: "idha vassaṁ upemi"
    -   repetitions:
        -   sakiṃ vā dvattikkhattuṃ vā vācaṃ nicchāretvā
        -   “Idha vassaṃ upemī”ti tikkhattuṃ vattabbaṃ
-   Note: this is the proper procedure

When to use them

-   in a senāsana: **vacībheda** should be made
    -   **imasmiṃ vihāre imaṃ temāsaṃ vassaṃ upemi**
        -   some also say **idha vassaṁ upemi**
    -   khds-p-ṭ: if ālaya is present, but vacībheda is forgotten: **no chinnavassa; can perform Pavāraṇā**
-   in vajādi: if in a 
    -   **vaja** (a dwelling place of cowherds)
    -   **sattha** (caravan)
    -   **nāvā** (boat)
    -   with kavāṭabandhasenāsana (lockable dwelling): **vacībheda** &#x2013; "idha vassaṁ upemi" (because it is not vihāra)
    -   without kavāṭabandhasenāsana: **ālaya** &#x2013; "idha vassaṁ vasissāmi"
        -   tattha senāsanaṃ alabhantassa labbhati
    -   upagacchantena ca senāsane asati “Idha vassaṃ vasissāmī”ti cittuppādamattaṃ vā kātabbaṃ, senāsane sati “Imasmiṃ vihāre imaṃ temāsaṃ vassaṃ upemī”ti ca “Idha vassaṃ upemī”ti ca vacībhedo vā kātabboti attho.”
-   without dwelling: not proper to enter the rains, leading to dkṭ

**Purimā pacchimā cāti, duve vassūpanāyikā,
ālayo vā vacībhedo, kattabbo upagacchatā** (vnv)
the observance of the rains retreat is of two kinds: the earlier and the later;
either through the mental intention or by formal verbal declaration, one who enters upon the rains residence should enter it

**Purimikā pacchimikā, duve vassūpanāyikā,
tatthālayapariggāho vacībhedo ca ediso,** (khds)
the earlier and the later are the two kinds of rains-entry; in this regard, the establishment of intention and the verbal declaration are of such a nature

**Imasmiṃ vihāre imaṃ, temāsaṃ vassaṃ upemi,
idha vassaṃ upemī'ti, cittuppādettha ālayo** (khds) 

-   edisa adj.: such; with such qualities (eta > e + ādisa) ✓
