## Abandoning Vassāvāsa due to Antarāya


**Vassaccheda but anāpatti; no pavāraṇā**:

-   jīvitantarāya
-   brahmacariyantarāya
-   lack of essential requirements such as food and medicine
-   vāḷa: wild animals attack, bite, kill them
-   cora: harass, plunder, assault them
-   pisāca: possess, drain their vitality
-   aggi: destroys the monastery or the piṇḍapāta village
-   udaka: destroys the monastery or the piṇḍapāta village
-   Note: under allowances beginning with “because of fear of wild beasts…” to schism:
    -   no offence is incurred by breaking the rains residence
        no permission to perform the pavāraṇā in such circumstances

   


<a id="org95ca593"></a>

### If Villagers Leave



-   Piṇḍapāta village was taken over by robbers
-   Villagers moved elsewhere
    -   Buddha:
        -   **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, yena gāmo tena gantuṃ**: monks, I allow you to go to the place where the village (the villagers) is situated
-   Villagers divided into two groups
    -   **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, yena bahutarā tena gantuṃ**: monks, I allow you to go to the side where the greater number of villagers (majority) reside
    -   if the bigger group have no faith of confidence (assaddhā appasannā)
        -   **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, yena saddhā pasannā tena gantuṃ**
-   If they moved to a nearby place: go there for alms round and return
-   If they moved to a distant place: go there and return within the 7-day period
    -   if unable to return: reside there in a suitable place (sabhāgaṭṭhāna)
    -   Srtd: iminā vassacchedakāraṇe sati sattāhakaraṇīyena gantuṃ vaṭṭatīti dīpeti
    -   when residing in another place in this manner
        -   if donors continue to offer the monks things such as sālākabhattas as before: inform them we are not residing there anymore 
            -   if donors say the offering is not to the vihāra / pāsāda but to you; reside wherever you wish and partake of it there: monks may accept and consume it according to their preference
            -   if donors say to take it to the new dwelling place and consume it according to seniority: monks should do so
-   After Pavāraṇā
    -   if donors offer vassāvāsika offering:
        -   monks who returned every 7 days and did not break the vassa should accept it
        -   monks who didn't do so: inform the vassa was broken
    -   if donors offer to those to whom they entrusted the dwelling place:
        -   all monks may accept it



1.  Distribution of Saṅghikaparikkhāra of the Original Vassāvāsa Monastery

    
    **Cīvarādivebhaṅgiyabhaṇḍaṃ**: robes, etc. to be distributed
    
    -   Bhaṇḍa initially kept at the original rains residence and later brought to the new location to protect them: return bhaṇda to the original residence, inform and obtain the approval of the Saṅgha through apalokanakamma, and then distribute them
        -   vmtv: idha āhaṭanti vihārato bahi āgataṭṭhāne ānītaṃ
    -   Same applies to produce / income received later from fields, lands, and other properties entrusted to kappiyakārakā to provide the four requisites for the venerable monks from the produce or income obtained from these
    -   Whether the saṅghika hvebhaṅgiyabhaṇḍa are within the vihāra-sīmā or outside it
        
        -   not appropriate to inform only the monks who are staying outside the monastery boundary and distribute those requesites merely with the approval of those monks
        -   there is no obstacle for apalokanakamma
        -   bhaṇḍa should be distributed within the vihārasīmā with apalokana
        -   v-a: saṅghikañhi vebhaṅgiyabhaṇḍaṃ antovihāre vā bahisīmāya vā hotu, bahisīmāya ṭhitānaṃ apaloketvā bhājetuṃ na vaṭṭati. ubhayattha ṭhitampi pana antosīmāya ṭhitānaṃ apaloketvā bhājetuṃ vaṭṭatiyeva

### Insufficient / Unsuitable Food / Medicine as Antarāya


**Vassaccheda but anāpatti**:

-   na labhanti lūkhassa vā paṇītassa vā bhojanassa yāvadatthaṃ pāripūriṃ: insufficient / unsuitable food (coarse / superior)
-   asappāyabhojana: unsuitable food for them
-   asappāyabhesajja: unsuitable medicine for them
-   lack of appropriate attendant or supporter




<a id="orgc99f44b"></a>

### Brahmacariyantarāya Protocol



-   **Vassaccheda but anāpatti**: allowed to leave to prevent the disturbance or corruption of the mind, thinkin: "lahuparivattaṃ kho cittaṃ vuttaṃ bhagavatā, siyāpi me brahmacariyassa antarāyo"
-   Brahmacariyantarāya: 
    -   **greed-inducing invitations** regarding wealth, land, cattle, servants, marriage, relationships, etc. made by itthī, purisa, rāja, cora, vesī, thullakumārī, paṇḍaka, ñāti, 
        -   vesī: prostitute/courtesan
        -   thullakumārī: unmarried mature woman
    -   **greed-inducing situations** such as finding an ownerless treasure




<a id="org4142b1f"></a>

### Leaving due to Saṅghabheda



-   Intention: preserve harmony in the Saṅgha, prevent schism, reconcile an arisen schism, prevent the heavy kamma of Saṅghabheda
-   Consequence: **vassaccheda but anāpatti**
-   Situations:
    -   to prevent a Saṅghabheda attempted by bhikkhus
        -   in his presence
            -   V: passati sambahule bhikkhū saṅghabhedāya parakkamante. Tatra ce bhikkhuno evaṃ hoti, ‘garuko kho saṅghabhedo vutto bhagavatā; mā mayi sammukhībhūte saṅgho bhijjī’ti
        -   on another monastery: he hears monks there are attempting to cause a Saṅghabheda
            -   to admonish directly his friendly monks at another monastery: schismatic monks there are his friends
                -   admonishment: “A Saṅghabheda is a serious offence. May you not find a Saṅghabheda pleasing. Do not approve of it.
            -   to admonish through his own friends: schismatics are not his friends but their friends are his friends, he goes to have his friends asmonish the schismatics
    -   to reconcile an arisen Saṅghabheda caused by bhikkhus
        -   including his friends
            -   saṅgho bhinno: here, schism is imminent or in which there has arisen a suspicion that the Saṅgha is about to become divided
                -   V-a: saṅgho bhinnoti ettha bhinne saṅghe gantvā karaṇīyaṃ natthi, yo pana ‘Bhijjissatī’ti āsaṅkito, taṃ sandhāya ‘Bhinno’ti vuttaṃ”
        -   to get his friends to admonish the schismatics
    -   to prevent a Saṅghabheda attempted by bhikkhunīs:
        -   V: idha pana, bhikkhave, vassūpagato bhikkhu suṇāti – ‘Amukasmiṃ kira āvāse sambahulā bhikkhuniyo saṅghabhedāya parakkamantī’ti
        -   if they are his friends: go there to admonish them
        -   if they are friends of the schismatics: go there to have them admonish the schismatics
    -   to reconcile an arisen Saṅghabheda caused by bhikkhunīs:
        -   V-a:
            -   this refers to when there is a suspicion that bhikkhus, encouraged/supported (anubalaṁ katvā) by bhikkhunīs, may cause a schism
            -   the bhikkhunīs cannot do it themselves &#x2013; na kho upāli bhikkhunī saṅghaṃ bhindati
                -   also applies to any non-bhikkhu
                -   Bhikkhu
                -   pakatatto
                -   samānasaṃvāsako
            -   samānasīmāyaṃ ṭhito
            -   bhikkhunīhi saṅgho bhinno: schism is imminent / suspicion arises about it
        -   if the bhikkhunīs are his friends: go there to admonish them
        -   if not: go there to have one's friends admonish them

## Anuññā: Vassūpanāyika in Vajjādi


<a id="org542a230"></a>

### Vassūpanāyika in a Vaja

1.  Background & 2 Anuññā

    
    
    -   monk wished to spend the rains at a cowherds’ settlement (vajo; gopālakānaṃ nivāsaṭṭhānaṃ)
    -   Buddha was informed and allowed: **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, vaje vassaṃ upagantuṃ**
    -   cowherds and their cattle left
    -   Buddha was informed and allowed: **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, yena vajo tena gantuṃ**: allowed to go to where the cowherds have gone together with the herd of cattle
    
    

2.  Vassaccheda or Not

    
    In general: V-a: **no vassaccheda; no āpatti; can perform pavāraṇā**: vajena saddhiṃ gatassa vassacchede anāpatti
    
    -   applies to vaja, sattha, and nāvā: vaje satthe nāvāyanti tīsu ṭhānesu natthi vassacchede āpatti, pavāretuñca labhati
        -   note: here, not nimittattha sattamī
    
    In detail:
    
    -   no vassaccheda or āpatti and can do pavāraṇā since he merely leaves the physical dwelling
        -   no vassaccheda
            -   Khu-si-abhi-ṭ: vassacchede: here means departing without the intention of returning because:
                -   of the aforesaid allowance
                -   sattha and nāvā are, by their very nature, things that continually move from place to place
                -   notes:
                    -   vaja is given an allowance since it is not naturally moving; sattha and nava do not need an allowance since they naturally move
                    -   vassacchedeti ca ‘anujānāmi, bhikkhave, yena vajo tena gantu’nti vuttattā, satthassa nāvāya ca gamanasabhāveneva ṭhitattā ca vassūpagataṭṭhāne avasitvā aññattha gamanamattaṃ sandhāya vuttanti veditabbaṃ
            -   Vjrb-ṭ: although one leaves, there is no vassaccheda: vajena saddhiṃ gatassa vassacchede anāpattīti vassacchedo na hotīti kira adhippāyo
        -   anāpatti because the residence is undertaken with the cowherds based on this allowance
            -   Srtd-ṭ: tehi saddhiṃ gacchantasseva natthi āpatti
            -   usually, anapekkhagamana is āpatti: yo pakkameyyāti ettha anapekkhagamanena vā aññattha aruṇaṃ uṭṭhāpanena vā āpatti veditabbā
    -   leaving due to āpadā: **vassaccheda but anāpatti**
        -   idha pana, bhikkhave, vassūpagatā bhikkhū vāḷehi ubbāḷhā honti. gaṇhantipi paripātentipi. eseva antarāyoti pakkamitabbaṃ. anāpatti vassacchedassa.
    -   Srt-ṭ: if he leaves them and travels alone: **vassaccheda, dkṭ, no pavāraṇā**
        -   viyujjitvā gamane pana āpattiyeva, pavāretuñca na labhati
    
    Summary:
    
    -   folowing cowherds, ship, caravan: no vassaccheda or āpatti; can do pavāraṇā
    -   if one abandons them: vassaccheda, āpatti, no pavāraṇā
    
    


<a id="org483aa30"></a>

### Vassūpanāyika in a Sattha

1.  Background & Anuññā

    
    
    -   A monk wished to spend the rains travelling with a sattha (merchant caravan)
    -   Buddha was reported and allowed it: **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, satthe vassaṃ upagantuṃ**
    
    

2.  Protocol on Observance Day & Pavāraṇā Day

    
    
    -   **On the vassa entering day**:
        -   inform caravan devotees that it would be suitable to have a small hut (kuṭikā) to enter vassa
            -   if they build one (within the caravan): enter it there with vacībheda "Idha vassaṃ upemi" 3x
                -   Srtd: not “Imasmiṃ vihāre&#x2026;" because it is not a vihāra
            -   if they don't build one:
                -   enter under a stationary cart with a covering, resembling a small hall (sālāsaṅkhepena ṭhitasakaṭassa heṭṭhā) with vacībheda
                -   Srtd-ṭ: if no suitable covered place is available: make ālaya (mental intention or determination)
                    -   ālaya: mere thought: “Idha vassaṃ vasissāmi”
                    -   vacībheda is not suitable without a covered place
                        -   V-a: satthe pana vassaṃ upagantuṃ na vaṭṭati
                            -   Vmv-ṭ: kuṭikādīnaṃ abhāvena ‘Idha vassaṃ upemī’ti evaṃ vacībhedaṃ katvā upagantuṃ na vaṭṭati
    -   **On the pavāraṇā day**:
        -   if still travelling: do it there
        -   if the destination is reached before the pavāraṇā day: leave the caravan, remain on the destination, and do pavāraṇā with the resident monks
        -   getting separated from the sattha without allowed reason: **vassaccheda, āpatti, and no pavāraṇā**
        -   if the caravan stops / breaks / disperses along the way: remain in that location and perform the pavāraṇā with the resident monks
            -   not proper to proceed onward without performing the pavāraṇā there
            -   Srtd-ṭ: vippakiratīti visuṃ visuṃ gacchati
    -   Notes:
        -   no allowance like for vaja (yena vajo tena gantuṁ) since it naturally moves
        -   no vassaccheda, no āpatti, and can do pavāraṇā because:
            -   he is afforded the opportunity to perform the pavāraṇā
            -   he separates from the merchant caravan precisely when he reaches the specific destination for which he had joined the caravan, in accordance with his original intention
            -   -a: vaje satthe nāvāyanti tīsu ṭhānesu natthi vassacchede āpatti, pavāretuñca labhati
            -   obhāsa: modern caravans vehicles: not included
                -   if stationary: senāsana, ok
                -   if not: vassa is broken
    
    


<a id="org013875f"></a>

### Vassūpanāyika in a Nāvā

1.  Background & Anuññā

    
    
    -   A monk wished to travel by ship when the vassūpanāyika was approaching
    -   Buddha was informed and allowed: **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, nāvāya vassaṃ upagantuṃ**
        -   **no vassaccheda or āpatti; can do pavāraṇā**
    
    

2.  Protocol for Senāsana & Pavāraṇā

    
    
    -   **Senāsana**
        -   search for one: V-a: nāvāyaṃ vassaṃ upagacchantenāpi kuṭiyaṃyeva upagantabbaṃ
        -   if unable: do ālaya
            -   pariyesitvā alabhantena ālayo kātabbo
    -   **Pavāraṇā**
        -   if the ship remains at sea throughout the entire three-month period: do pavāraṇā aboard
        -   if the ship reaches the destination:
            -   if the monk wishes to continue travelling onward with the ship:
                -   not allowed: seem to be vassaccheda, dkṭ, no pavāraṇā
                -   he should remain in the first village reached by the ship and do pavāraṇā with local monks
            -   notes:
                -   as with sattha
                -   if intention is just to cross the sea: get down on the first village
                -   if having a particular destination: can continue until the destination

## Paṭisedha: Vassūpanāyika Locations


Entering vassa in an unsuitable place: **dkṭ**

-   includes gāma, raṭṭha, etc
-   purpose:
    -   comfort and safety during the residence
    -   to preserve the faith and goodwill of the lay community; to avoid their displeasure and criticism

Disallowed locations:

-   **Rukkhasusira**: on a hollow tree
    -   monks did so; people criticized (comparing them to demons)
    -   **Na, bhikkhave, rukkhasusire vassaṃ upagantabbaṃ. Yo upagaccheyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**
    -   hollow:
        -   if naturally occurring: **dkṭ**
        -   if properly converted into a kuṭi (roof, walls, door): **allowed**
            -   SrtD-ṭ: sakavāṭabaddhameva yojetvā
            -   VmV-ṭ: sakavāṭadvāraṃ katvā
            -   V-a: mahantassa pana rukkhasusirassa anto padaracchadanaṃ kuṭikaṃ katvā pavisanadvāraṃ yojetvā upagantuṃ vaṭṭati
        -   if the upper part of the tree, which contains the hollow, is cut down and a proper dwelling is constructed atop the remaining hollow stump: **allowed**
            -   Vmv-ṭ: rukkhaṃ chinditvāti susiraṭṭhānassa uparibhāgaṃ chinditvā. khāṇumatthaketi susirakhāṇumatthake
            -   V-a: Rukkhaṃ chinditvā khāṇukamatthake padaracchadanaṃ kuṭikaṃ katvāpi vaṭṭatiyeva
            -   [hollow-kuti-1](file:///c:/users/Lenovo/Pictures/Screenshots/hollow-kuti-1.png)
            -   [hollow-kuti-2](file:///c:/users/Lenovo/Pictures/Screenshots/hollow-kuti-2.png)
            -   [hollow-kuti-3](file:///c:/users/Lenovo/Pictures/Screenshots/hollow-kuti-3.png)
-   **Rukkhaviṭabhī**: on the forks or branches of a tree
    -   monks did so; people criticized (comparing them to deerhunters)
    -   **Na, bhikkhave, rukkhaviṭabhiyā vassaṃ upagantabbaṃ. Yo upagaccheyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**
    -   if naturally occurring: **dkṭ**
    -   if a platform or scaffold (aṭṭaka) is constructed there and a dwelling is built: **allowed**
        -   V-a: mahāviṭape pana aṭṭakaṃ bandhitvā tattha padaracchadanaṃ kuṭikaṃ katvā upagantabbaṃ
-   **Ajjhokāsa**: in the open, in an uncovered place
    -   issues:
        -   causes hardship
        -   when it rains, one has to flee to find cover
    -   **Na, bhikkhave, ajjhokāse vassaṃ upagantabbaṃ. Yo upagaccheyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**
-   **Asenāsanika**: without a proper dwelling
    -   issues: cold and heat
    -   **Na, bhikkhave, asenāsanikena vassaṃ upagantabbaṃ. Yo upagaccheyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**
    -   V-a: yassa pañcannaṃ chadanānaṃ aññatarena channaṃ yojitadvārabandhanaṃ senāsanaṃ natthi, tena na upagantabbaṃ
        -   5 chadanas (tiṇa, paṇṇa, itthakā, silā, sudhā): general statement based on what is normally the case (yebhuyyena); not limited to these 5
            -   Srtd-ṭ: idañca yebhuyyena vuttanti veditabbaṃ
            -   e.g., under rukkhasusira: padaracchadana
    -   some: this only prohibits vacībheda vassūpagamana, not ālaya vassūpagamana
        -   refutation:
            -   rule does not make such a specific distinction
            -   Adinnādānasikkhāpada-a:
                -   vassūpanāyika only in a dwelling with covering and lockable door
                    -   if not available: obtain the labour of others and build it
                        -   if not possible: built it oneself
                -   without such dwelling: one should not undertake vassūpanāyika
                -   note: this applies even to moneyyapaṭipadā
                    -   Moneyyapaṭipadā: fulfilled by V. Nālaka in this sāsana
                        -   ukkaṭṭha: 7 months
                        -   majjhima: 7 years
                        -   mudukaṃ: 16 years
            -   Srtd-ṭ: only in 3 circumstances did the Buddha allow ālayavassūpanāyika: vaja, sattha, nāvā regardless of whether or not a dwelling was available
                -   asenāsanikassa nāvādiṃ vinā aññattha ālayo na vaṭṭatīti amhākaṃ khanti
                -   this prohibition does not apply to these 3 cases
-   **Chavakuṭikā**: mortuary chamber
    -   monks did so
    -   people criticized ("like those who burn corpses")
    -   Buddha prohibited: **Na, bhikkhave, chavakuṭikāya vassaṃ upagantabbaṃ. Yo upagaccheyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**
    -   chavakuṭikā: a chamber built for corpses, furnished with things such as a ṭaṅkitamañca
        -   ṭaṅkitamañca:
            -   elevated platform/bed on which corpses are placed
            -   made by boring holes through the middle portions of the legs of a long bed and inserting crosspieces of wood (aṭani) through those holes
            -   it is the same upside up or upside down
            -   placed in cemeteries or shrines
            -   alternatively, a stone platform made with a stone slab on top of four stones
    -   V-a: a proper senāsana (for for chava) in a susāna is allowed
-   **Chatta**: under an umbrella (a flat, parasol-like umbrella)
    -   monks did so
    -   people criticized ("like cowherds")
    -   Buddha prohibited: **Na, bhikkhave, chatte vassaṃ upagantabbaṃ. Yo upagaccheyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**
    -   if a chattakuṭikā: allowed
        -   chattakuṭikā (parasol hut): 4 posts with a large umbrella over them, the sides are enclosed, and a door is fitted
-   **Cāṭī**: in a big pot / vessel
    -   monks did so
    -   people criticized ("like heretics")
    -   **Na, bhikkhave, cāṭiyā vassaṃ upagantabbaṃ. Yo upagaccheyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**
    -   if a large kapalla: allowed
        -   kapalla: a covering/vessel like a clay pot used to construct a hut in the same manner as described in connection with the umbrella (parasol)

## Paṭisedha: Adhammakatikā During Vassāvāsa



-   Background
    -   Saṅgha at Sāvatthī made a katikā not to ordain anyone during the vassāvāsa
    -   Visākhā's grandson saught ordination and was told to wait
    -   by the end of the residence, he no longer wished to
    -   Visākhā criticized them, saying no limitation should be put to the practice of Dhamma
    -   Buddha was informed and prohibited &#x2013; **Na, bhikkhave, evarūpā katikā kātabbā – ‘Na antarāvassaṃ pabbājetabba’nti. Yo kareyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa.**
        -   reason: some may not be capable of fulfilling the agreement due to having weaker physical strength or being unable to exert themselves sufficiently




<a id="org1c5ce1e"></a>

## Evarūpā Katikā &#x2013; Type of Prohibited Katikas


Any adhammika-katikā (agreement agains Dhammavinaya)

-   e.g., 
    -   Prjk 4-a:
        -   yo imamhā āvāsā paṭhamaṃ pakkamissati taṃ mayaṃ arahāti jānissāma (whoever leaves the dwelling first, we shall know him as an arahat)
        -   of practicing dhutaṅgas / becoming arahats: imasmiṃ temāsabbhantare sabbeva āraññakā hontu, piṇḍapātikaṅgādiavasesadhutaṅgadharā vā atha vā sabbeva khīṇāsavā hontūti evamādi
        -   imaṃ temāsaṃ sabbeheva na uddisitabbaṃ, na paripucchitabbaṃ, na pabbājetabbaṃ, mūgabbataṃ gaṇhitabbaṃ, bahi sīmaṭṭhassāpi saṅghalābho dātabbo’ti evamādikaṃ pana na kātabbameva: not teaching mūla / commentaries; not ordaning others, observing silence (mūgavata), giving to a monk staying outside the sīmā should be given his share of the Saṅgha’s requisites
    -   Senāsanakkhandhaka-a:
        -   anāgatassa na dātabbā: agreeing to not give a salāka to an absent monk
            -   normally: an absent monk should not be deprived of the salāka he is entitled to when requisites are distributed; another may receive or his behalf or the salāka set aside for him
