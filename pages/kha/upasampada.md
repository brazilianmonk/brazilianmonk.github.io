<h1>
<b>Upasampadā</b>
</h1>
<br/>
<ul>
<li><h2 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;">upasampadā <b>sīla</b> (morality of higher ordination) 
            </h2><ul>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;">Why is it called <mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>adhisīla</b></mark>?→the upasampadā sīla <b>brings</b> all great and invaluable <b>mundane and supramundane benefits</b>; it is<b> the highest among all moralities</b></h3> </li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;">Why is it called <mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>pātimokkha saṁvara sīla</b></mark>?→As the one who protects it is released from suffering in apāya realms and self-reproach it is also called ‘pātimokkha saṃvara sīla'.</h3></li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;">Total number of upasampadā precepts <mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>in detail</b></mark> (adhisīla):
                    </h3><ul>
<li>It has been shown in the Visuddhimagga that the number of precepts to be protected in upasampadā sīla (morality of higher ordination) is <b>ninety-one billion eight hundred and five million and thirty-six thousand</b>.</li>
<li>Vmt-ṭ. I. 44: <i>Nava koṭisahassāni, asītisatakoṭiyo; 
paññāsasatasahassāni, chattiṃsā ca punāpare. 
Ete saṃvaravinayā, sambuddhena pakāsitā; 
peyyālamukhena niddiṭṭhā, sikkhā vinayasaṃvare’ti</i>
(91 billions 805 millions 36 thousands)
                            <ul>
<li><i>Nava koṭisahassāni: 9.000 x 10.000.000 = 90.000.000.000</i> </li>
<li><i>asītisatakoṭiyo:</i> 180 x 10.000.000 = 1.800.000.000</li>
<li><i>paññāsasatasahassāni:</i> 50 x 100.000 = 5.000.000</li>
<li><i>chattiṃsa (sahassa): 36 x 1000 = 36.000</i></li>
<li>TOTAL: 91.805.036.000</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><h2 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;">modes of upasampadā 
            </h2><ul>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;">8 types of <mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>upasampadās</b></mark><b> (+1)</b></h3>
<ul>
<li><b>ehibhikkhū</b>pasampadā</li>
<li><b>saraṇagamanū</b>pasampadā (by taking refuge) </li>
<li><b>ovādapaṭiggahaṇū</b>pasampadā (by accepting advice)</li>
<li><b>pañhabyākaraṇū</b>pasampadā (by solving questions)</li>
<li><b>garudhammapaṭiggahaṇū</b>pasampadā (by accepting 8 cardinal rules)</li>
<li><b>dūtenū</b>pasampadā (by a messenger)</li>
<li><b>aṭṭhavācikū</b>pasampadā (by 8 kammavācās)</li>
<li><b>ñatticatutthakammū</b>pasampadā (by a motion-and-three-proclamations vinaya kamma)</li>
<li>(extra) Ven. <b>Nālaka</b>'s upasampadā: he ordained by himself (an exception) 
                            <ul>
<li>1st arahant to attain parinibbāna→Ven. Nālaka </li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>(memory aid: 1st, 3, 3, 10, 3 for bhī (8, dūta, 8), 4)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;"><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>Ehibhikkhū</b></mark>pasampadā 
                    </h3><ul>
<li>only conferred to those who fulfilled 4 conditions:
                            <ul>
<li><b>males</b> </li>
<li>who had the opportunity to become <b>arahats</b> in the same life</li>
<li>and who had <b>past merit</b> (including have offered the basic requisites of a bhikkhu in the past)  </li>
<li>who have <b>requested ordination</b> </li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>How did it happen?→When the expectant of ordination requested ordination, the Tathāgata (after having <b>reflected</b> if the candidate had all conditions fulfilled) stretched the <b>right</b> <b>hand</b> and stated, “<b>Ehi</b> <b>bhikkhu</b>, svākkhāto dhammo, caratha brahmacariyaṃ sammā dukkhassa antakiriyāya”. With the completion of stating “Ehi bhikkhu” the ordination and higher ordination of the disciple took place, the <b>lay appearance</b> of the expectant of ordination disappeared, his head hair was shaved and he appeared bearing bowl and robes obtained due to his past merits.</li>
<li>Pāḷi: Ehi bhikkhu, svākkhāto dhammo, caratha brahmacariyaṃ sammā dukkhassa antakiriyāya </li>
<li>Ven. Aññākoṇḍañña thera was the first recipient of ehi bhikkhu upasampadā #<mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: red; color: inherit;"><b>important</b></mark> </li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;"><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>Saraṇagamanū</b></mark>pasampadā (by taking refuge) 
                    </h3><ul>
<li><b>Background</b>→after the 1st vassa 60 arahats were sent to spread the dhamma and those looking for ordination were brought to the Buddha. An easier means of ordination became necessary and this type of ordination was established by the Buddha. If the candidate was below 20 he would become a sāmaṇera, if above 20 a bhikkhu.</li>
<li>How was this ordination done?→It is the same procedure as for sāmaṇera ordination </li>
<li>The first recipient is unknown </li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;"><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>Ovādapaṭiggahaṇū</b></mark>pasampadā (by accepting advice)
                    </h3><ul>
<li><b>Background</b>
<ul>
<li>Where? in <b>Rājagaha, </b>under the <b>Bahuputtaka Banyan </b>tree 
Who was the recipient? a young man <b>Piphali </b>who ten became Ven. <b>Mahākassapatthera</b><b>
</b>How did it happen? he met the Buddha and expressed his disciple status saying: "Satthā me bhante bhagavā sāvakohamasmi" the Tathāgata gave the young man Piphali <b>three advices</b>. His higher ordination took place merely by the acceptance of these advices. In 7 days he attained Arahatship. </li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>3 admonishments the Buddha gave to Mahākassapa:
                            <ul>
<li>always contemplate the <b>repulsiveness</b> of the body (to avoid lust because he was good looking)</li>
<li>listen always very <b>attentively</b> to dhamma (to avoid māna because he was wise)</li>
<li>when approaching the saṅgha always approach with <b>respect</b> (to avoid māna because he was from the brahmin clan)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>only one with the 13 dhutaṅgas could wear the robe given by the Buddha to Ven. Mahākassapa</li>
<li>Can chief disciples pass away after their respective Buddhas?→no, the 2 chief disciples always pass away before their respective Buddhas</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;"><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>Pañhabyākaraṇū</b></mark>pasampadā (by solving questions)
                    </h3><ul>
<li><b>Background</b>
<ul>
<li>Where? In <b>Sāvatthi</b>, at the <b>Pubbārāma </b>monastery
Recipient?<b> </b><b>Sopāka </b>Sāmaṇera (arahant) 
How old was he? 7 years old
How did it happen? he was <b>walking</b> behind the Tathāgata when He turned towards Sopāka Sāmaṇera and <b>while standing</b> asked ten questions about the <b>unpleasant perception</b> (asubha saññā) (according to Vinaya; but according to Suttanta it was the kumārapañhāsutta). The Sopāka Sāmaṇera solved them all very well. The Tathāgata pleased by it declared the higher ordination of the Sopāka Sāmaṇera. </li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;"><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>Garudhammapaṭiggahaṇū</b></mark>pasampadā (by accepting 8 cardinal rules)
                    </h3><ul>
<li><b>Background</b>
<ul>
<li>When (who?) Pajāpatī Gotamī, the stepmother/aunt of the Tathāgata requested higher ordination from the Tathāgata, with the assistence of Ven. Ānanda, these 8 cardinal rules were established and her acceptance of them resulted in her ordination.
                                    <ul>
<li>Some scholars think the 8 garudhammas were only for pajāpatī but according to theravāda it is for all bhikkhunīs.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;"><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>Dūtenapaṭiggahaṇū</b></mark>pasampadā (by a messenger)
                    </h3><ul>
<li><b>Background</b>
<ul>
<li>The ex-prostitute named Aḍḍhakāsī got ordained among the bhikkhuṇīs and was preparing to go to the bhikkhusaṅgha to receive upasampadā when it came to be known that a group of men were waiting to rape her. Then the Tathāgata approved the sending of another bhikkhunī as an agent in order to perform the upasampadā. This can be done for others as well.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;"><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>Aṭṭhavācikū</b></mark>pasampadā (by 8 kammavācās)
                    </h3><ul>
<li><b>Background</b>
<ul>
<li>Bhī ordination was done first by bhikkhusaṅgha (500 followers of pajāpatī and those who changed sex - these are called ekato upasampannā but because some ladies were ashamed of answering certain questions to the monks during ordination the Buddha established ordination first under bhikkhunīs and then under bhikkhus (aṭṭhavācika (because 2 times ñatticatuttha)). Until they are ordained by the bhikkhu saṅgha they are not yet bhikkhunīs and they are called ekato upasampannā as well.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>two types of <b>ekatoupasampannā</b>
<ul>
<li><b>aparipuṇṇa</b> - <b>not yet bhikkhunī</b>: candidates of upasampadā who have received it from the bhī saṅgha but not yet from the bhikkhu saṅgha (not yet bhikkhunīs)</li>
<li><b>paripuṇṇa</b> - <b>bhikkhunī</b>: those who received ordination from the bhikkhu saṅgha (the 500 followers of pajāpatī and bhikkhus who have changed sex). They are bhikkhunīs.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;"><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>Ñatticatutthakammū</b></mark>pasampadā (by a motion-and-three-proclamations vinaya kamma)
                    </h3><ul>
<li><b>Background</b>
<ul>
<li>First recipient? Ven. Rādha
Who was responsible? Ven. Sāriputta
How did it happen? he was taken to the Buddha by the elder <b>Sāriputta</b> who inquired about the procedure and the Buddha abolished the saraṇagamanūpasampadā and established ñatticatutthakammūpasampadā. Within majjhimadesa 10 bhikkhus are needed in the assembly, outside of it only 5. This is the upasampadā method used nowadays.  #<mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: red; color: inherit;"><b>important</b></mark> </li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>motion (ñatti) is done in (which tense) present tense and proclamations (anusāvana) are done in the (which tense) optative mood </li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;">Of the 8 types of upasampadā 4 were done by the Buddha and 4 by the sāvakas</h3></li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;">Buddhupajjhāyika↔one who has the Buddha as upajjhāya</h3></li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;">Buddhācariyaka↔one who has the Buddha as ācariya (27.000+ monks)</h3></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><h2 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;">ñatticatutthakammūpasampadā 
            </h2><ul>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;">Where can upasampadā be done?→technically anywhere but all bhikkhus of the area must be present or give chanda therefore upasampadās are always done in sīmās. 
                    </h3><ul>
<li>2 main types of sīmā
                            <ul>
<li><b>baddha</b>sīmā - established by the saṅgha</li>
<li><b>abaddha</b>sīmā<b> - </b>not established by the saṅgha (village, forest or water sīmā)</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;">upasampadā in the majjhimadesa must be done with at least 10 bhikkhus. Pāḷi: <i>"anujānāmi, bhikkhave, dasavaggena vā atirekadasavaggena vā gaṇena upasampādetuṁ.</i> 
If performed outside of the majjhimadesa only 5 are needed (with one of them being a vinayadhara). Pāḷi: anujānāmi, bhikkhave, sabbapaccantimesu janapadesu vinayadharapañcamena gaṇena upasampadaṃ. </h3></li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;"><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>Majjhimadesa </b></mark>limits:
                    </h3><ul>
<li>In brief:
To the <b>east</b> (The Great Sal Tree after Gajaṅgala town)
To the <b>southeast</b> the river Sallavatī
To the <b>south</b> the Setakaṇṇika town
To the <b>west</b> the brahmin village of Thūna
To the <b>north</b> the Usīraddhaja mountain</li>
<li><b>Pāḷi: </b>tatrime paccantimā janapadā – <b>puratthimāya</b> disāya <b>gajaṅgalaṃ</b> [kajaṅgalaṃ (sī. syā.)] nāma nigamo, tassā parena mahāsālā, tato parā paccantimā janapadā, orato majjhe. 
<b>Puratthimadakkhiṇāya</b> disāya <b>sallavatī</b> [salalavatī (sī.)] nāma nadī, tato parā paccantimā janapadā, orato majjhe. 
<b>Dakkhiṇāya</b> disāya <b>setakaṇṇikaṃ</b> nāma nigamo, tato parā paccantimā janapadā, orato majjhe. 
<b>Pacchimāya</b> disāya <b>thūnaṃ</b> nāma brahmaṇagāmo, tato parā paccantimā janapadā, orato majjhe. <b>
Uttarāya</b> disāya <b>usīraddhajo</b> nāma pabbato, tato parā paccantimā janapadā, orato majjhe.</li>
<li>vocab: #[[<mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: transparent; color: red;">pāḷi</mark> vocab (1st year)   ]] 
                            <ul>
<li>mahāsālo (m.)→great sal tree (Shorea robusta)</li>
<li>paccanta (vis.) [pati + anta]↔border; frontier; outlying</li>
<li>orato (nip.)↔on this side (of), on the nearer side; before</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;">expectant of higher ordination is called upasampadāpekkha </h3></li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;">Who established the candidate in higher ordination?→It is not the reciters nor the upajjhāya but <b>the saṅgha</b></h3> </li>
<li><h3 data-toc-injected="true" style="display:inline; font-size:inherit; font-weight:inherit; font-style:inherit; font-family:inherit; color:inherit; margin:0; padding:0; line-height:inherit;"><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>ordination procedure</b></mark> #<mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: red; color: inherit;"><b>important</b></mark></h3>
<ul>
<li><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>before </b></mark>the ordination and <mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>kammavācā</b></mark> (12 steps)
                            <ul>
<li><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>bringing</b></mark><b> </b>the candidate to the saṅgha in the sīmā</li>
<li>(optional) changing <mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>names</b></mark>: <b>Nāga</b> for upasampadapekkha and <b>Tissa</b> for upajjhāya by informing all present</li>
<li><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>upajjhāyagahaṇa</b></mark><b> </b>(taking upajjhāya): 
                                    <ul>
<li>Pāḷi by the ācariya: "paṭhamaṃ upajjhaṃ gāhāpetabbo" </li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>pattacīvaraṁ</b></mark><b> </b>ācikkhitabbaṁ (mentioning bowl and robes) 
                                    <ul>
<li>the bowl and robes should <b>belong to the candidate</b> but in case they are not the ordination is still valid</li>
<li>kappabindu is done by an <b>upasampanna</b> </li>
<li>pāḷi by the ācariya: "upajjhaṃ gāhāpetvā  pattacīvaraṃ ācikkhitabbaṃ – ayaṃ te patto, ayaṃ saṅghāṭi, ayaṃ uttarāsaṅgo, ayaṃ antaravāsako."   </li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>sending</b></mark><b> </b>the candidate to a side
                                    <ul>
<li>pāḷi by the ācariya: "gaccha, amumhi okāse tiṭṭhāhi" </li>
<li>he should not turn his back to the saṅgha</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>the upasampadācariya requests the saṅgha <mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>permission</b></mark><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"> </mark><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>to advise</b></mark> the candidate on a side
                                    <ul>
<li>pāḷi: na, bhikkhave, asammatena anusāsitabbo. yo anusāseyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa. anujānāmi, bhikkhave, sammatena anusāsituṃ. </li>
<li>pāḷi for requesting to advise: "suṇātu me, bhante, saṅgho. itthannāmo itthannāmassa āyasmato upasampadāpekkho. yadi saṅghassa pattakallaṃ, itthannāmo itthannāmaṃ anusāseyyā"ti </li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>he <mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>advises</b></mark><b> </b>the candidate on a side about the 13 antarāyikadhammas
                                    <ul>
<li>13 antarāyikadhammas asked during ordination
                                            <ul>
<li>anujānāmi, bhikkhave, upasampādentena terasa [tassa (ka.)] antarāyike dhamme pucchituṃ. evañca pana, bhikkhave, pucchitabbo – "santi te evarūpā ābādhā – kuṭṭhaṃ, gaṇḍo, kilāso, soso, apamāro? manussosi? purisosi? bhujissosi? aṇaṇosi? nasi rājabhaṭo? anuññātosi mātāpitūhi? paripuṇṇavīsativassosi? paripuṇṇaṃ te pattacīvaraṃ? kiṃnāmosi? konāmo te upajjhāyo"ti?  </li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>ovādācariya requests the saṅgha <mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>permission to bring back</b></mark> the candidate </li>

<li><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>vatthu</b></mark><b> </b>sampatti / vipatti - the qualities required in the upasampadāpekkha (3 for males, 4 for females)</li>
<li><mark remnotemark="true" style="background-color: yellow; color: inherit;"><b>ñatti</b></mark><b> </b>sampatti / vipatti<b> </b>- informing the Saṅgha about the disciplinary act. It belongs to the kammoavācā of the vinayakamma. There are 5 <b>parāmasana</b>:
                            <ul>
<li>i. <b>vatthu </b>parāmasana - (vatthu refers to the candidate) The upasampadapekkha's <b>name </b>must be mentioned in the ñatti. Failing to do so is vatthu aparāmasana. Mentioning it as ‘<b>ayaṃ nāgo’</b> is vatthu parāmasana.

<li>iii. <b>puggala </b>parāmasana - (puggala refers to the upajjhāya) The preceptor should be mentioned as “Nāgo <b>tissassa </b>upasampadāpekkho<b>”</b>. Failing to do so is aparāmasana. Doing it properly is parāmasana.</li>
<li>iv. <b>ñatti </b>parāmasana - Not stating the ñatti is ñatti aparāmasana. Stating the ñatti in the manner advised is ñatti parāmasana. </li>
