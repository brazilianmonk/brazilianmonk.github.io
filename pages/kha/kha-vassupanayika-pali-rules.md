## Pāḷi Rules

1.  **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, vassaṃ upagantuṃ**
2.  **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, vassāne vassaṃ upagantuṃ**
3.  **Dvemā, bhikkhave, vassūpanāyikā – purimikā, pacchimikā. Aparajjugatāya āsāḷhiyā purimikā upagantabbā, māsagatāya āsāḷhiyā pacchimikā upagantabbā. Imā kho, bhikkhave, dve vassūpanāyikā**
4.  **Na, bhikkhave, vassaṃ upagantvā purimaṃ vā temāsaṃ pacchimaṃ vā temāsaṃ avasitvā cārikā pakkamitabbā. yo pakkameyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**
5.  **Na, bhikkhave, vassaṃ na upagantabbaṃ. Yo na upagaccheyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**
6.  **Na, bhikkhave, tadahu vassūpanāyikāya vassaṃ anupagantukāmena sañcicca āvāso atikkamitabbo. Yo atikkameyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**
7.  **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, rājūnaṃ anuvattituṃ**
8.  **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, sattāhakaraṇīyena gantuṃ**
9.  **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, sattannaṃ sattāhakaraṇīyena pahite gantuṃ, na tveva appahite. Bhikkhussa, bhikkhuniyā, sikkhamānāya, sāmaṇerassa, sāmaṇeriyā, upāsakassa, upāsikāya – Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, imesaṃ sattannaṃ sattāhakaraṇīyena pahite gantuṃ, na tveva appahite. Sattāhaṃ sannivatto kātabbo.**:
10. **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, pañcannaṃ sattāhakaraṇīyena appahitepi gantuṃ, pageva pahite. Bhikkhussa, bhikkhuniyā, sikkhamānāya, sāmaṇerassa, sāmaṇeriyā – Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, imesaṃ pañcannaṃ sattāhakaraṇīyena appahitepi gantuṃ, pageva pahite. Sattāhaṃ sannivatto kātabbo**
11. **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, sattannaṃ sattāhakaraṇīyena appahitepi gantuṃ, pageva pahite. Bhikkhussa&#x2026; mātuyā ca pitussa ca – anujānāmi&#x2026;**
12. **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, saṅghakaraṇīyena gantuṃ. Sattāhaṃ sannivatto kātabbo**
13. **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, yena gāmo tena gantuṃ**
14. **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, yena bahutarā tena gantuṃ**
15. **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, yena saddhā pasannā tena gantuṃ**
16. **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, vaje vassaṃ upagantuṃ**
17. **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, yena vajo tena gantuṃ**
18. **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, satthe vassaṃ upagantuṃ**
19. **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, nāvāya vassaṃ upagantuṃ**
20. **Na, bhikkhave, rukkhasusire vassaṃ upagantabbaṃ. Yo upagaccheyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**
21. **Na, bhikkhave, rukkhaviṭabhiyā vassaṃ upagantabbaṃ. Yo upagaccheyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**
22. **Na, bhikkhave, ajjhokāse vassaṃ upagantabbaṃ. Yo upagaccheyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**
23. **Na, bhikkhave, asenāsanikena vassaṃ upagantabbaṃ. Yo upagaccheyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**
24. **Na, bhikkhave, chavakuṭikāya vassaṃ upagantabbaṃ. Yo upagaccheyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**
25. **Na, bhikkhave, chatte vassaṃ upagantabbaṃ. Yo upagaccheyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**
26. **Na, bhikkhave, cāṭiyā vassaṃ upagantabbaṃ. Yo upagaccheyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**
27. **Na, bhikkhave, evarūpā katikā kātabbā – ‘Na antarāvassaṃ pabbājetabba’nti. Yo kareyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa.**
28. **Idha pana, bhikkhave, bhikkhunā vassāvāso paṭissuto hoti purimikāya. So taṃ āvāsaṃ gacchanto passati antarāmagge dve āvāse bahucīvarake. Tassa evaṃ hoti – “Yaṃnūnāhaṃ imesu dvīsu āvāsesu vassaṃ vaseyyaṃ. Evaṃ me bahuṃ cīvaraṃ uppajjissatī”ti. So tesu dvīsu āvāsesu vassaṃ vasati. Tassa, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno purimikā ca na paññāyati, paṭissave ca āpatti dukkaṭassa.**
    -   28 variations
29. **Idha pana, bhikkhave, aññatarasmiṃ āvāse sambahulā bhikkhū vassaṃ vasanti bālā abyattā. Te na jānanti uposathaṃ vā uposathakammaṃ vā pātimokkhaṃ vā pātimokkhuddesaṃ vā . . . Evañcetaṃ labhetha, iccetaṃ kusalaṃ. No ce labhetha, na, bhikkhave, tehi bhikkhūhi tasmiṃ āvāse vassaṃ vasitabbaṃ. Vaseyyuṃ ce, āpatti dukkaṭassa.**
