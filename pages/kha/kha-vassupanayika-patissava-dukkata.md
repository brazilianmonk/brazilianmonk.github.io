## Paṭissava Dukkaṭa


<a id="org92b0c45"></a>

### Background



-   V. Upananda promised/undertook to spend the purimikā rains at a residence belonging to King Pasenadi Kosala
-   on the way, he saw 2 monasteries were well supplied with robes
-   desiring to get robes, he stayed there
-   the King criticized his breaking of the promise, calling it false speech
-   monks also criticizes him and reported to the Buddha
-   Buddha questioned and reproached him, gave a Dhamma teaching on false speech and the breaking of a promise
-   **idha pana, bhikkhave, bhikkhunā vassāvāso paṭissuto hoti purimikāya. So taṃ āvāsaṃ gacchanto passati antarāmagge dve āvāse bahucīvarake. Tassa evaṃ hoti – “Yaṃnūnāhaṃ imesu dvīsu āvāsesu vassaṃ vaseyyaṃ. Evaṃ me bahuṃ cīvaraṃ uppajjissatī”ti. So tesu dvīsu āvāsesu vassaṃ vasati. Tassa, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno purimikā ca na paññāyati, paṭissave ca āpatti dukkaṭassa**
    -   purimikā vassūpanāyika is not established
    -   paṭissava dkṭ
-   notes: this shows
    -   the importance of keeping a promise made concerning the observance of the Rains
    -   not altering one's undertaking because of an expectation of material gain
    -   avoiding a monk's inappropriate desire for gain




<a id="orgf70bf2b"></a>

### 8 Types of Dukkaṭas



-   **Pubbapayoga dukkaṭa**: preliminary to stealing, etc.
-   **Sahapayoga dukkaṭa**: clearing the ground to steal something
-   **Anāmāsa dukkaṭa**: touching unallowable things like gems, raw grains, female images, weapons, etc.
-   **Durūpaciṇṇa dukkaṭa**: touching fruits on the tree
-   **Vinaya dukkaṭa**: consuming food into which dust has falling into the patta during piṇḍapāta
-   **Ñāta dukkaṭa**: one hears of a schism, micchāduṭṭhi, etc., the monk cannot stay silent; he must go and admonish; otherwise, dkṭ
-   **Ñatti dukkaṭa**: in 11 samanubhāsanas, dukkaṭa incurred in the ñatti of the samanubhāsanakammas
-   **Paṭissava dukkaṭa**: breaking promises




<a id="org6e2542d"></a>

### Vassūpanāyika in Two Monasteries



-   One enters vassūpanāyika in monastery A but later decides to enter it on monastery B
    -   when leaving the upacārasīmā of monastery A intending to observe vassa elsewhere: previous vassūpagamana / senāsanaggāha is terminated
        -   tassa, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno purimikā ca na paññāyati: refers to either
            -   the previous senāsanaggāha is terminated when going to another monastery
            -   purimikā does not become established at any residence (in the case of changing monastery after the entering day)
        -   vmv-ṭ: yasmā nānāsīmāyaṃ dvīsu āvāsesu vassaṃ upagacchantassa “dutiye vasissāmī”ti upacārato nikkhantamatte paṭhamo senāsanaggāho paṭippassambhati
    -   when he enters vassa in the second monastery: his purimikā is established there
        -   at the end of the vassūpanāyika, he is purimavassaṁvuttha and can do pavāraṇā
            -   Vmv-ṭ: dutiye senāsanaggāhe pana purimikā paññāyateva, tattheva temāsaṃ vasanto purimavassaṃvuttho eva hoti
        -   if he does so and then, on the same day, returns to the monastery A and re-enters there: 2nd vassūpagamana is terminated when leaving the upacārasīmā and is again re-established in monastery A
        -   doing so on another day: vassūpanāyika is terminated and he can only observe it again on pacchimikā day
-   Moment of termination:
    -   intending to observe in another place: when leaving the upacāra
    -   deciding when already outside the monastery: upon the arising of that decision




<a id="org96fa097"></a>

### Conditions for the Paṭissava Dukkaṭa


**Paṭissave ca āpatti dukkaṭassa**: dkṭ for breaking a promise under the following conditions:

-   Type of promise:
    -   I'll spend these three months of the rains here
    -   Imaṃ temāsaṃ bhikkhaṃ gaṇhatha (I'll accept alms-food for these three months)
    -   Ubhopi mayaṃ idha vassaṃ vasissāma (Let us both spend the rains here together)
    -   Ekato uddisāpessāma (Let us teach/learn the Pāli Canon and the Commentaries together)
    -   Other similar promises
-   Status of the promise: long as the status of having made the promise remains in force
    -   Srtd-ṭ: paṭissavassa visaṃvādanapaccayā hontampi dukkaṭaṃ satiyeva paṭissave
    -   sincere promise subsequently broken
    -   deceitful promise (vaṅkacittena): **musāvāda pc** when making it, **paṭissava dkṭ** when breaking it

### 1st Heptad: Uposatha elsewhere


<a id="org449164f"></a>

#### 1: Tadaheva Akaraṇīyo Pakkamati


**Tadaheva akaraṇīyo pakkamati**: purimikā ca na paññāyati, paṭissave ca āpatti dukkaṭassa

-   promises to spend the purimikā rains somewhere
-   on the Āsāḷhapuṇṇamā, observes the uposatha on the way
-   on the Pāṭipadika, arrives at the vassa-monastery but then departs:
    -   without having observed the vassa
    -   akaraṇīya: and without an appropriate or necessary reason
        -   Srtd-ṭ: akaraṇīyoti sattāhakaraṇīyena akaraṇīyo
    -   notes:
        -   he leaves with nirapekkhagamana
        -   note: since he hasnt' observed the vassa, the sattāhakaraṇīya would not apply anyways



1.  Sattāha-karaṇīyena

    
    
    -   7-day period is explicitly mentioned in this way
    -   if it were sakaraṇīyo pakkamati: it would not be possible to convey the idea that he spends the ensuing seven days at an external place
    
    Tadaheva akaraṇīyo pakkamati:
    
    -   if a monk departs without having entered upon the Rains, or
        -   here, the purimikā is never established
        -   departing with anapekkhagamanena
    -   if, after entering upon the Rains, he spends seven days at an external place, his purimikā (first/earlier Rains observance) is not established, and, because he had made a promise to spend the Rains there, an offence is also incurred (due to the break of that promise)
        -   here, the purimikā is initially established but is subsequently broken after the lapse of the seven days
    
    


<a id="orgf53750c"></a>

#### 2: Tadaheva Sakaraṇīyo Pakkamati



-   **Tadaheva Sakaraṇīyo Pakkamati**: same as 1 but he departs with a valid reason
    -   Srtd-ṭ: sakaraṇīyoti sattāhakaraṇīyeneva sakaraṇīyo
    -   he departs with
        -   sakaraṇīya
        -   nirāpekkhagamana
    -   note: monk has not yet entered the vassa
-   **Purimikā ca na paññāyati, paṭissave ca āpatti dukkaṭassa**




<a id="org44f720a"></a>

#### 3: Dvīhatīhaṃ vasitvā akaraṇīyo pakkamati



-   **Dvīhatīhaṃ vasitvā akaraṇīyo pakkamati**: same as 1 but:
    -   he enters vassa there
    -   stays 2 or 3 days
    -   departs with
        -   akaraṇīya
        -   nirapekkhagamana
-   **Purimikā ca na paññāyati, paṭissave ca āpatti dukkaṭassa**
    -   **vassaccheda dukkaṭa** upon crossing the upacārasīmā as well




<a id="orgf7560b5"></a>

#### 4: Dīhatīhaṁ vasitvā sakaraṇīyo pakkamati



-   **Dvīhatīhaṃ vasitvā sakaraṇīyo pakkamati**: same as 1 but:
    -   he enters vassa there
    -   stays 2 or 3 days
    -   departs with
        -   sakaraṇīya
        -   nirapekkhagamana
-   **Purimikā ca na paññāyati, paṭissave ca āpatti dukkaṭassa**
    -   **vassaccheda dukkaṭa** upon crossing the upacārasīmā as well (tbc)

    


<a id="org73145eb"></a>

#### 5: Dīhatīhaṁ vasitvā sattāhakaraṇīyena pakkamati. So taṃ sattāhaṃ bahiddhā vītināmeti



-   **Dvīhatīhaṃ vasitvā sakaraṇīyo pakkamati**: same as 1 but:
    -   he enters vassa there
    -   stays 2 or 3 days
    -   departs with
        -   sakaraṇīya
        -   sāpekkhagamana (intending to return)
        -   **so taṃ sattāhaṃ bahiddhā vītināmeti** - He spends that entire seven-day period outside)
-   **Purimikā ca na paññāyati, paṭissave ca āpatti dukkaṭassa**
    -   **vassaccheda dukkaṭa** upon the 8th dawn outside




<a id="orgd454155"></a>

#### 6: Dīhatīhaṁ vasitvā sattāhakaraṇīyena pakkamati. So taṃ sattāhaṃ anto sannivattaṃ karoti

 

-   **Dvīhatīhaṃ vasitvā sakaraṇīyo pakkamati**: same as 1 but:
    -   he enters vassa there
    -   stays 2 or 3 days
    -   departs with
        -   sakaraṇīya
        -   sāpekkhagamana (intending to return)
    -   **so taṃ sattāhaṃ anto sannivattaṃ karoti**: returns within the 7-day period
-   **Purimikā ca paññāyati, paṭissave ca anāpatti**



1.  Entering & Leaving the Same Day

    
    
    -   V-a: Vassaṃ upagantvā pana aruṇaṃ anuṭṭhāpetvā tadaheva sattāhakaraṇīyena pakkamantassāpi antosattāhe nivattantassa anāpatti
        -   enter vassa
        -   depart on the same day with a sattāhakaraṇīya
        -   returns within the 7-day period: anāpatti
    -   Note: same for one who does so on a later day
    
    

2.  Forgetting to Enter

    
    
    -   intention or inclination (ālaya) to enter upon the rains residence and stay at a particular monastery
    -   forgets to verbally enter
    -   regarded as having entered
    -   senāsanaggāha is undertaken
    -   no vassaccheda
    -   can do pavāraṇā
    -   V-a: sace idha vasissāmīti ālayo atthi, asatiyā pana vassaṃ na upeti, gahitasenāsanaṃ suggahitaṃ, chinnavasso na hoti, pavāretuṃ labhatiyeva
    -   Srtd-ṭ: asatiyā pana vassaṃ na upetīti “imasmiṃ vihāre imaṃ temāsaṃ vassaṃ upemī”ti vacībhedaṃ katvā na upeti
    
    


<a id="org290d58a"></a>

#### 7: So sattāhaṃ anāgatāya pavāraṇāya sakaraṇīyo pakkamati



-   **So sattāhaṃ anāgatāya pavāraṇāya sakaraṇīyo pakkamati**: when 7 days remain before the Pāvāraṇā
    -   navamito paṭṭhāya: from the 9th day of the last pakkha before the pavāraṇā onwards
    -   same as 1 but:
        -   he enters vassa there
        -   7 days or less before the Pāvāraṇā, he departs with
            -   sakaraṇīya
            -   sāpekkhagamana
-   **Āgaccheyya vā so, bhikkhave, bhikkhu taṃ āvāsaṃ, na vā āgaccheyya**: whether he returns or not
-   **Purimikā ca paññāyati, paṭissave ca anāpatti**
    -   no vassaccheda
    -   can do pavāraṇā elsewhere
    -   Sattāhaṃ anāgatāya pavāraṇāyāti ettha navamito paṭṭhāya gantuṃ vaṭṭati, āgacchatu vā mā vā, anāpatti

### Komudiyā Cātumāsiniyā


**Komudiyā cātumāsiniyāti pacchima-kattika-puṇṇamāyaṃ**

-   Komudī / Cātumāsinī: full-moon day of the latter Kattika (pacchima-kattika) month
    -   Komudī: because the kumuda (white water-lily) is abundant on that day
    -   Cātumāsinī: because it marks the end of the four months belonging to the rainy season
-   Pubba-kattika: Assayuja
    -   3rd month of vassāna
-   Pacchima-kattika: Cīvaramāsa / Kattika
    -   from Assayujapuṇṇamā to Kattikapuṇṇamā
    -   usually from the October full moon to November full moon
    -   4th month of the vassāna
