Background: due to the behavior of the Chabbaggiyas


<a id="org00faf1d"></a>

### Paṭisedha: Travelling During Vassāvāsa



-   Background:
    -   Chabbaggiyas did so
    -   people criticized
-   Prohibition: **Na, bhikkhave, vassaṃ upagantvā purimaṃ vā temāsaṃ pacchimaṃ vā temāsaṃ avasitvā cārikā pakkamitabbā. yo pakkameyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**: having entered the rains, one should not travel without having completed it; otherwise, dkṭ
    


<a id="org941420a"></a>

#### 2 Situations for Āpatti


V-a:

-   **Anapekkhagamanena**: leaving not intending to return: dkṭ when upacārātikkame (going beyond the upacāra)
    -   note: **sāpekkhagamana**: leaving intending to return
-   **Aññattha aruṇaṃ uṭṭhāpanena**: leaving with sāpekkhagamana but akaraṇīyena: dkṭ when dawn breaks elsewhere
    -   vmv: sāpekkhassa akaraṇīyena gantvā aññattha aruṇaṃ uṭṭhāpanena vā
    -   srtd: sāpekkhagamanena aññattha aruṇuṭṭhāpanena āpatti veditabbā

### Paṭisedha: Not Entering Vassāvāsa



-   Background: Chabbaggiyas intentionally avoided entering the rains residence
-   Prohibition: **Na, bhikkhave, vassaṃ na upagantabbaṃ. Yo na upagaccheyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**: one should not not enter the rains-residence; otherwise, dkṭ




<a id="orgc807009"></a>

### Paṭisedha: Avoiding Vassāvāsa



-   Background: Chabbaggiyas deliberately went beyond the monastery boundary on the rains-entry day to avoid entering vassāvāsa
-   Prohibition: **Na, bhikkhave, tadahu vassūpanāyikāya vassaṃ anupagantukāmena sañcicca āvāso atikkamitabbo. Yo atikkameyya, āpatti dukkaṭassa**: one should not intentionally go beyond the monastery boundary on the day of entering the rains residence to evade the rains-observance; otherwise, dkṭ
    -   comm:
        -   crossing one and returning: 1 offense
        -   crossing multiple upacāras: multiple offenses
    -   **āvāsātikkamana**: going beyond the monastery (boundary)

    


<a id="orgea7ad05"></a>

### Anuññā: Vassukkaḍḍhana



-   Background: King Bimbisāra wanted the Saṅgha to postpone the rains residence by one month for practical reasons
    -   **vassukkaḍḍhana**: postponing the rains residence
-   **Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, rājūnaṃ anuvattituṃ**: allowed to comply with the kings
    -   **rājūnaṁ anuvattituṁ**: complying with kings regarding the timing of vassāvāsa when circumstances require it
        -   vnlkr: kings used to postpone the rains (vassaṃ uddhaṃ kaḍḍhanti) every 3rd year, adding an additional month (adhikamāsa) after āsāḷha also called āsāḷha
            -   normally: 12 months (āsāḷha -> sāvaṇa)
                every 3rd year: 13 months (āsāḷha -> extra āsāḷha -> sāvaṇa)




<a id="org01d3a48"></a>

#### Meaning of "Rājūnaṃ Anuvattituṃ"


**Aññasmimpi dhammike kamme anuvattitabbaṃ, adhammike pana na kassaci anuvattitabbaṃ**

-   not against dhammavinaya: follow
    -   includes vassāvāsa since there is no disadvantage
    -   vmtvndn: uposatha observance:
        -   14th or 15th: can be followed
            13th or 16th: cannot be followed because
            -   **Na, bhikkhave, anuposathe uposatho kātabbo**
            -   they are not uposatha days
            -   there would be a decline in virtue
            -   it would be a dkṭ
-   against dhammavinaya: don't follow
-   note: includes royal customs, regulations, or administrative arrangements
